# Socheon (소천)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 24
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Fourteen-year-old survivor of the Sakju Branch; older brother and protector of Soyul
- **Personality:** Watchful, frightened, and determined to survive and protect his sister after witnessing the massacre of his home
- **Voice:** A guarded child’s voice that becomes resolute under pressure
- **Relationships:** Son of the Sakju Branch Leader; older brother of Soyul; protected by Gong Yacheong
- **Continuity:** Flees the destruction of the Sakju Branch with Soyul after their mother leads other survivors away. Gong Yacheong guides them toward Honju and stays behind to delay the pursuers. Socheon reaches Taekyung’s reconnaissance squad carrying his sister and is rescued when Taekyung defeats the pursuing force.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 24
