# Jin Taekyung (진태경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 8
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Modern-world protagonist; recently fired after seven years at his job; F-rank Hunter; newly logged into Murim as the youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Hungry, self-aware, dryly observant, and willing to take a questionable opportunity when desperate; treats the impossible as a game until the danger becomes undeniable
- **Voice:** First-person, conversational, dryly self-mocking; uses vivid trap-and-prey imagery, game jargon, and occasional profanity
- **Relationships:** Son of a deceased father; supports his mother and younger sibling
- **Continuity:** Lives in a hillside neighborhood; discovers an old game capsule beneath a streetlamp and registers it as a player; has been fired from his Guild job; is trapped in Murim after logging in as Jin Taekyung and learning that the character is the Jin Family’s notorious third son; reaches Second Rate Martial Artist, unlocks Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, defeats a bandit leader during Tutorial—Stage 3, completes the final tutorial by circulating qi, gains Qi Sense, and arrives at the Jin Family of Taiyuan; moves into a residence, discovers hundreds of martial arts manuals, and begins practicing footwork late into the night
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 0–5 and 8
