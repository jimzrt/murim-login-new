# Jin Taekyung (진태경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 10
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Modern-world protagonist; recently fired after seven years at his job; F-rank Hunter; newly logged into Murim as the youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Hungry, self-aware, dryly observant, and willing to take a questionable opportunity when desperate; treats the impossible as a game until the danger becomes undeniable
- **Voice:** First-person, conversational, dryly self-mocking; uses vivid trap-and-prey imagery, game jargon, and occasional profanity
- **Relationships:** Son of a deceased father; supports his mother and younger sibling
- **Continuity:** Reaches Second Rate Martial Artist, unlocks Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, defeats a bandit leader during Tutorial—Stage 3, completes the final tutorial by circulating qi, gains Qi Sense, and arrives at the Jin Family of Taiyuan; after fighting Hyuk Mujin, he is taken to Medicine King Hall, feigns amnesia, discovers his residence’s martial arts library, practices footwork late at night, then enters the training hall, acquires Jin Family’s Spear Technique, and begins using internal energy with a sharp iron spear
- **Sources:** Korean source, Chapters 0–8 and 10
