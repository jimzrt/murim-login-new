# Han Yeop (한엽)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 22
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Level 13 martial artist; member of White Tiger Hall’s reconnaissance squad
- **Personality:** Cheerful, eager, and openly enthusiastic about following Jin Taekyung’s orders
- **Voice:** Bright and excitable; addresses Taekyung as Squad Leader
- **Relationships:** Reconnaissance squad subordinate to Jin Taekyung; serves under deputy squad leader Hyuk Mujin
- **Continuity:** A spear user with combat experience but fewer than five battles, Han Yeop is the only squad member to enthusiastically support Taekyung when he takes command. He leaves with the reconnaissance squad on its first deployment.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 22
