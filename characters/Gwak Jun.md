# Gwak Jun (곽준)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 37
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Level 40 martial artist of the Three Paths Sect
- **Personality:** Friendly, fearless, intensely confident, and unsettlingly eager for battle
- **Voice:** Cheerful and casual, with blunt conviction beneath his jokes
- **Relationships:** Leads martial artists of the Three Paths Sect; serves alongside Jin Taekyung’s rear guard
- **Continuity:** Introduces himself to Taekyung during the march to Honju and complains that rear-guard duty offers too few chances to distinguish himself. He praises the Jin Family’s strength and the Head Elder’s reputation as the Blade of Flowers, then insists that the Jin Family will win. On the morning of the planned battle, he changes into black martial robes with the Three Paths Sect fighters he leads and proposes that they set out.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 37
