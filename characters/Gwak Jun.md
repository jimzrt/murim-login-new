# Gwak Jun (곽준)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 38
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Level 40 martial artist of the Three Paths Sect
- **Personality:** Friendly and cheerful as a cover for cold, fanatical violence; fearless, intensely confident, and eager for battle
- **Voice:** Cheerful and casual, with blunt conviction beneath his jokes
- **Relationships:** Leads martial artists of the Three Paths Sect; serves alongside Jin Taekyung’s rear guard
- **Continuity:** Introduces himself to Taekyung during the march to Honju and complains that rear-guard duty offers too few chances to distinguish himself. He praises the Jin Family’s strength and the Head Elder’s reputation as the Blade of Flowers, then insists that the Jin Family will win. On the morning of the planned battle, he changes into black martial robes with the Three Paths Sect fighters he leads and proposes that they set out. In Chapter 38, he reveals that the twenty supposed Three Paths Sect martial artists are assassins ordered to eliminate everyone in the rear guard and join the main force. The infiltrators are all First Rate, but Taekyung and the reconnaissance squad defeat them; Gwak is subdued and then severs his own heart meridian before revealing the full plan. He claims the Three Paths Sect was established thirty years earlier according to an unidentified conspirator’s will.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 37–38
