# Jin Wikyung (진위경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 18
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Thirty-five-year-old Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Calm and authoritative in public; affectionate and protective toward Taekyung beneath a stern mask; accepts responsibility from his subordinates and shows immediate concern for family
- **Voice:** Restrained, formal, and commanding with subordinates; openly affectionate with Taekyung
- **Relationships:** Taekyung’s eldest brother and future Family Head; head of Wipeng; member of the Jin Family
- **Continuity:** At Medicine King Hall, he worries over Taekyung’s memory loss and supplies him with basic family facts; despite long workdays, he visits Taekyung’s pavilion and discovers him practicing footwork late at night. He stages Taekyung’s confinement in the training hall to shield him from the family power struggle, orders Shanxi branches to prepare after contact from the Mount Heng Sword Sect, and waits in the main assembly hall with its envoy. He refuses the envoy’s demand and the duel, then tries to intervene through Sound Transmission when Taekyung accepts, but Wipeng restrains him. After Taekyung wins, Wikyung uses force and family pressure to end his confinement and is openly delighted by the victory. The next day, he draws heroic pictures of Taekyung, receives an urgent messenger hawk, and summons a secret late-night family-council meeting before the Head Elder appears. At the council, he acts as Family Head, guides Taekyung through the interrogation with Sound Transmission, remains on Taekyung’s side when the senior faction proposes handing him over, and declares the family must fight. After Mount Heng declares war, he takes operational control and seals the family grounds against entry or exit without his seal.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 6, 8, and 10–18
