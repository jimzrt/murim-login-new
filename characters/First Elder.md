# First Elder

- **Safe through:** Chapter 57
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** First Elder of the Jin Family of Taiyuan; leader within the Head Elder's faction
- **Personality:** Secretive, calculating, and ruthless
- **Voice:** Direct and taunting when revealing the betrayal
- **Relationships:** Serves the Head Elder's faction; commands the Second and Third Elders
- **Continuity:** At Eight Spring Gorge, the First Elder reveals that he and the other elders have concealed Peak-level martial arts. After Jin Wikyung orders Wipeng to cut him down, he has the Second Elder kill the White Tiger Hall Leader, fires a signal flare, and turns the Five Gates of Shanxi forces against the battlefield. The full conspiracy remains unresolved.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 57
