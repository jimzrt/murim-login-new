# Wolhwa (월화)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 20
- **Aliases:** Eun Sowol (은소월); Wolhwa is the name used at Honghwaru
- **Role:** Level 50 martial artist; Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch; formerly posing as a high-ranking courtesan at Honghwaru, a pleasure house in central Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Striking, composed, observant, direct, and quietly amused; comfortable teasing Taekyung while conducting serious information and negotiation work
- **Voice:** Polite and lightly playful; addresses Taekyung as Young Master Jin and delivers embarrassing observations without raising her voice
- **Relationships:** Knows Taekyung as a Honghwaru regular and the youngest son of the Jin Family; says she likes him, though her sincerity is unclear
- **Continuity:** Smokes a long-stemmed tobacco pipe; explains Taekyung’s identity, family reputation, and immediate surroundings during his first moments in Murim. In chapter 19, she arrives at the Jin Family’s main residence to negotiate aid for the family. She reveals herself as Eun Sowol, the Shanxi Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect, identifies Honghwaru as the sect’s Shanxi branch, and demands half of Mount Heng’s shops and assets as compensation; Jin Wikyung accepts. In chapter 20, she finalizes the alliance by securing exclusive pleasure-district rights and the Lower District Sect’s exclusive information support for the duration of the war. Her investigation has found neither proof clearing Taekyung nor Lee Seogeun’s killer, so she presents the alliance as a calculated investment in either Taekyung’s legitimacy or the Jin Family’s ability to hide the truth. She covers the alliance by telling the gate guards she came to collect Taekyung’s unpaid tab, teases him about their supposed night together, and uses Yama Whip as her coachman.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 2 and 19–20
