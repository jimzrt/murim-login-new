# Wolhwa (월화)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 2
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** High-ranking courtesan at Honghwaru, a pleasure house in central Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Striking, composed, observant, and quietly amused; comfortable teasing Taekyung while watching his unusual behavior
- **Voice:** Polite and lightly playful; addresses Taekyung as Young Master Jin and delivers embarrassing observations without raising her voice
- **Relationships:** Currently knows Taekyung as the youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan; their deeper relationship is not yet established
- **Continuity:** Smokes a long-stemmed tobacco pipe; explains Taekyung’s identity, family reputation, and immediate surroundings during his first moments in Murim
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 2
