# Team Leader Choi

- **Safe through:** Chapter 44
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** C-rank Hunter; leader and employer of the Peace Guild’s E-rank Gate party
- **Personality:** Calm, observant, practical, and decisive under pressure
- **Voice:** Polite and measured in ordinary conversation; clipped and commanding during combat
- **Relationships:** Hires Jin Taekyung as a porter and leads him, Im Kkeokjeong, and three veteran E-rank Hunters through an E-rank Gate
- **Continuity:** Treats Taekyung respectfully and recognizes his unusually fast corpse-processing work. Fights with a sword, expensive equipment, and wind-based Haste magic. In the Boss Zone, identifies the old Hobgoblin Priest and summoned Hobgoblin Great Warrior as C-rank Rare Monsters, attacks the Priest, and orders the rest of the party to hold the Great Warrior down. During the fight, he kills the Priest and witnesses Taekyung, the supposed F-rank porter, severely wound the Level 45 Great Warrior with a spear.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 42–44
