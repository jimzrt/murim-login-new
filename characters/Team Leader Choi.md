# Team Leader Choi

- **Safe through:** Chapter 49
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** C-rank Hunter; leader and employer of the Peace Guild’s E-rank Gate party
- **Personality:** Calm, observant, practical, and decisive under pressure
- **Voice:** Polite and measured in ordinary conversation; clipped and commanding during combat
- **Relationships:** Hires Jin Taekyung as a porter and leads him, Im Kkeokjeong, and three veteran E-rank Hunters through an E-rank Gate
- **Continuity:** Treats Taekyung respectfully and recognizes his unusually fast corpse-processing work. Fights with a sword, expensive equipment, and wind-based Haste magic. In the Boss Zone, identifies the old Hobgoblin Priest and summoned Hobgoblin Great Warrior as C-rank Rare Monsters, attacks the Priest, and orders the rest of the party to hold the Great Warrior down. During the fight, he kills the Priest and witnesses Taekyung, the supposed F-rank porter, kill the Level 45 Great Warrior with a spear. When officials question the party about the two C-rank Rare Monsters in the E-rank Gate, Choi claims he killed both monsters, concealing Taekyung’s role. He later offers Taekyung a Peace Guild position with a 100 million won signing bonus; after investigating Taekyung’s history, he concludes Taekyung is at least C-rank and may have recently reawakened.
- **Chapter 48 continuity:** Choi pays Taekyung 10.3 million won so far for the raid, including an extra ten million won, with additional payment due after the Hobgoblin Priest and Great Warrior’s equipment, leather, and Magic Gems are sold.
- **Chapter 49 continuity:** Choi offers Taekyung a generous formal Peace Guild contract and, after Taekyung asks for time, a seven-day provisional contract. He supplies First Rate Lizardman armor and a harpoon, arranges restricted access to a D-rank Gate, and enters it with Taekyung alone. He reveals that he is a reawakened Hunter; Taekyung infers Choi is at least B-rank, while Choi’s exact current rank and the Gate’s outcome remain unresolved.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 42–49
