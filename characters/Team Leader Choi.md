# Team Leader Choi

- **Safe through:** Chapter 43
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** C-rank Hunter; leader and employer of the Peace Guild’s E-rank Gate party
- **Personality:** Calm, observant, practical, and decisive under pressure
- **Voice:** Polite and measured in ordinary conversation; clipped and commanding during combat
- **Relationships:** Hires Jin Taekyung as a porter and leads him, Im Kkeokjeong, and three veteran E-rank Hunters through an E-rank Gate
- **Continuity:** Treats Taekyung respectfully and recognizes his unusually fast corpse-processing work. Fights with a sword, expensive equipment, and wind-based Haste magic. In the Boss Zone, identifies the old Hobgoblin Priest and summoned Hobgoblin Great Warrior as C-rank Rare Monsters, attacks the Priest, and orders the rest of the party to hold the Great Warrior down.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 42–43
