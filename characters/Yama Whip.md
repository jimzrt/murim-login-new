# Yama Whip (염라편)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 20
- **Aliases:** Yama Whip; personal name not revealed
- **Role:** Peak martial artist and whip master; Wolhwa’s coachman
- **Personality:** Affable and physically demonstrative; speaks of dangerous encounters with disarming warmth
- **Voice:** Deep and gentle; conversational even when recounting a fight
- **Relationships:** Knows Jin Taekyung from their encounter with the Heavenly Axe; works with Wolhwa
- **Continuity:** Previously drove Taekyung to the Jin Family of Taiyuan. At the family gate, he reveals that he survived an encounter with the Heavenly Axe, a Peak master of the Eighteen Strongholds of Green Forest, with Taekyung’s help. The guards identify him as the legendary Yama Whip, a whip master who vanished more than ten years earlier and once fought remnants of the Demonic path. He is serving as Wolhwa’s coachman.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 20
