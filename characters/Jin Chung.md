# Jin Chung (진충)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 62
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Sect Leader of Gunggwimun; one of the Five Gates of Shanxi leaders
- **Personality:** Responsible, protective, and resigned to the consequences of the conspiracy
- **Voice:** Firm and compassionate when addressing his disciples
- **Relationships:** Leader and teacher of Gunggwimun's martial artists; connected to the Head Elder's conspiracy
- **Continuity:** After the Eight Spring Gorge battle, Jin Chung orders his fifty disciples to scatter so they can escape Shanxi, intending to remain behind because the Jin Family would otherwise hunt them. He reveals that the conspirators were saved from the Demonic Cult by Dark Heaven forty years earlier and accepted a bargain that implanted gu in their heads. A concealed Dark Heaven agent kills him and massacres the disciples.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 62
