# Wipeng (위팽)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 10
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Jin Wikyung’s personal guard
- **Personality:** Loyal, observant, teasing, and resigned to his master’s impulsive departures
- **Voice:** Weary and knowing; jokes with Jin Wikyung and uses Sound Transmission
- **Relationships:** Trusted guard and retainer of Jin Wikyung
- **Continuity:** Follows Jin Wikyung after work, jokes about the light in Taekyung’s pavilion, and is stunned to find Taekyung practicing footwork late at night; explains the protective confinement arrangement to Taekyung, negotiates it down to three days, and discusses Taekyung’s supposed frailty with Jin Wikyung
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 6, 8, and 10
