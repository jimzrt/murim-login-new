# Wipeng (위팽)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 25
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Jin Wikyung’s personal guard
- **Personality:** Loyal, observant, teasing, and resigned to his master’s impulsive departures
- **Voice:** Weary and knowing; jokes with Jin Wikyung and uses Sound Transmission
- **Relationships:** Trusted guard and retainer of Jin Wikyung
- **Continuity:** Follows Jin Wikyung after work, jokes about the light in Taekyung’s pavilion, and is stunned to find Taekyung practicing footwork late at night. He explains the protective confinement arrangement, negotiates it down to three days, and escorts Taekyung from the training hall to the main assembly hall, where he questions him about the Mount Heng accusation. During the duel, he recognizes Taekyung’s concealed change, restrains Jin Wikyung from intervening, and helps him force the family seniors to accept Taekyung’s release. The next day, he endures Wikyung’s absurd heroic duel drawings before identifying an urgent messenger hawk. At the war council, he is identified as one of the Jin Family’s three Peak masters. In the Lesser Family Head’s office, he voices his suspicion of the Head Elder, proposes killing the disloyal rebels, and reports the spreading poisoning rumor and the failed support requests. He joins Wikyung and Taekyung in receiving Wolhwa, then objects when Wikyung immediately accepts her demand for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive rights to the pleasure district. He attends the final alliance negotiations and accompanies Taekyung and Wolhwa to the gate, where he witnesses Taekyung turn the guards’ mistaken story about Yama Whip into another boost for the Sleeping Dragon rumor. In Chapter 25, Wikyung dispatches him with more than twenty riders to find and protect Taekyung.
- **Chapter 22 continuity:** After Taekyung’s reconnaissance squad departs, Wipeng points out that Taekyung should be doing fine and questions Wikyung’s denial that he is worried. He receives the Lower District Sect’s messenger hawk report about Jopil, One Question, One Kill, and twenty special-detachment members in Jeongyang, then tells Wikyung that Taekyung went there.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 6, 8, and 10–25
