# Jin Mukyung (진무경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 8
- **Aliases:** Heaven Shaking Sword; Jin Family Second Young Master
- **Role:** Second son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan; a young Peak-level genius swordsman
- **Personality:** Not yet directly observed; the coachman speaks of him with intense admiration
- **Voice:** Not yet heard
- **Relationships:** Taekyung’s older brother; the coachman mistakenly identifies Taekyung as him
- **Continuity:** Reached the Peak realm before the age of twenty and is known throughout Shanxi as the Heaven Shaking Sword; at twenty-five, he is a cadet at Heaven’s Gate Temple
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 4 and 8
