# Lee Cheonbaek (이천백)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 20
- **Aliases:** Blood Wolf Sword
- **Role:** Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect; Lee Seogeun’s father
- **Personality:** Grief-stricken, resolute, and fiercely vengeful
- **Voice:** Quietly mournful when addressing his son; firm and uncompromising when declaring revenge
- **Relationships:** Father of Lee Seogeun; leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect
- **Continuity:** Finds Lee Seogeun’s poisoned corpse and is affected by the poison through touch before expelling it with his internal energy. He vows to remember his son’s pain and make the perpetrators pay a hundred or a thousand times over. He leaves the pavilion and orders about two hundred armed martial artists to begin Mount Heng’s revenge that night.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 20
