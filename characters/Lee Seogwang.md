# Lee Seogwang (이석광)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 33
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Young Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect; Lee Cheonbaek’s son; Lee Seogeun’s elder brother
- **Personality:** Frightened, submissive, and shaken by the destruction of his detachment
- **Voice:** Hesitant and trembling when reporting failure to his father
- **Relationships:** Son of Lee Cheonbaek; elder brother of Lee Seogeun
- **Continuity:** Ordered to annihilate the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s branch and advance to Jeongyang, Seogwang instead pushed to Honju after accepting Jopil’s false report that Jin Taekyung had been captured. He returns with only twenty-three Mount Heng survivors, including himself, and Lee Cheonbaek orders him back to the sect.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 33
