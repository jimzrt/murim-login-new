# Gong Yacheong (공야청)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 25
- **Aliases:** Uncle Gong
- **Role:** Guide and protector of the Sakju Branch survivors Socheon and Soyul
- **Personality:** Weary, responsible, and determined to keep the children alive despite the pursuit
- **Voice:** Protective and restrained
- **Relationships:** Longtime friend of Socheon’s father, the Sakju Branch Leader; guardian of Socheon and Soyul during their flight
- **Continuity:** Finds Socheon and Soyul after they fail to return, warns that they have been followed, and leads them toward Honju. When the pursuers catch up, he hands Soyul to Socheon and stays behind to bring up the rear. Taekyung rescues him after his fall, and Gong explains the Sakju Branch massacre and identifies Jopil, One Question, One Kill, as its leader. He and the children are the three survivors recognized by the System. Taekyung gives them the false name Hong Gil-dong before Hyuk Mujin exposes his identity. 
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 24–25
