# Black Mountain Blade (흑산도)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 24
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** First Rate wandering martial artist; Jopil’s right-hand man
- **Personality:** Ruthless participant in Jopil’s hunt; deferential to Jopil’s command
- **Voice:** No distinctive speech pattern established
- **Relationships:** Subordinate to Jopil, One Question, One Kill
- **Continuity:** Helps massacre Jin Family survivors from the Sakju Branch. Jopil orders him to take half the group and pursue one martial artist and two children who escaped through Jeongyang toward Honju. In Chapter 24, he leads the pursuit and is identified as Level 32 before Taekyung defeats him with the final form of the Jin Family’s Spear Technique.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 23–24
