# Black Mountain Blade (흑산도)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 23
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** First Rate wandering martial artist; Jopil’s right-hand man
- **Personality:** Ruthless participant in Jopil’s hunt; deferential to Jopil’s command
- **Voice:** No distinctive speech pattern established

- **Continuity:** Helps massacre Jin Family survivors from the Sakju Branch. Jopil orders him to take half the group and pursue one martial artist and two children who escaped through Jeongyang toward Honju.
