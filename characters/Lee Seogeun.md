# Lee Seogeun (이석근)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 15
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Mount Heng Sword Sect disciple and brother of the sect daughter accused of being assaulted by Jin Taekyung
- **Personality:** Hostile, suspicious, and contemptuous; treats the accusation as settled fact
- **Voice:** Initially formal and probing, then openly abusive
- **Relationships:** Member of the Mount Heng Sword Sect; hostile toward Taekyung
- **Continuity:** Lv. 30. He confronts Taekyung in the main assembly hall, displays intense killing intent, presents witness statements about the Myeongwollu incident, and challenges him to a duel as the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s envoy. He demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan. Taekyung defeats him in the duel; a masked assassin then kills him in the departing Jin Family carriage with a blue-black needle.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 13–15
