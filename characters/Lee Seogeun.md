# Lee Seogeun (이석근)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 14
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Mount Heng Sword Sect disciple and brother of the sect daughter accused of being assaulted by Jin Taekyung
- **Personality:** Hostile, suspicious, and contemptuous; treats the accusation as settled fact
- **Voice:** Initially formal and probing, then openly abusive
- **Relationships:** Member of the Mount Heng Sword Sect; hostile toward Taekyung
- **Continuity:** Lv. 30. He confronts Taekyung in the main assembly hall, displays intense killing intent, presents witness statements about the Myeongwollu incident, and challenges him to a duel as the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s envoy. He demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan. During the duel, Taekyung blocks his attacks and kicks his unguarded chest, despite Lee’s initial Intimidation effect.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 13–14
