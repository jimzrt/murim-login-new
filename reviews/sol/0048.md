# Review

Several meaning-level defects: the 총무/국무총리 joke is turned visual, 막내야 is stripped of kinship, the capsule is recast as a fresh lie rather than a lie left in place, and 일단은 is rewritten as a base pay figure.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 얼핏 들으면 고시원 총무가 아니라 국무총리인 줄 알겠다.
- **Current:** At a glance, anyone would think he was the prime minister instead of a goshiwon manager.
- **Defect:** The joke is auditory (총무 vs 국무총리) and cued by 들으면; “at a glance” makes it visual and drops the mechanism.
- **Correction:** If you only heard that, you’d think he was the prime minister, not a goshiwon manager.
- **Rationale:** 얼핏 들으면 names hearing as the basis for mistaking 총무 for 국무총리.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 막내야, 살아남아라.
- **Current:** “Kid, survive.”
- **Defect:** 막내야 is a youngest-child/youngest-member address, not a generic “kid.”
- **Correction:** “Youngest, survive.”
- **Rationale:** 막내 is kinship/hierarchy, not age-slang; “Kid” erases that cue.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 진호 형에게는 허무맹랑한 거짓말로 남는 게 좋을 것 같다는 판단이었다.
- **Current:** It seemed better to let Jinho hyung believe some absurd lie.
- **Defect:** The capsule story is to remain an absurd lie; the draft reads as a decision to feed him a new lie.
- **Correction:** I figured it was better if, for Jinho hyung, it just stayed an absurd lie.
- **Rationale:** 남는 게 marks leaving the prior capsule account as a lie, not inventing one.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “좀 더 들어오긴 했는데 일단은.”
- **Current:** “I got a little more, but that’s the basic amount.”
- **Defect:** 일단은 is a hedge (“for now”), not a statement that ten million is the base pay; the contract base is later 300,000 won.
- **Correction:** “A little more came in, but that’s for now.”
- **Rationale:** 일단은 is temporal withholding, not a quantity label.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** “네, 네가 왜 거기 있어?”
- **Current:** “Y-you? What are you doing here?”
- **Defect:** Kim asks 거기 (“there”); only the reply uses 여기 (“here”).
- **Correction:** “Y-you? What are you doing there?”
- **Rationale:** The 거기/여기 shift is in the source pair and should be kept.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 내가 한 걸음 내딛자 김상식이 힘없이 뒷걸음질 친다.
- **Current:** I took a step forward, and Kim Sangshik stumbled backward.
- **Defect:** 힘없이 뒷걸음질 is a feeble retreat, not a stumble.
- **Correction:** I took a step forward, and Kim Sangshik backed away weakly.
- **Rationale:** 뒷걸음질 친다 is stepping back; 힘없이 is lack of strength, not loss of footing.

## F07 — Minor

## F10 — Minor

- **Correction:** His face was dazed, and his voice was badly hoarse.
