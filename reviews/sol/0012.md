# Review

One critical subject reversal on Seong Jinho waking Taekyung; several major losses of Murim identity, coping-thought causality, form numbering, and register, plus System/footnote formatting defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 일어났으면 해장국이라도 한 그릇 하자고 날 깨웠어야 했다.
- **Current:** If I had woken up, he should have woken me too, so we could at least have a bowl of hangover soup.
- **Defect:** Omitted subject is Jinho, already collapsed/idle; the draft makes Taekyung the one who woke.
- **Correction:** If he'd gotten up, he should have woken me so we could at least have a bowl of hangover soup.
- **Rationale:** 자빠진 거야 frames Jinho as the one who should have risen and then woken Taekyung; 날 깨웠어야 requires Jinho as waker, not Taekyung waking himself.
