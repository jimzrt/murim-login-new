# Review

Action sequence error around Wipeng’s seizure imitation; several hierarchy, register, and joke cues flattened (vulgar name slip, 경련, 똥칠, 삼공차/님, casualty quantity, anxiety cause).

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 나는 깜짝 놀라 외쳤다.
“세상에, 위 대협!”

이 인간 간질 환자구나. 아니, 무슨 절정 고수씩이나 되는 양반이 간질에 걸렸대?

내가 황급히 일어나려 할 때 떨림이 멈췄다.
- **Current:** I jumped up in alarm.

“My God, Great Hero Wipeng!”

*This man’s epileptic! No, how does a Peak master even have epilepsy?*

Just as I hurriedly tried to stand, the trembling stopped.
- **Defect:** He only shouts in shock, then is about to stand; the draft already has him on his feet, then trying to stand.
- **Correction:** I cried out in alarm. “My God, Great Hero Wipeng!” … Just as I hurriedly tried to stand, the trembling stopped.
- **Rationale:** 일어나려 할 때 marks an intended rise after the shout, not a completed jump.
