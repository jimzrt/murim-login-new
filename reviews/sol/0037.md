# Review

One reversed quantity in the handshake aside, plus a wrong mountain romanization and two reaction/description shifts that change astonishment and clothing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 명성치 올리려고 잡은 손이 한 둘이냐. 아마 그들 중 하나였겠지.
- **Current:** How many hands had I shaken while trying to raise my Fame? One or two?

He was probably one of them.
- **Defect:** 한 둘이냐 is a rhetorical denial: the hands shaken were many, not one or two. The English reads as if the number might actually be one or two.
- **Correction:** It wasn’t as if I’d shaken only one or two hands while trying to raise my Fame. He was probably one of them.
- **Rationale:** The idiom marks quantity as more than a couple; rendering it as “One or two?” reverses that count and the joke that Gwak Jun was lost in a crowd of Fame handshakes.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 이틀 전 적들의 원군이 오태산을 넘었다는군.
- **Current:** It seems the enemy reinforcements crossed Mount Ota two days ago.
- **Defect:** 오태산 romanizes as Otae, not Ota. The place name is wrong.
- **Correction:** It seems the enemy reinforcements crossed Mount Otae two days ago.
- **Rationale:** 오태 is Otae; Ota would be 오타. An unlisted toponym still has to match the syllables on the page.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “허어.”
- **Current:** “What?”
- **Defect:** 허어 is impressed astonishment, not a request for clarification or a confused “What?”
- **Correction:** “Hoh.”
- **Rationale:** The Head Elder’s first spoken reaction to the hidden bows is wonder; the tent’s uproar and Taekyung’s dropped jaw follow that beat, not puzzlement.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 시커먼 사내놈들의 뜨거운 시선에 막사가 후끈 달아오른다.
- **Current:** The tent grew hot beneath the fervent gazes of the dark-clad men.
- **Defect:** 시커먼 describes the men as swarthy/dark-looking, not dressed in dark clothes. Clothing color is not in the source.
- **Correction:** The tent grew hot beneath the fervent gazes of those swarthy men.
- **Rationale:** Black martial robes are specified later for Gwak Jun’s group. Calling the council “dark-clad” adds a costume fact the Korean does not give.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.83
- **Source:** 태원진가의 무사 삼백과 중소 문파의 지원군 백오십. 도합 사백오십의 병력이 협곡을 향해 떠났다.
- **Current:** three hundred martial artists from the Jin Family of Taiyuan and one hundred fifty reinforcements from the small and mid-sized sects departed for the gorge.
- **Defect:** The explicit total of four hundred fifty is omitted.
- **Correction:** three hundred martial artists from the Jin Family of Taiyuan and one hundred fifty reinforcements from the small and mid-sized sects—four hundred fifty in all—departed for the gorge.
- **Rationale:** 삼백 and 백오십 remain, but 도합 사백오십 is a stated headcount, not just arithmetic the reader is left to do.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.80
- **Source:** 두 배에 달하는 병력
- **Current:** They had nearly twice our numbers
- **Defect:** 달하는 is “amounting to twice,” not “nearly twice.”
- **Correction:** They had twice our numbers
- **Rationale:** The hedge changes the force ratio Jin Taekyung uses to explain why the enemy will choose a clash.
