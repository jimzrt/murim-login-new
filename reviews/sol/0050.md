# Review

The D-rank Gate raid is mostly faithful, but a Guild Master callback joke is broken, lizardman eye color is misparsed, and the Guild Master’s self-reference reads as a third person.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 궁금하지? 이 인간이 또 뭔 지랄을 떨려고 아침부터 불렀나, 싶지? / 맞아. / 지랄 떨려고 부른 거라고.
- **Current:** You’re curious, aren’t you? Wondering what the hell I called you in first thing this morning to do? / That’s right. / I called you to raise hell.
- **Defect:** The first line never mentions raising hell, so 맞아 has nothing specific to confirm and the repeated 지랄 punchline is lost.
- **Correction:** You’re curious, aren’t you? Figuring I called you in first thing this morning to raise hell? / That’s right. / I called you to raise hell.
- **Rationale:** The joke is that Kim’s guessed 지랄 is exactly why he was summoned; both halves have to name that fit.
