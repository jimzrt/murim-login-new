# Review

Several semantic mismatches: a meal-time calque for 밥 먹고 ~만, 혀를 내두르다 rendered as a tongue-click, knockback speed reattached to landing, 그 대신 reduced to a generic But, and 여러분들이 flattened to ambiguous You.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “이야, 넌 밥 먹고 이것만 했냐?”
- **Current:** “Damn, did you do nothing but this after you ate?”
- **Defect:** 밥 먹고 ~만 하다 is an idiom for doing nothing else with one’s life, not an action after a meal.
- **Correction:** “Damn, have you been doing nothing but this?”
- **Rationale:** The line praises obsessive specialization. Post-meal wording invents a lunch break the source does not mention.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 임꺽정이 혀를 내둘렀다.
- **Current:** Im Kkeokjeong clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** 혀를 내두르다 is astonishment; tongue-clicking is 혀를 차다 and reads as disapproval.
- **Correction:** Im Kkeokjeong was blown away.
- **Rationale:** He is reacting to Taekyung’s nonstop butchering, then calling him a tough bastard. A click reverses the affect.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 빠르게 튕겨 나온 최 팀장이 일그러진 얼굴로 말했다.
- **Current:** Team Leader Choi was knocked back. He landed quickly and spoke with a twisted expression.
- **Defect:** 빠르게 modifies being flung back. “Landed quickly” is added and reassigns the adverb.
- **Correction:** Team Leader Choi was sent flying back. With a twisted expression, he said,
- **Rationale:** The black mass slams his side; the next beat is the grim line to stop it, not a controlled quick landing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** “제사장을 먼저 처리한다면 희망이 있습니다. 그 대신…….”
- **Current:** “If we take out the Priest first, there’s hope. But…”
- **Defect:** 그 대신 signals a tradeoff/cost, not a generic contrast.
- **Correction:** “If we take out the Priest first, there’s hope. In exchange…”
- **Rationale:** The interrupted catch is the price of hope; the next lines assign the party to hold the Great Warrior while Choi moves.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “아뇨. 여러분들이.”
- **Current:** “No. You.”
- **Defect:** 여러분들이 is plural and excludes Choi. Bare You can read as Im alone.
- **Correction:** “No. All of you.”
- **Rationale:** Choi answers 우리가요? by pushing the hold onto the rest of the party, then recasts Haste himself.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 빠른 속도에 슬쩍 다가온 임꺽정도 입을 벌렸다.
- **Current:** Im Kkeokjeong, who had quietly approached at my speed, even gaped.
- **Defect:** 빠른 속도에 is why he sidles over, not the speed of his approach.
- **Correction:** Im Kkeokjeong, who had sidled over at that speed, even dropped his mouth open.
- **Rationale:** He is drawn by the butchering pace; “at my speed” can mean he himself moved that fast.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.82
- **Source:** “모두 비켜!”
- **Current:** “Everyone, move!”
- **Defect:** 비켜 is clear the path for a charge, not a generic relocate.
- **Correction:** “Everyone, out of the way!”
- **Rationale:** Im is about to rush the restored gate with the tower shield; the command is to make space.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.78
- **Source:** 레이드 중에 한눈을 팔다니.
- **Current:** Who was I to let my mind wander during a raid?
- **Defect:** Adds the English idiom “who was I to,” which frames unworthiness rather than the source’s “I’ve lost it / I zoned out.”
- **Correction:** Zoning out in the middle of a raid.
- **Rationale:** The preceding line is 내가 단단히 미쳤구나; this fragment continues that self-rebuke, not a rights claim.
