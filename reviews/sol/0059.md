# Review

The draft tracks the fight and raid turn, but several action, motive, address, and terminology errors are actionable; the largest are the omitted darkened-face beat, Taekyung merely “facing” the Head Elder, and 흑심 rendered as ambition.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 진위경의 안색이 어두워진 그때였다.
- **Current:** It was then that the First Elder’s Sword Energy, advancing without resistance, suddenly changed direction.
- **Defect:** Omits Jin Wikyung’s darkened expression, the timing beat that frames the redirected strike.
- **Correction:** Jin Wikyung’s face darkened. It was then that the First Elder’s Sword Energy, rushing forward without pause, suddenly changed direction.
- **Rationale:** The source times the next attack to Wikyung’s darkened look, not only to the Sword Energy’s turn.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 눈에 넣어도 아프지 않을 막냇동생이 대장로를 막고 있다.
- **Current:** His youngest brother—the brother so precious that he could put him in his eye without feeling pain—was facing the Head Elder.
- **Defect:** 막고 있다 is blocking/intercepting the Head Elder, not merely facing him.
- **Correction:** His youngest brother—so precious he could put him in his eye and feel no pain—was blocking the Head Elder.
- **Rationale:** Wikyung’s panic is that Taekyung is standing in the Head Elder’s way, which is why delay is fatal.

## F03 — Major

- **Rationale:** Ambition prejudges the later exchange in which Wikyung denies that the plan was for wealth, glory, or becoming a provincial ruler.

## F04 — Major
