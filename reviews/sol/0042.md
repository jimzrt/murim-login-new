# Review

Major fidelity hits: Im’s line on Choi liking equipment recognition is reversed into Choi lecturing; 인원이 없어 is rendered as zero members; 고생하셨습니다 is boss-to-subordinate ‘Good work.’ Minors: Dongdaemun lacks a first-use note, 기세를 탄 is dropped, and 웃어? is recast as third-person narration.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 저 친구 장비 알아봐 주는 거 엄청 좋아해.
- **Current:** That guy really loves telling people about his equipment.
- **Defect:** 알아봐 주다 is other people recognizing/identifying his gear, not Choi explaining it.
- **Correction:** That guy really loves it when people recognize his equipment.
- **Rationale:** The beat is Taekyung naming the Red Drake set; Im explains Choi likes being made much of for that, not that he loves giving lectures.
