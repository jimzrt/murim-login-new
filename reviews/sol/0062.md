# Review

Major register softening of 지랄 and omitted stacked level-up continuation understate quantity/tone; first-use 궁귀문 lacks a footnote; a few subject, judgment, and hierarchy nits.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** - 레벨 업!
- 레벨 업!
- 레벨 업!
.
.
- 레벨 업의 중첩 효과로 모든 상태 이상이 회복됩니다!
- **Current:** - Level up!
> - Level up!
> - Level up!
> - All status ailments have been cured by the cumulative effect of the level-ups!
- **Defect:** The source’s `. .` after three notices marks further consecutive level-ups before the stack-heal line; the draft presents exactly three.
- **Correction:** Keep the three Level up! lines, then a continuation mark (ellipsis or extra Level up! lines) before the cumulative-heal notice.
- **Rationale:** Quantity and System presentation: 중첩 효과 follows an indicated run of additional level-ups, not a closed set of three.
