# Review

Several semantic defects: a negated emotion is flipped, the gate youth’s smile is recast as the gatekeeper receiving an answer, killing-intent comparison is inverted in one sentence, 익히다 is overstated as mastery, and 고시원 형 is identified as a manager. Smaller register, onomatopoeia, and glossary/footnote misses remain.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 짜증이 아니다. 그저 의심이 들 뿐이다.
- **Current:** I wasn’t merely annoyed. I was suspicious.
- **Defect:** Negation flipped: the source denies irritation and allows only suspicion; “wasn’t merely annoyed” keeps annoyance and adds suspicion.
- **Correction:** It wasn’t irritation. I was simply suspicious.
- **Rationale:** 짜증이 아니다 is a full negation, not a hedge under 단지/단지 …만이 아니다. 그저 … 뿐이다 limits the reaction to suspicion alone.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 내가 겪어 온 그것과는 차원이 다르다.
- **Current:** What I had experienced before was on an entirely different level.
- **Defect:** Comparison subject reversed: the new killing intent, not prior monster killing intent, is the thing on another level.
- **Correction:** This was on an entirely different level from anything I had experienced.
- **Rationale:** 그것과는 marks prior experience as the baseline. The following 하급/중급 contrast treats Lee Seogeun’s killing intent as the stronger, more refined one.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 태원진가 수문위사의 긴장 섞인 물음에 선두의 청년이 웃었다. 비뚜름하게 올라간 입술에는 감출 수 없는 적의가 배어 있었다.
- **Current:** At the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s gate, the tense gatekeeper received an answer from the young man leading the group. His lips curled into a crooked smile, unmistakable hostility seeping through.
- **Defect:** The narrated action is the lead youth smiling at the gate guard’s tense question, not the gatekeeper receiving an answer; location “at the gate” is also added.
- **Correction:** At the Jin Family of Taiyuan gate guard’s tense question, the young man in the lead smiled. Hostility he could not hide seeped from his crooked, lifted lips.
- **Rationale:** 물음에 … 웃었다 is cause then smile. 수문위사 is the asker; 선두의 청년 is the one who smiles before “항산.”
