# Review

Several meaning-level defects: a dropped melee-training causal line, a misattached ‘overwhelmed’ subject, Wikyung’s dismissal of worry flattened into ‘never mind,’ and a plot-gloss footnote. No scene-identity reversal in the letter once read as an appositive.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 요령만 가르쳐도 난전에서는 확실한 효과를 발휘할 것이다.
- **Current:** Not for their sake.

For my survival.

I worked like a dog for seven years.
- **Defect:** Omits the mechanical reason he would drill them: even a few tricks would clearly work in a melee.
- **Correction:** Not for their sake. For my survival. Even teaching them a few tricks would make a real difference in a melee.
- **Rationale:** The Korean states a combat-effectiveness claim between the survival motive and the seven-year thought; dropping it loses why training matters now.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 헌터 훈련소에서 배운 지식, 훈련은 우주 저 멀리 날아가고 원초적인 죽음의 냄새에 압도되는 것이다.
- **Current:** Everything they had learned and trained for at the Hunter training center flew straight out into space, overwhelmed by the primal scent of death.
- **Defect:** Makes the training the thing overwhelmed; the source’s subject of 압도되는 is the rookie Hunters.
- **Correction:** Everything they had learned and trained for at the Hunter training center flew off into outer space, and they were overwhelmed by the primal scent of death.
- **Rationale:** Two coordinated results: knowledge/training vanish, and the newbies are crushed by the smell of death.

## F03 — Major

- **Defect:** Shifts the care onto the rookies; veterans administered the rookies’ mental care.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 난 또 뭐라고. 아닐세. / 아닙니까?
- **Current:** “What are you talking about? Never mind.”

“Never mind?”
- **Defect:** Wikyung is not brushing Wipeng off; he thought the topic was something else and denies that Taekyung is the worry.
- **Correction:** “I thought you meant something else. That’s not it.”

“It’s not?”
- **Rationale:** 난 또 뭐라고 plus 아닐세 dismisses worry about Taekyung after the identification; 아닙니까 questions that denial, which then cues the ‘don’t treat him like a child’ speech.

## F05 — Major

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** ……정양입니다.
- **Current:** “……Jeongyang.”[^2]

[^2]: The final repetition emphasizes Jin Wikyung’s sudden fear that Taekyung may be heading toward the danger in Jeongyang.
- **Defect:** Adds a plot-interpretation footnote the source does not state and that English readers do not need.
- **Correction:** Delete [^2] and the note; leave “……Jeongyang.”
- **Rationale:** Footnotes are for culture/terms, not for explaining an already staged realization; this also adds motive/fear as narrator fact.
