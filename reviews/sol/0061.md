# Review

Critical unit errors (일 갑자 as ten years; 다섯 장 as fifteen feet) plus several mechanism, subject, terminology, and register misses.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 대장로의 단전에 웅크리고 있던 일 갑자의 공력이 전신 사지백해로 뻗어 나갔다.
- **Current:** The ten years of internal energy coiled in the Head Elder’s dantian spread through every limb and bone.
- **Defect:** 일 갑자 is a sexagenary unit of internal energy (sixty years), not ten years.
- **Correction:** The sixty years of internal energy coiled in the Head Elder’s dantian spread through every limb and bone.
- **Rationale:** 갑자/甲子 is the sixty-year cycle; rendering it as ten years reverses the quantity of qi he unleashes to form Sword Force.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 한 걸음 만에 다섯 장의 거리를 압축시킨 그가 내게로 검을 내리그었다.
- **Current:** With a single step, he compressed the distance of fifteen feet and brought his sword down toward me.
- **Defect:** 다섯 장 is about fifteen meters / fifty feet, not fifteen feet.
- **Correction:** With a single step, he compressed the distance of fifty feet and brought his sword down toward me.
- **Rationale:** One 장 is ten 자 (~3 m / ~10 ft). The draft treats 장 like a foot/yard and shrinks an unblockable closing step to close range.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 한순간에 공력이 흩어지고 내부가 진탕된 진위경의 얼굴이 창백하게 변했다.
- **Current:** His internal energy scattered in an instant, and his organs shook violently. Jin Wikyung’s face went pale.
- **Defect:** The subject of the scattered qi and wrenched insides is Jin Wikyung; unspecified His can attach to the Head Elder.
- **Correction:** Jin Wikyung’s internal energy scattered in an instant, and his insides were wrenched. His face went pale.
- **Rationale:** Korean names 진위경 as the subject of the whole result of the treasure-sword being cut.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 예전 같았다면 검기로 가로막는 모든 걸 베었을 것이다.
- **Current:** If he had been as he once was, his Sword Energy would have cut apart everything it blocked.
- **Defect:** The Sword Energy is the cutting means, not the thing doing the blocking; 가로막는 모든 것 is what stood in his way.
- **Correction:** If he had been as he once was, he would have cut down everything in his way with Sword Energy.
- **Rationale:** 검기로 modifies the cutting; the draft reverses the mechanics of how he used to stop surrounding blades.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** ‘장로들과 산서오문의 문주들.’
- **Current:** The Elders and the leaders of the Five Gates of Shanxi.
- **Defect:** 문주 is the bound term Sect Leader, not generic leaders.
- **Correction:** The Elders and the Sect Leaders of the Five Gates of Shanxi.
- **Rationale:** Exact glossary match: 문주 → Sect Leader.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 물론 가차 없이 사람을 반쪽 내는 신선은 없겠지만.
- **Current:** Of course, there were no immortals who casually cut people in half.
- **Defect:** 가차 없이 is merciless, not casual.
- **Correction:** Of course, there were no immortals who mercilessly cut people in half.
- **Rationale:** The joke contrasts immortal bearing with ruthless bisecting; casually weakens the violence.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 자조하듯 중얼거린 그가 검을 들어 올렸다.
- **Current:** He muttered as if speaking to himself, then raised his sword.
- **Defect:** 자조하듯 is self-mocking; the draft keeps only the mutter.
- **Correction:** He muttered as if mocking himself, then raised his sword.
- **Rationale:** After Taekyung’s accusation, the source marks self-derision, not mere private speech (중얼거린 already covers muttering).

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 시산혈해, 아비규환. 말 그대로의 광경이 눈앞에 있었다.
- **Current:** A sea of corpses and blood. Pandemonium. The scene was right in front of us, exactly as described.
- **Defect:** 말 그대로 means the idioms were literal; exactly as described invents a prior description and drops 시산.
- **Correction:** Mountains of corpses, a sea of blood. Pandemonium. The scene before us was literally that.
- **Rationale:** The point is that the set phrases are the actual battlefield, not that they match some unspecified description.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 여러 이유로 한때 의좋은 형제였던 우리는 점차 멀어졌다.
- **Current:** For many reasons, the brothers who had once been close gradually grew apart.
- **Defect:** 우리는 is first-person; the brothers turns the Head Elder’s own history into a third-person summary.
- **Correction:** For many reasons, we brothers, who had once been close, gradually grew apart.
- **Rationale:** He is speaking of himself and his elder brother.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** 진위경이 파리한 얼굴로 되물었다.
- **Current:** Jin Wikyung asked in a bloodless voice,
- **Defect:** 파리한 얼굴 is a wan face, not the voice.
- **Correction:** Jin Wikyung asked with a wan face,
- **Rationale:** The source’s cue is visual pallor, later echoed by his pale smile.

## F11 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.78
- **Source:** ‘썩어도 준치라더니.’
- **Current:** Even a rotten fish is still a fish.
- **Defect:** The idiom is that a prized fish (준치) is still a prized fish even rotten, i.e. still first-rate in decline.
- **Correction:** Even rotten, a prized fish is still a prized fish.
- **Rationale:** Still a fish is nearly tautological and underplays why the one-armed Head Elder remains formidable.
