# Review

Mostly faithful, but Wikyung’s war speech drops the attribution of the rabble to ‘that man,’ and Hyuk’s comic collapse is mistranslated as an eye-roll.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 그 순간, 사나이 혁무진이 눈을 부릅뜨며 주저앉았다.
- **Current:** At that moment, the manly Hyuk Mujin rolled his eyes wide and collapsed to the ground.
- **Defect:** 부릅뜨다 is eyes flaring/bulging open (pain, strain), not rolling the eyes; 주저앉다 is sinking into a sit as his body gives out. The draft turns a physical failure into sarcasm.
- **Correction:** At that moment, the manly Hyuk Mujin’s eyes bulged wide and he sank to the ground.
- **Rationale:** Same verb is later rendered correctly as eyes flying open when he sees Wikyung. Here it must stay a pain/shock flare, or the 사나이 혁무진 punchline (boast, then body fails) is a different joke.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.82
- **Source:** 머릿수만 많은 승냥이에 불과하다. 저자의 무뢰배, 황금에 눈이 먼 낭인과 양민을 약탈하던 마적 떼!
- **Current:** They are nothing but jackals with numbers on their side. A pack of ruffians—wandering martial artists blinded by gold and mounted bandits who have plundered the common people!
- **Defect:** 저자의 is omitted. 저자 is a singular, contemptuous ‘that man’; the force is named as his rabble, not a generic pack.
- **Correction:** They are nothing but jackals with numbers on their side. That man’s rabble—wandering martial artists blinded by gold and a pack of mounted bandits who have plundered the common people!
- **Rationale:** The speech first concedes numbers and Peak masters, then recasts the enemy as that opponent’s hired thugs (wanderers and mounted bandits). Losing 저자 strips the personal indictment.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 오백의 병력은 선두, 중앙, 후미로 나뉘었다.
- **Current:** The five hundred troops were divided into the vanguard, the main force, and the rear guard.
- **Defect:** 중앙 is the center. The next sentence already uses “the center” for the same word; “main force” is a different unit concept.
- **Correction:** The five hundred troops were divided into the vanguard, the center, and the rear guard.
- **Rationale:** 선두 / 중앙 / 후미 is a three-part front–center–rear split. Rendering 중앙 two ways in adjacent sentences is inconsistent and slightly mislabels the middle body.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.74
- **Source:** 아니, 오르긴 오른다. 정말 쥐똥만큼. 조금씩, 아주 조금씩.
- **Current:** No, it was going up. Just barely. Little by little. Very, very little.
- **Defect:** 쥐똥만큼 is a comic unit of measure (a rat dropping’s worth), collapsed into generic scarcity already covered by 조금씩.
- **Correction:** No, it was going up. Really, by about a rat dropping. Little by little. Very, very little.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung’s voice uses the crude image to mock the trickle of Fame; “just barely” keeps quantity but drops the joke.
