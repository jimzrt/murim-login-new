# Review

Several semantic and register errors: Wipeng’s yellow-to-gold “bad seed” pun is recast as wealth; 장중보옥을 건드렸다 is weakened to “provoked… jade”; Wikyung’s “뭔 레기?” and 지랄한다 are sanitized; 눈이 뒤집혔다 becomes a literal faint; 북망산 is misromanized and stripped of its graveyard/death idiom; the assassination shout and burning-throat cue are blurred.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 오늘 항산검문의 장중보옥을 건드렸다는 얘길 듣고 깨달음이 왔다.
- **Current:** But when he heard today that the Third Young Master had provoked Mount Heng Sword Sect’s most treasured jade, enlightenment had struck him.
- **Defect:** 건드렸다 is laying hands on their cherished person-treasure, not “provoking” an object called jade; the alleged offense is misstated.
- **Correction:** But when he heard today that the Third Young Master had laid a hand on the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s cherished jewel, it dawned on him.
- **Rationale:** 장중보옥 is the palm-treasure metaphor for a beloved child; 건드리다 is physical interference with that person, which is what Wipeng treats as proof Taekyung never changed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 위팽의 눈에 비친 진태경은 싹수가 노란 정도를 넘어 황금빛에 가깝다.
- **Current:** In his eyes, Jin Taekyung was beyond merely being a bad seed—he was practically a golden spoon.[^1]
- **Defect:** 황금빛 extends the yellow of 싹수가 노랗다 (hopeless) into gold; it is not 금수저 / born-rich “golden spoon,” and the footnote teaches the wrong joke.
- **Correction:** In his eyes, Jin Taekyung was past a bad seed with yellow sprouts—he was practically gold.[^1] Footnote: 싹수가 노랗다 (“the sprouts are yellow”) means a hopeless case; the line pushes yellow to gold to intensify that, not inherited wealth.
- **Rationale:** The next sentence contrasts that hopelessness with family status. Recasting the pun as privilege reverses Wipeng’s evaluation in this line.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “뭔 레기?”

어버. 어버버.

발음도 제대로 못 하는 무사를 가만히 내려다보던 진위경이
- **Current:** “Wh—what?”

“Buh… buh-buh…”

Jin Wikyung looked down at the warrior, who could no longer pronounce anything properly.
- **Defect:** 뭔 레기? is a contemptuous “what trash?” (from 쓰레기), almost certainly Wikyung’s line before the warrior’s broken 어버; the draft makes a confused “Wh—what?” and drops the insult.
- **Correction:** “What trash?”

Buh. Buh-buh.

Jin Wikyung looked down calmly at the warrior, who could no longer get the words out.
- **Rationale:** Wikyung’s casual contempt is the beat before the slap; losing 레기 and assigning a stunned question changes speaker, register, and characterization.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** ‘미친놈, 지랄한다.’
- **Current:** This lunatic’s completely lost it.
- **Defect:** 지랄한다 is crude (“he’s talking shit / throwing a fit”); “completely lost it” sanitizes the profanity and collapses 미친놈 and 지랄한다 into one milder gloss.
- **Correction:** Crazy bastard. He’s talking shit.
- **Rationale:** Rules require matching strong profanity; this is Wipeng’s private register when Taekyung accepts the duel.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그 말을 들은 이소군의 눈이 뒤집혔다.
- **Current:** Lee Seogeun’s eyes rolled back at those words.
- **Defect:** 눈이 뒤집혔다 means he flew into a rage; English “eyes rolled back” reads as fainting or losing consciousness, which he does not do before the next charge.
- **Correction:** Those words made Lee Seogeun’s eyes go wild with rage.
- **Rationale:** He immediately shouts and attacks; a literal eye-roll contradicts the following action.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** - 이렇게 하자. 우선은 북망산 먼저. 항산검문은 다음에 가는 걸로 하자고.
- **Current:** - Let’s do this. We’ll go to Mount Beomang first. We can head to the Mount Heng Sword Sect afterward.
- **Defect:** 북망산 is Beimang/Bukmang, the burial mountain and a death idiom, not a place romanized Beomang (that would be 범망). The assassin’s itinerary joke is “you die first.”
- **Correction:** - Let’s do this. Mount Beimang first. We can go to the Mount Heng Sword Sect after. Add a brief footnote: Beimang is a burial mountain; “going to Beimang” means dying.
- **Rationale:** The line is the murder’s punchline, immediately followed by paralysis and the needle. A fake toponym Beomang hides that mechanism.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 누구냐! 이소군은 외쳤지만 소리가 새어 나가지 않았다.
- **Current:** Who was it?

Lee Seogeun shouted, but no sound escaped his throat.
- **Defect:** 누구냐! is the shouted line that fails to leave his throat; unquoted “Who was it?” reads as narrator/thought and splits the shout from its content.
- **Correction:** “Who is it!” Lee Seogeun shouted, but no sound escaped his throat.
- **Rationale:** The failed vocative is the first proof the Sound Transmission / needle has already locked his voice.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** 가슴이 답답하고 목이 타는 듯 아프다.
- **Current:** His chest felt constricted, and his throat burned as if it were parched.
- **Defect:** 목이 타는 듯 아프다 is burning pain in the throat, not thirst/parchedness; the simile changes the poisoning symptom.
- **Correction:** His chest felt tight, and his throat hurt as if it were on fire.
- **Rationale:** This is the needle/toxin taking hold, not dryness from shouting.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.82
- **Source:** 바람이 불었고, 십여 개의 이빨이 하늘을 날았다.
- **Current:** The wind split, and more than a dozen teeth went flying through the air.
- **Defect:** 바람이 불었고 is “the wind blew,” not split; 십여 개 is about ten, not more than twelve.
- **Correction:** The wind blew, and some ten teeth went flying through the air.
- **Rationale:** Quantity and the physical cue are both slightly rewritten without source support.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.80
- **Source:** 복면인은 싱긋 웃으며 망자의 미간에 꽂힌 검푸른 대침을 회수했다.
- **Current:** The masked man smiled faintly as he pulled the blue-black needle from the dead man’s brow.
- **Defect:** 싱긋 is a clear, almost sweet smile, not faint; 대침 is a large needle, reduced to an unmarked needle.
- **Correction:** The masked man smiled brightly as he retrieved the blue-black needle from the dead man’s brow.
- **Rationale:** The killer’s cheerful 싱긋 against 잘 가게, 이 소협 is the closing tone; “faintly” softens it.
