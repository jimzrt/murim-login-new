# Review

One critical speaker-identification error and several hierarchy, subject, register, and characterization defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - 전음입니다. 놀라지 말고 들으십시오.
- **Current:** > **System**
>
> - This is Sound Transmission. Do not be alarmed. Listen carefully.
- **Defect:** Wipeng’s Sound Transmission is formatted as a System message, falsely identifying the System as the speaker. The same error recurs for every subsequent Sound Transmission line.
- **Correction:** Format all of Wipeng’s transmitted speech as ordinary dialogue or normal blockquotes without a **System** heading: “This is Sound Transmission. Don’t be alarmed—just listen.”
- **Rationale:** The prose explicitly says Wipeng’s lips move and identifies the voice as Sound Transmission. These are Wipeng’s words, not game notifications.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 너무 오랫동안 수련을 하지 않아 잊고 있었다는 태원진가의 가전 무공이다.
- **Current:** They were hereditary martial arts of the Jin Family of Taiyuan—arts I had forgotten because they hadn’t been practiced in far too long.
- **Defect:** The passive “they hadn’t been practiced” obscures the subject and can imply that the family arts had fallen out of general use. The source says Taekyung had not practiced them for so long that he had forgotten them.
- **Correction:** They were hereditary martial arts of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, forgotten after he had gone far too long without practicing them.
- **Rationale:** The causal subject is Taekyung, not the techniques or the Jin Family as a whole.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 조만간 가로회의가 열릴지도 모르겠습니다. 저들 입장에서는 틈이 보이니 물어뜯는 것이 당연하지요.
- **Current:** A meeting of the elders may be held soon. From their perspective, they’ve spotted an opening. It’s only natural that they would try to tear into it.
- **Defect:** “A meeting of the elders” conflates 가로회의 with the separately named 장로원, the Elder Council. The same conflation recurs later as “the elders’ meeting.”
- **Correction:** A family council meeting may be held soon. From their perspective, they’ve spotted an opening, so naturally they’ll sink their teeth into it.
- **Rationale:** The passage distinguishes the Elder Council as the faction applying pressure from the meeting in which that pressure will be exerted.

## F05 — Major

## F06 — Major
