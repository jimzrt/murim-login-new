# Review

Actionable fidelity issues: the killing thrust SFX is rendered as a blunt thud; a gentle foot-press becomes firm; a stinging glare becomes icy; an unquoted aside is spoken; the secret request is demoted to thought and the asking verb is dropped; the earth-caving sigh gag is flattened; and 없어서 못 먹습니다 is read as appetite rather than scarcity.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “가라, 이제.” / 푹. / 끄륵. 단말마와 함께 녹색 거체가 벽면을 타고 미끄러진다.
- **Current:** “Go on. It’s over.” / Thud. / With a final gurgle, the hulking green body slid down the wall.
- **Defect:** 푹 is a piercing/plunge SFX for the finishing stab, not a dull impact. Thud makes the kill sound like a body blow or fall and drops the spear thrust.
- **Correction:** “Go now.” / Shunk. / With a death rattle, the hulking green body slid down the wall.
- **Rationale:** The kill beat is spear-in, death cry, slide. Rendering 푹 as Thud changes the mechanical action of the finishing blow.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 그 순간, 나는 한쪽 발로 창의 중앙을 지그시 눌렀다.
- **Current:** At that moment, I pressed down firmly on the middle of my spear with one foot.
- **Defect:** 지그시 is a gentle, steady press. Firmly adds effort and kills the gag that light pressure plus a three-digit Strength stat bends the spear.
- **Correction:** At that moment, I pressed down gently on the middle of the spear with one foot.
- **Rationale:** The comedy is effortless strength. Changing the pressure quality alters the action and the joke.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그래서 그에게 부탁했다. / ‘저에 관해서는 비밀로 해 주실 수 있을까요?’
- **Current:** *Could you keep this a secret?*
- **Defect:** The narration that he asked Choi is omitted, and the request is italicized as thought. The Korean is a spoken request about keeping matters concerning him secret.
- **Correction:** So I asked him. / “Could you keep what concerns me a secret?”

## F06 — Major
