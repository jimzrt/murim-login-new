# Review

Critical false identification of Choi Min-su as the C-rank; several major reversals of stance, motive, appearance, and the Guild Master’s incentive, plus System-window markup and the Sangshik address.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “예. 상동 길드 최민숩니다. 다름이 아니고……”
- **Current:** “Yes. He’s Choi Min-su from Sangdong Guild. The thing is—”
- **Defect:** Adds He is and makes Choi Min-su the measured C-rank. The Korean is self-identification (name + 입니다) plus the phone preamble 다름이 아니고, i.e. a Sangdong Guild speaker starting a call, not a report of who just tested.
- **Correction:** “Yes. This is Choi Min-su from Sangdong Guild. The thing is—”
- **Rationale:** 입니다 on a personal name is how the speaker names himself. 다름이 아니고 is the conventional ‘I’m calling because…’ opener. The next lines are lobby scouts already moving; the C-rank reveal at the elevator is Jin Taekyung. Labeling Choi the catch changes identity and the chapter’s payoff.
