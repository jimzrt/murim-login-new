# Review

Three meaning-level defects: Hayeon’s question genders Jin Wikyung as female; the taxi beat is broken by rendering 차 시간 as taxi; Wikyung’s remembered order is formatted as Taekyung’s thought after Mom’s words. One major humor/register miss on 통탄을 금치 못하겠다.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 아는 사람이야? 꿈에도 나올 정도면 친한가 보네.
- **Current:** Is it someone you know? If she even appears in your dreams, you must be close.
- **Defect:** English adds a female pronoun the Korean does not have and misgenders Jin Wikyung.
- **Correction:** Is it someone you know? If that name is showing up in your dreams, you must be close.
- **Rationale:** Hayeon uses no gender. 진위경 is already established as Taekyung’s elder brother. The later girlfriend lie is Taekyung’s, not Hayeon’s assumption.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “오빠, 차 시간 늦겠다.”
“택시 타고 갈 건데?”
- **Current:** “Oppa, you’re going to miss your taxi.”
“I’m taking a taxi.”
- **Defect:** Hayeon is made to name a taxi first, so Taekyung’s taxi line no longer rebuts a scheduled ride.
- **Correction:** “Oppa, you’re going to miss your bus.” / “I’m taking a taxi, though?”
- **Rationale:** 차 시간 is a timetable. His point is there is no such deadline because he is taking a taxi; she then pivots to the night surcharge.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 택시를 타고 고시원으로 돌아가는 길 내내 엄마의 마지막 말과 그 온기를 떠올렸다.

‘살아남아라, 뒤도 돌아보지 말고 도망치란 말이다. 그게 네 임무다.’

점점 흐릿해지는 기억 속 누군가의 목소리도.
- **Current:** All the way back to the goshiwon in the taxi, I thought about Mom’s final words and the warmth in them.

*Survive. I’m telling you to run without looking back. That’s your mission.*

And the voice of someone in a memory that was growing increasingly hazy.
- **Defect:** The Survive/run order is set as italic thought right after Mom’s words, so it can be read as hers or as narration; in the source it is the fading memory-voice.
- **Correction:** Keep Mom’s last words separate. Render “Survive. I’m telling you to run without looking back. That’s your mission.” as that hazy remembered voice, not as a thought.
- **Rationale:** The quoted command is 기억 속 누군가의 목소리. Italics-for-thought plus the following “And the voice…” splits one speaker into Mom/Taekyung plus an unnamed extra voice.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 저 인간이 서른이라니. 통탄을 금치 못하겠다.
- **Current:** I couldn’t believe this man was thirty. I was overwhelmed by despair.
- **Defect:** Mock-classical 통탄 becomes genuine despair and kills the deadpan joke.
- **Correction:** I couldn’t believe this man was thirty. I couldn’t help but lament.
- **Rationale:** 통탄을 금치 못하겠다 is comic deploring of Jinho, not Taekyung being overwhelmed.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.72
- **Source:** “네, 누나.”
- **Current:** “Yes, ma’am.”
- **Defect:** The joke is calling his younger sister 누나; ma’am keeps mock deference but drops the inverted kinship.
- **Correction:** “Yes, nuna.”
- **Rationale:** 누나 is a role reversal at his little sister, not generic politeness.
