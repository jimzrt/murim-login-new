# Review

One critical subject swap has the passenger hugging the blue truck instead of the drink box; several major lines also change who is cooking, how much danger is claimed, and the ‘mere pork belly’ beat.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 청년은 파란 트럭이 시야에서 사라질 때까지 드링크 박스를 꼭 껴안고 있었다. 마치 누군가가 뺏어 가기라도 할 것처럼.
- **Current:** The young man hugged the blue truck until it disappeared from view. As if someone might snatch it away.
- **Defect:** The hugged object is the drink box; the blue truck is only what he watches until it leaves sight. Hugging the truck reverses the action and makes the snatching simile incoherent.
- **Correction:** The young man hugged the drink box until the blue truck disappeared from view. As if someone might snatch it away.
- **Rationale:** The Korean subject-object split is explicit: 파란 트럭 is the disappearing thing, 드링크 박스를 꼭 껴안고 is the continuing action from the earlier sniffing beat.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “요리 많이 준비하고 계셔?”

- **Defect:** Honorific 계셔 marks Mom in the kitchen as the cook. English “Are you making a lot?” makes Hayeon the cook, though she has just sat down and is on her phone.

- **Rationale:** Mom is already 부엌에서 아침 준비로 한창이다; Hayeon only answers about the feast with her eyes glued to the phone.
