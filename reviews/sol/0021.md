# Review

Three scene-fact errors: 검은 is the issued sword (검+은), not a black Inventory or old clothes; 철창 is the iron spear, not a cage; Jin Wikyung gazes at the Head Elder’s smile, not the reverse.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 옷을 갈아입고, 검은 인벤토리 깊숙이 처박은 다음 [예리한 창]을 꺼냈다.
- **Current:** I changed clothes, shoved my old ones deep into the black Inventory, then pulled out the *Sharp Spear*.
- **Defect:** 검은 is 검+은 (the issued sword), not ‘black’. He stows the sword and draws the spear; old clothes and a ‘black Inventory’ are invented.
- **Correction:** I changed clothes, shoved the sword deep into my Inventory, then pulled out the *Sharp Spear*.
- **Rationale:** Issued gear is a sword, black uniform, and plaque. After putting on the uniform he hides 검 and takes out [예리한 창], which the next paragraph explains he cannot summon in communal quarters.
