# Review

Action, subject, injury, proverb, and register errors, plus missing required cultural notes; System/glossary hits in QA are mostly false leads.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 잠깐이라도 반응이 늦었다면 지금쯤 북망산을 등산하고 있었을 거다.
- **Current:** If my reaction had been even a moment slower, I would have been hiking up Mount Beimang by now.
- **Defect:** Death idiom is left as a literal mountain hike with no cue or footnote, so the meaning (he would already be dead) is not recoverable.
- **Correction:** Keep the hike wording and add a brief footnote that Mount Beimang is a burial mountain and ‘hiking it’ means being dead.
- **Rationale:** Binding footnote rules cover this historical/idiom image; continuity already treats Beimang as a death expression.
