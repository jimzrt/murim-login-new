# Review

Several speaker, subject, and mechanism errors change who speaks, what the promo line means, what Taekyung is counting, and how the God-bless-you beat works.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** ‘새로운 영웅을 기다린다!’라는 흔한 게임 홍보 멘트
- **Current:** the common game-promotional line: *A new hero awaits!*
- **Defect:** The omitted subject waits for a new hero; the English makes the hero the one who waits.
- **Correction:** the common game-promotional line: *Awaiting a new hero!*
- **Rationale:** 기다린다 takes 새로운 영웅 as object, the usual ‘we/the world await a new hero’ ad line, not ‘a new hero awaits (something)’.

## F06 — Major
