# Review

Several semantic errors: Socheon’s blazing eyes are attached to the torches; 반 시진 is rendered as half an hour; Mother is said to leave with others rather than lead them; formation orders become “spread out”; Taekyung’s stunned 응? at the levels is dropped; footwork technique is reduced to stepping; the finishing strike’s burst SFX becomes a whoosh.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 언덕 위, 소천은 불길이 쏟아지는 눈동자로 일렁이는 횃불들을 바라봤다.
- **Current:** From the top of the hill, Socheon stared at the torches flickering in the eyes where flames seemed to pour forth.
- **Defect:** The flaming eyes belong to Socheon; he is looking at the torches, not seeing torches flicker inside eyes.
- **Correction:** From the hill, Socheon stared at the flickering torches with eyes that seemed to pour fire.
- **Rationale:** 불길이 쏟아지는 눈동자로 modifies how Socheon looks; 일렁이는 횃불들 is the object.
