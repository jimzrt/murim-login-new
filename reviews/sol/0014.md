# Review

The draft reverses the Duel Quest’s honor clause, recasts the Mount Heng plan as targeting “one of us” instead of Taekyung, and several insults, kinship terms, and fight cues are softened or misread.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 쥐꼬리만큼 남은 명예와 항산검문의 분노를 피하기 위해서 남은 방법은 하나뿐입니다.
- **Current:** To avoid the tiny scrap of honor you have left and the anger of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, only one option remains.
- **Defect:** The English makes the remaining honor something to avoid, inverting the quest’s reason for fighting.
- **Correction:** To protect the tiny scrap of honor you have left and to avoid the anger of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, only one option remains.
- **Rationale:** 피하다 governs 항산검문의 분노; 쥐꼬리만큼 남은 명예 is the honor still at stake, not an object of avoidance.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이게 실패하더라도 나 하나 정도는 작살내 버리겠다는 것 같은데
- **Current:** even if that failed, they wanted to cripple at least one of us
- **Defect:** 나 하나 is Taekyung himself, not an unspecified family member.
- **Correction:** even if that failed, they were going to wreck me, at least
- **Rationale:** The narrator is the named target of the setup; “one of us” spreads the threat and changes who they mean to destroy.
