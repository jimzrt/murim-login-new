# Review

Nickname, refusal, and kinship are the load-bearing misses: Im Kkeokjeong’s bandit-folk-hero force is unexplained, 거절 is reduced to a vague No, and 아는 동생 reads as blood family.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 다만 산적을 연상시키는 외모라 모두 그를 임꺽정이라고 불렀다.
- **Current:** However, he looked enough like a bandit that everyone called him Im Kkeokjeong.
- **Defect:** First use of the unlisted nickname is romanized with no footnote. The joke and the reason everyone uses it depend on Im Kkeokjeong being the famous Korean folk-hero bandit, not a random hairy-man label.
- **Correction:** Keep Im Kkeokjeong and add a first-use footnote: famous Joseon-era folk-hero bandit, hence the look-based nickname.
- **Rationale:** Binding footnote/ledger rule for unlisted names; without the namesake, the bandit appearance → 임꺽정 causal link is lost.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “거절.”
- **Current:** “No.”
- **Defect:** 거절 is an explicit rejection of the E-rank job, not a vague negative. “No” can read as disbelief or a one-word prompt rather than Taekyung trying to turn the contract down before the porter line.
- **Correction:** “Rejected.”
- **Rationale:** The next line (positions full; porter) answers a refusal. Softening 거절 to “No” changes the speech act.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.82
- **Source:** “자, 다들 인사해. 여긴 내 아는 동생.”
- **Current:** “All right, everyone, say hello. This is a younger brother of mine.”
- **Defect:** Omits 아는 and makes 동생 sound like kinship. Source is a junior he knows, not a blood brother.
- **Correction:** “All right, everyone, say hello. This is a kid brother I know.”
- **Rationale:** Audit target is kinship. 아는 marks acquaintance; English “younger brother of mine” overstates the relationship.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.80
- **Source:** 너무 대놓고 차별하는 것 아니냐며 분노하는 사람도 있지만, 차별하는 거 맞다.
- **Current:** Some people got angry and asked if the discrimination had to be so blatant.

It did.
- **Defect:** Punchline confirms that it is discrimination, not that the blatancy “had to” exist.
- **Correction:** Some people got angry, asking if this wasn’t just open discrimination.

It was.
- **Rationale:** 차별하는 거 맞다 affirms the fact of discrimination; “It did” answers a different question.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** “젊은 친구가 훤칠하네. 잘 싸울 것 같어.”
- **Current:** “Young man, you’re quite a looker. You seem like you can fight.”
- **Defect:** 훤칠하다 is tall, lanky, striking stature, not facial attractiveness. “Looker” breaks the link to looking like he can fight.
- **Correction:** “Young man, you’re a tall one. You seem like you can fight.”
- **Rationale:** Wrong physical attribute; the compliment is build/height, not being a looker.

## F06 — Minor
