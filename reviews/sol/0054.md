# Review

Several subject and sense errors: the Jin banner is attached to the charging army, kinship is reversed from “I was a brother” to “they were brothers,” and a few register/term choices change what was said.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 시퍼렇게 날 선 병장기의 끝에, 또 다른 깃발이 있다.

진(振).

그리고 협곡을 틀어막은 수백의 무인들.
- **Current:** At the tips of their keenly honed weapons flew another flag.

Jin (振).

And hundreds of martial artists blocking the gorge.
- **Defect:** Added “their” ties the Jin flag to the charging Mount Heng force just described, instead of the separate blocking side.
- **Correction:** At the tips of keenly honed weapons was another flag.

Jin (振).

And hundreds of martial artists blocking the gorge.
- **Rationale:** The source starts a new image: weapon-tips, the Jin banner, then the hundreds sealing the gorge. It does not make that banner belong to the pack charging with the Mount Heng flag.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 그리고 누군가에게는…….

‘네가 자랑스럽구나.’
‘다친 곳은 없느냐?’
‘살아남아라, 막내야.’

피를 나눈 형제이기도 했다.
- **Current:** And to someone…

“I’m proud of you.”
“Are you hurt anywhere?”
“Survive, youngest.”

They had been blood brothers, too.
- **Defect:** Subject of kinship is reversed: the narrator was a blood brother to someone, not that the remembered group were blood brothers.
- **Correction:** And to someone…

“I’m proud of you.”
“Are you hurt anywhere?”
“Survive, youngest.”

I had been a blood brother, too.
- **Rationale:** The thread is 나는 그들에게 있어… 그리고 누군가에게는… 형제이기도 했다. The quotes are what those someones said to him. “They had been blood brothers” can also smear that bond onto Wipeng, the scouts, and the rest.

## F03 — Major

- **Current:** Butler Kim answered in a certain tone.
- **Defect:** “Certain tone” reads as “some tone or other,” not a conclusive delivery.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 자랑스러운 아들, 오빠가 되어 가족들을 호강시켜 주는 것.
- **Current:** Becoming a proud son and brother, and giving my family a comfortable life.
- **Defect:** “Proud son” makes him the one feeling pride; the Korean is a son and older brother the family can be proud of.

- **Rationale:** 자랑스러운 아들/오빠 is the family’s pride in him, which is the dream stated in that line.
