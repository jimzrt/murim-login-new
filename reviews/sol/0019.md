# Review

The System maxim in the War quest is clause-reversed; the gate scene inserts Taekyung as “I” where the guard is the perceiver; Wikyung’s “back down” subject and the 누나가 왜 거기서 나와 beat are also off.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 살아남는 자가 강하고, 강한 자만이 살아남을 것입니다.
- **Current:** The strong will survive, and only those who survive will be strong.
- **Defect:** The two clauses are reversed, so the System defines strength as a consequence of surviving instead of stating that survivors are strong and only the strong survive.
- **Correction:** Those who survive are strong, and only the strong will survive.
- **Rationale:** Source order is 살아남는 자가 강하고 then 강한 자만이 살아남을 것입니다. Swapping them changes the quest text’s causal claim.

- **Current:** The question that had briefly arisen vanished the moment I saw the coachman’s sharp eyes.
- **Defect:** Added first-person “I” in a gate-guard focalized scene, so Taekyung appears to be at the gate seeing the coachman.
- **Correction:** The question that had briefly arisen vanished the moment he saw the coachman’s sharp eyes.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding beats (범상치 않다, 수문위사 swallowing, blocking the carriage) belong to the gate guard. The Korean has no 내가; Taekyung is still in the Lesser Family Head’s office.
