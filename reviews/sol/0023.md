# Review

Critical fight mechanic reversal on Hyuk’s fourth blow; major losses of Han Yeop’s address correction, shichen duration, and the 백문이 불여일퍽 joke; smaller quantity, command, and register slips.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 하지만 용케도 쓰러지지 않았다. 그건 내가 녀석의 주먹을 놔줘야만 가능한 일이니까.
- **Current:** Yet somehow, he didn’t fall.

That was only possible because I had to let go of his fist.
- **Defect:** Reverses the causal mechanic: Hyuk stays up only because Taekyung is still holding the caught fist; falling is possible only if that grip is released. “I had to let go” makes release the reason he remains standing.
- **Correction:** Yet somehow, he didn’t fall. He couldn’t—not unless I let go of his fist.
- **Rationale:** 놔줘야만 가능한 일 attaches to collapsing, not to remaining upright. The next slap lands after that held pose; the draft’s obligation-to-release reading changes the action.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 삼공자, 아니 조장님께서 다 생각이 있으셔서 그런 게 아닐까……요?
- **Current:** Squad Leader must have a reason for doing this… right?
- **Defect:** Omits Han Yeop’s audible self-correction from Third Young Master to Squad Leader, a hierarchy and characterization beat.
- **Correction:** The Third Young Master—I mean Squad Leader—must have a reason for doing this… right?
- **Rationale:** 삼공자, 아니 조장님 is a pragmatic register shift, not filler. Dropping it flattens how he addresses Taekyung in front of Hyuk.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 불과 몇 시진 전에 정양을 통과, 혼주로 향하고 있다고 합니다.
- **Current:** They passed through Jeongyang only a few hours ago and are headed for Honju.
- **Defect:** 시진 was already rendered as two hours in this chapter; 몇 시진 is several two-hour periods, not “a few hours.”
- **Correction:** They passed through Jeongyang only a few two-hour periods ago and are headed for Honju.
- **Rationale:** 한 시진 이동 was translated as two hours of travel. Treating 몇 시진 as ordinary hours understates elapsed time and breaks the chapter’s own conversion.
