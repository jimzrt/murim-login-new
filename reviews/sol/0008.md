# Review

One spatial mistranslation and several smaller fidelity or prose issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이 층이 침실입니다. 내부 곳곳에 종을 설치해 두었으니 필요한 일이 생기시면 종을 울려 주십시오.”
- **Current:** “This floor is the bedroom. Bells have been installed throughout the building, so please ring one if you need anything.”
- **Defect:** “이 층” means the second floor here, not the floor where the servant and Taekyung are currently standing. The current wording conflicts with Taekyung immediately exploring the first-floor rooms.
- **Correction:** “The second floor contains your bedroom. Bells have been installed throughout the building, so please ring one if you need anything.”
- **Rationale:** The building has just been identified as two stories, and the following narration explicitly places the six rooms Taekyung explores on the first floor.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 2평 남짓한 고시원 원룸에 살던 내게는 올림픽 경기장이나 다름없다.
- **Current:** To someone who had lived in a goshiwon studio barely seven square meters across,[^1] this was no different from an Olympic stadium.
- **Defect:** “Seven square meters across” incorrectly treats an area measurement as a linear dimension and sounds unnatural.
- **Correction:** To someone who had lived in a goshiwon room measuring barely seven square meters,[^1] this was no different from an Olympic stadium.
- **Rationale:** Two pyeong is approximately seven square meters of floor area.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 됐다!
- **Current:** Yes!
- **Defect:** Placed directly after the System’s yes-or-no prompt, “Yes!” can be read as Taekyung accepting the martial art, even though the subsequent prose establishes that he has not accepted it.
- **Correction:** Jackpot!
- **Rationale:** Here 됐다 is a celebratory reaction to finding a martial arts manual, not an answer to the System prompt.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 정략혼으로 가문을 일으킬 수 있다면 그로서는 값싼 희생이었다.
- **Current:** If a political marriage could restore the family’s fortunes, it would be a cheap sacrifice as far as he was concerned.
- **Defect:** “Restore the family’s fortunes” adds that the family previously declined or lost its fortunes, which the source does not state.
- **Correction:** If a political marriage could strengthen the family, it would be a small price to pay as far as he was concerned.
- **Rationale:** 가문을 일으키다 means to elevate, strengthen, or bring prosperity to the family; it does not inherently imply restoration after a decline.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 다음 순간, 위팽의 입이 딱 벌어졌다. 내가 방금 뭘 본거지?
- **Current:** The next moment, Wipeng’s mouth fell open.

*What the hell did I just see?*
- **Defect:** “The hell” adds profanity absent from this reaction.
- **Correction:** The next moment, Wipeng’s mouth fell open.

*What did I just see?*
- **Rationale:** The source conveys stunned disbelief without profanity; the chapter’s explicit profanity appears later in Taekyung’s speech and should retain that contrast.
