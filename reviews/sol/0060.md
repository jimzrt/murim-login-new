# Review

Two scene facts are wrong: survivors actively deflecting the stone volley become passive victims, and the encircling-formation order is rendered as dispersal. Several register/action cues are also off (feigned calm, the chili euphemism, scruff vs neck, added deus-ex framing, lost 따위 contempt).

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 누군가는 쓰러지고, 누군가는 튕겨 냈다. 그러나 쓰러진 자들은 다시 일어나지 못했다.
- **Current:** Some people collapsed. Others were knocked backward.

But the ones who fell did not get up again.
- **Defect:** 튕겨 냈다 is an active deflection of the projectiles, not the victims being knocked backward. The line contrasts those who fall with those who bat the shots away.
- **Correction:** Some collapsed. Others knocked the shots aside. The ones who fell did not get up again.
- **Rationale:** Misreading 튕겨 냈다 as 튕겨 나갔다 reverses who acts and who is hit; only the fallen stay down.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “포위 대형, 펼쳐.”
- **Current:** “Encircling formation. Spread out.”
- **Defect:** 펼쳐 here deploys the encircling formation (they then close in). Spread out is the chapter’s wording for 산개/거리 벌려 and reverses the tactic.
- **Correction:** “Encircling formation. Form up.”
- **Rationale:** Next sentence has the squad tightening from all sides; this is not a dispersal order.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 나는 태연한 척 대답했다.
- **Current:** I answered as calmly as I could.
- **Defect:** 척 is feigned composure, not effortful genuine calm.
- **Correction:** I answered, pretending to be calm.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is bluffing the Head Elder; dropping the pretence changes characterization.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “할배, 고추는 서요?”
- **Current:** “Old man, does it still get up?”
- **Defect:** 고추 is a crude chili/pepper euphemism; the English drops the image and makes the sexual meaning more direct.
- **Correction:** “Gramps, does your pepper still stand?”
- **Rationale:** The Sword Energy then standing 우뚝 is the punchline on 서요; keep the euphemism.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 나는 공포에 질린 혁무진의 목덜미를 잡아챘다.
- **Current:** I grabbed Hyuk’s terrified neck.
- **Defect:** 목덜미 is the nape/scruff; neck can read as the throat. 공포에 질린 modifies Hyuk, not the neck.
- **Correction:** I snatched terrified Hyuk by the scruff of the neck.
- **Rationale:** He yanks Hyuk out of the cut so he can run, not seizes his throat.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 너 따위가 날 막을 수 있겠냐. 그런 뜻이겠지.
- **Current:** You think you can stop me?
- **Defect:** 따위 (the likes of you) is the Head Elder’s contempt; the gloss is a plain challenge.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.78
- **Source:** “허허, 그놈 참. 어린 녀석이 혀가 짧구나.”
- **Current:** “Heh heh. What a boy. Your tongue is too short.”
- **Defect:** 혀가 짧구나 is a register jab at a mouthy junior, not a literal short tongue; the calque is opaque.
- **Correction:** “Heh heh. What a brat. You’ve got a mouth on you.”
- **Rationale:** Natural English should carry impertinence, not a body measurement, unless a preserved pun requires the literal.
