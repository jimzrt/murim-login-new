# Review

Several semantic and register defects: a profanity/insult is sanitized, 입맛 다시다 is rendered as the wrong gesture, and 방언 is mistranslated as “dialect.” Glossary Equipment is twice called an outfit, and the boss’s 대족장 title is flattened.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 이게 누굴 병신으로 보나.
- **Current:** Who does he think he’s talking to?
- **Defect:** Softens and recasts a direct 병신 insult as a generic “who are you talking to” complaint, losing both the idiot/moron meaning and the profanity.
- **Correction:** Does he take me for an idiot?
- **Rationale:** 병신 names the addressee a fool; the line is Taekyung’s offended reaction to being walked through Friday→Saturday, not a question about who is being addressed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 입맛을 다시는 나를 최 팀장이 특유의 묘한 눈빛으로 바라본다.
- **Current:** Team Leader Choi looked at me with that peculiar expression of his as I clicked my tongue.
- **Defect:** Wrong physical action: 입맛을 다시다 is smacking/licking the lips over a missed treat, not clicking the tongue.
- **Correction:** Team Leader Choi looked at me with that peculiar expression of his as I smacked my lips.
- **Rationale:** He has just learned weekend raids are off and is tasting the lost overtime; 혀를 차다 would be a tongue-click of disapproval, which the source does not use.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 나도 모르게 방언이 터져 나온다.
- **Current:** A dialect burst out of me before I knew it.
- **Defect:** 방언 here is an involuntary torrent of speech (glossolalia/rant), not a regional dialect; the English fact is wrong.
- **Correction:** I burst into tongues before I knew it.
- **Rationale:** The line follows a frantic Shin Saimdang recitation and earlier church-deacon comedy; “dialect” names a language variety the source is not claiming.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.78
- **Source:** 제가 오늘 한정판을 입고 와서. / 한정판 장비에 피 튈까 봐 뒤에서 팔짱만 끼고 있는 인간
- **Current:** I wore a limited-edition outfit today. / The man standing behind me with his arms folded so blood wouldn’t splatter on his limited-edition outfit
- **Defect:** Second beat is explicitly 장비 (ledger: Equipment); both lines are localized as clothing “outfit.”
- **Correction:** I wore a limited-edition piece today. / The man standing behind me with his arms folded so blood wouldn’t splatter on his limited-edition Equipment
- **Rationale:** Binding Equipment should be kept on the 장비 reprise; “outfit” makes the later hide/blood gag read as fashion rather than gear.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.72
