# Review

Several major fidelity issues: Gwak’s death-claim is softened into motive, Taekyung’s crude fear idiom is recast as nerves, Hyuk’s shocked beats are flattened or dropped, and the logout catch is rewritten as a grab. Quest-window hierarchy and a few local quantities/register points also slip.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 배신한 적도, 죽을 일도 없다. 네놈들 따위한테는 더더욱.
- **Current:** I have never betrayed anyone, and there’s no reason for me to die. Especially not at the hands of trash like you.
- **Defect:** 죽을 일도 없다 is a claim that they will not die, not that they lack a reason to die; the English turns swagger into justification.
- **Correction:** There has been no betrayal, and we aren’t going to die. Least of all to trash like you.
- **Rationale:** Gwak is rejecting Hyuk’s threat, not arguing that death would be unwarranted.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 똥줄이 활활 탄다. 내가 위축될수록 곽준은 기세등등해졌다.
- **Current:** My nerves were burning. The more intimidated I became, the more triumphant Gwak Jun looked.
- **Defect:** 똥줄이 활활 탄다 is crude comic terror (scared shitless), not burning nerves, which can read as anger.
- **Correction:** I was scared shitless. The more I shrank back, the more triumphant Gwak Jun looked.
- **Rationale:** Narrator register and the intimidation beat both depend on bodily fear, not taut nerves.
