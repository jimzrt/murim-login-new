# Review

Major fidelity misses: Han Yeop’s ‘far stronger than Jopil’ correction is hedged; Hyuk’s 더 is rendered as ‘too’; 가관/기특/동참 shift humor, attitude, and agency; NPC quantity and the life-gamble are altered; 무림인 drops Murim; garden dash-speech is recast as ordinary dialogue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 강해질 겁니다. 조필만큼. 아니, 조필보다 훨씬.
- **Current:** I’ll become stronger. As strong as Jopil. No—as strong as or stronger than Jopil.
- **Defect:** The self-correction 훨씬 (‘far more’) is reduced to a hedge, ‘as strong as or stronger.’
- **Correction:** I’ll become stronger. As strong as Jopil. No—far stronger than Jopil.
- **Rationale:** 한엽 first matches Jopil, then raises the target well beyond him; the draft’s ‘or stronger’ undoes that escalation.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 내가 더 강해질 거다.
- **Current:** I’ll become stronger too.
- **Defect:** 더 marks a comparative ‘even more,’ not additive ‘also/too.’
- **Correction:** I’ll become even stronger.
- **Rationale:** After 한엽’s vow, 혁무진 answers with 더, a competitive raise, not 나도.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 소심하던 이류 무사도 어느새 그 흐름에 동참했다.
- **Current:** Even the timid Second Rate martial artist had been swept up in the current.
- **Defect:** 동참했다 is active joining; ‘swept up’ makes 한엽 a passive object of the current.
- **Correction:** Even the timid Second Rate martial artist had, before anyone noticed, joined that current.
- **Rationale:** 어느새 동참했다 is his participation in the changed flow, matching the ‘this guy changed too’ beat.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 무슨 얘길 하나 가만히 듣고 있었는데 아주 가관이다, 가관.
- **Current:** I’d stood there listening to them for a while, and it had been absolutely unbearable.
- **Defect:** 가관이다 is a derisive ‘what a spectacle,’ not endurance-breaking ‘unbearable.’
- **Correction:** I’d stood there quietly listening to see what they were saying, and it was quite a scene—a real spectacle.
- **Rationale:** The gag is secondhand cringe at the earnest talk, not that he could not bear to hear it.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그래도 좀 기특하긴 하네.
- **Current:** Still, I have to admit it’s kind of touching.
- **Defect:** 기특하다 is patronizing approval (‘good for them’), not sentimental ‘touching.’
- **Correction:** Still, I have to admit it’s kind of admirable.
- **Rationale:** 진태경’s beat is grudging pride in subordinates, then 정나미; ‘touching’ shifts him into softness the source withholds until the next lines.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 고작 게임 속 NPC를 위해서 목숨을 건 도박을 하다니.
- **Current:** I had risked my life for a couple of NPCs in a game.
- **Defect:** NPC is singular (공야청) with 고작; 목숨을 건 도박 is a life-stakes gamble, and ‘a couple’ invents a count.
- **Correction:** Gambling my life for a mere NPC in a game.
- **Rationale:** The self-reproach is the insane bet for one game NPC; plurality and loss of 도박 change the causal self-explanation.

## F07 — Major
