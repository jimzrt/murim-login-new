# Review

Signal causality is reversed at the fire arrow, and the Head Elder’s Number One Under Heaven line can be read as the opposite claim. Several fight and terminology facts are also off (back-room callback, snapped wrist, stab sounds, 이소군, 신공).

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 태원진가의 고수들이 땅을 박차고 날아올랐다. 그것을 신호로 등 뒤에서 불화살 하나가 높게 솟구쳤다.
- **Current:** The Jin Family’s masters kicked off the ground and leaped into the air. As if signaling their charge, a single fire arrow rose high behind them.
- **Defect:** The leap is the signal for the fire arrow; the draft makes the arrow signal the charge and adds ‘as if.’
- **Correction:** The Jin Family’s masters kicked off the ground and leaped into the air. Taking that as the signal, a single fire arrow rose high behind them.
- **Rationale:** 그것을 신호로 points back to the masters launching, not the arrow announcing their charge.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 천하제일이 아니라면 변방의 무부(武夫)에 불과하지. 나도, 자네도 그 정도 그릇은 아니야.
- **Current:** If you're not Number One Under Heaven, you're nothing more than a martial brute from the frontier. Neither you nor I are cut out to be that.
- **Defect:** ‘That’ most naturally refers to ‘martial brute,’ reversing the Head Elder’s claim that neither man has the capacity to be Number One Under Heaven.
- **Correction:** If you're not Number One Under Heaven, you're nothing more than a martial brute from the frontier. Neither you nor I have the capacity to be that.
- **Rationale:** 그 정도 그릇 names the stature of 천하제일, not the frontier brute.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** “위명은 무슨. 뒷방 늙은이에 불과하네.” “그럼 계속 뒷방에 있지, 뭣 하러 나온 거요?”
- **Current:** “Reputation? I'm nothing more than an old man put out to pasture.” “Then why leave your back room?”
- **Defect:** 뒷방 is dropped in the first line, so Cheonbaek’s ‘back room’ retort no longer answers the same image.
- **Correction:** “Reputation? I'm nothing more than an old man in the back room.” “Then why leave your back room?”
- **Rationale:** The exchange is a 뒷방 callback; ‘put out to pasture’ breaks it.

## F04 — Major
