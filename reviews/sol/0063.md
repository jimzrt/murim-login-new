# Review

Two subject errors reverse who said the contract line to Choi and whose face Hyuk is mirroring; Hyuk’s Shanxi-hegemon boast is dropped, and two source-bound wordplays (개판/말판; 산 자) are flattened.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 문득 무림으로 돌아오기 전, 최 팀장과 했던 대화가 떠올랐다. 다음에 나를 볼 때는 계약서를 고쳐야 할 거라는 말. 그는 허풍으로 받아들였겠지만 나는 사실로 만들었다.
- **Current:** I suddenly remembered my conversation with Team Leader Choi before returning to Murim. He had said that he would have to revise my contract the next time he saw me.

He had taken it as a bluff, but I had made it a fact.
- **Defect:** Speaker reversed: 나를 볼 때 is Taekyung as the one being seen, so Taekyung said the contract would need revising; 그는 is Choi taking that as a bluff.
- **Correction:** I suddenly remembered my conversation with Team Leader Choi before returning to Murim. Next time you see me, you’ll have to revise the contract.

He had probably taken it as a bluff, but I had made it a fact.
- **Rationale:** 나를 볼 때 cannot be Choi talking about seeing Taekyung as “me”; 그는 허풍으로 받아들였겠지만 needs Choi as the one who dismissed Taekyung’s line.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 나는 놈과 비슷한 표정을 짓고 있는 혁무진에게 물었다.
- **Current:** I turned to Hyuk Mujin, who was wearing an expression similar to mine.
- **Defect:** Comparison target changed from 놈 (the newcomer) to the narrator; Hyuk is mirroring the stranger’s round-eyed look, not Taekyung’s.
- **Correction:** I turned to Hyuk Mujin, who was wearing an expression similar to the bastard’s.
- **Rationale:** 짓고 있는 혁무진 is modified by 놈과 비슷한 표정; Taekyung has been deadpan, so “similar to mine” invents a shared look with the viewpoint character.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 멈춰라! 이 몸은 산서성의 패자, 대태원진가의 수문조장이자 산서잠룡의 오른팔 혁무진이다. 순순히 신원과 목적을…….
- **Current:** Stop right there! I am Hyuk Mujin, Captain of the Gatekeepers of the mighty Jin Family of Taiyuan and the right-hand man of the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi! State your identity and purpose, and—
- **Defect:** Drops 산서성의 패자, the first and loudest self-title in Hyuk’s gate speech.
- **Correction:** Stop right there! I am the hegemon of Shanxi, Captain of the Gatekeepers of the Great Jin Family of Taiyuan, and the right-hand man of the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi—Hyuk Mujin! State your identity and purpose, and—
- **Rationale:** 이 몸은 산서성의 패자 is a separate boast before the gate-captain and right-hand claims; omitting it flattens Hyuk’s bombast.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “개판이군.” “말판이죠. 마적 떼들 아닙니까.”
- **Current:** “What a fucking mess.”

“A horse mess, more like. They’re mounted bandits, aren’t they?”
- **Defect:** 개판/말판 is a dog/horse pun; “mess” has no 개 for 말판 to flip.
- **Correction:** “What a dog’s mess.”

“A horse’s mess, more like. They’re mounted bandits, aren’t they?”
- **Rationale:** 말판 only works as a retort to 개판 because 마적 are horse bandits; the draft keeps the second half and loses the first.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 산서잠룡의 산 자만 들어도 내상을 입은 것처럼 속이 울렁거리고 머리가 아파 왔다.
