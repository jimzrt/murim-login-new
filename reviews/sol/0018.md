# Review

Several character and register errors: Head Elder’s gym-grandpa aside can attach to the White Tiger Hall Leader; Wikyung’s unusual anger, Taekyung’s sneaking, 모함, and 좆 됐네 are weakened; Wikyung is given a smile the source does not state.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 표정만 보면 밥 먹었냐 물어보는 헬스장 몸짱 할아버진데, 말하는 내용은 살벌하기 그지없다.
- **Current:** Going by his expression, he looked like a muscular old man at a gym asking whether you’d eaten. But the content of his words was vicious beyond belief.
- **Defect:** After the White Tiger Hall Leader’s line, “his” attaches to the wrong speaker. The gym-grandpa face and vicious speech are the Head Elder’s.
- **Correction:** Going by the Head Elder’s expression, he looked like a muscular old man at a gym asking whether you’d eaten. But the content of his words was vicious beyond belief.
- **Rationale:** 백호당주 only says “노, 노야.” The 몸짱 할아버지 simile and 살벌한 내용 describe the Head Elder. Misassigned identity is a scene-fact error.

## F02 — Major

- **Defect:** Turns a man who is not usually like this into someone who is already frightening “as usual.”

- **Rationale:** 안 그러던 사람 marks Wikyung’s anger as uncharacteristic, not an intensification of a usual aura.
