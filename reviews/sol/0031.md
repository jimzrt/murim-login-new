# Review

Rank, kinship-characterization, and several action/advice beats are altered; D-rank becomes E-rank, 애처가 becomes henpecked, and the Team Leader’s indifferent gaze and “live his share” counsel are softened or recast.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** D급만 되어도 이렇게 살지는 않을 텐데, 우리 같은 F급들은 별수 없다.
- **Current:** Even an E-rank Hunter probably wouldn’t have to live like this, but people like us at F-rank had no other choice.
- **Defect:** The cited Hunter rank is D, not E. That is a scene fact, and it also collides with the Team Leader later being an E-rank who is still broke.
- **Correction:** Even a D-rank probably wouldn’t have to live like this, but F-ranks like us had no other choice.
- **Rationale:** Source says D급만 되어도. The Team Leader is an E-rank with three kids and money trouble; D is the rank that would change that life.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 말은 저렇게 해도 소문난 애처가에 좋은 아빠다.
- **Current:** Despite saying things like that, he was a famous henpecked husband and a good father.
- **Defect:** 애처가 is a devoted, wife-loving husband. Henpecked is 공처가 and reverses the characterization under the swamp talk.
- **Correction:** Despite saying things like that, he was a famous devoted husband and a good father.
- **Rationale:** The joke is that he calls marriage a swamp while being known for doting on his wife, then looking fondly at family photos.
