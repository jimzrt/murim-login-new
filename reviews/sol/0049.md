# Review

Critical subject reversal in Choi’s explanation of the B-rank terms (Guild trusts him, not “I trust you”). Major: “The System picked it” replaces a self-correction about the System’s quality; System/item UI is not in required blockquote panels and keeps square brackets.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “저를 믿으니까요.” / “네?” / “제 촉을 믿고, 사람 보는 눈을 믿습니다. 그래서 진태경 씨를 꼭 잡고 싶어요.”
- **Current:** “Because I trust you.” / “What?” / “I trust my instincts, and I trust my eye for people. That’s why I want to hold on to Jin Taekyung.”
- **Defect:** 저를 믿으니까요 is others trusting Choi, not Choi trusting Taekyung. The omitted subject of 제 촉을 믿고 is the same: they trust his instinct and eye for people.
- **Correction:** “Because they trust me.” / “What?” / “They trust my instincts, and they trust my eye for people. That’s why I want to hold on to Jin Taekyung.”
- **Rationale:** 저 is Choi. 믿으니까요 takes 저 as object. “I trust you” reverses who is trusted and why the Guild can offer B-rank terms.
