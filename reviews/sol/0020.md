# Review

Mostly faithful, but the departure force is inflated from ‘about two hundred,’ 주범 is recast as a mastermind, 초일류 is flattened to First Rate, and the gate seniors’ insult is softened.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 철저히 무장한 이백여 명의 무리. / 그날 밤, 이백여 명의 무사가 항산검문을 빠져나왔다.
- **Current:** A group of more than two hundred fully armed men stood outside. / That night, more than two hundred martial artists left the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- **Defect:** 이백여 명 is about two hundred, not more than two hundred. The departing force is counted high twice.
- **Correction:** A group of some two hundred fully armed men stood outside. / That night, some two hundred martial artists left the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- **Rationale:** 여 marks an approximate count. Raising it to ‘more than two hundred’ changes the size of the revenge party, a scene quantity.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 중요한 하나가 빠졌네요. 내가 결백하다면 이소군을 독살한 주범은 누구인가?
- **Current:** You’re overlooking one important thing. If I’m innocent, then who was the mastermind behind Lee Seogeun’s poisoning?
- **Defect:** 주범 is the principal culprit who poisoned Lee Seogeun, not a behind-the-scenes mastermind.
- **Correction:** You’re overlooking one important thing. If I’m innocent, then who actually poisoned Lee Seogeun?
- **Rationale:** The missing puzzle piece is the perpetrator of the poisoning. Mastermind implies an orchestrator the Korean does not name. The next lines still say 범인, ‘culprit.’

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** 고수야. 틀림없어. 최소 초일류, 어쩌면 절정 고수일지도 모르지.
- **Current:** He’s a master. No doubt about it. At least First Rate, maybe even Peak.
- **Defect:** 초일류 is a higher rung than 일류 (First Rate). The guards’ floor is Super First Rate, not ordinary First Rate.
- **Correction:** He’s a master. No doubt about it. At least Super First Rate, maybe even Peak.
- **Rationale:** The packet binds 일류 to First Rate and 절정 to Peak. 최소 초일류 sets the estimate above First Rate on the way to Peak.

## F04 — Major

## F05 — Minor

- **Source:** 그녀는 단 한 번도 이소군과 연관된 이야기를 꺼내지 않았다. 깐깐한 사업가처럼 협의를 조정하고 당연하다는 듯 동맹을 맺었다.
- **Current:** Now that I thought about it, she had never once mentioned Lee Seogeun. She had adjusted the negotiations like a shrewd businesswoman, then formed an alliance as if it were the most natural thing in the world.
- **Defect:** 깐깐한 is strict, exacting, fastidious. Shrewd adds cunning the source does not use here.

- **Rationale:** This is Taekyung’s read of how she handled the talks: picky and businesslike, not necessarily shrewd.
