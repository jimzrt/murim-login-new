# Review

Three meaning-changing defects: a reversed guilt clause, twice-inflated 반 시진, and an invented Squad Leader address on Socheon’s line.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 피부 위로 소름이 돋을 정도로 생생했다. 게임이라고는 생각할 수 없을 정도로.

고작 NPC 하나 버린 걸로 양심의 가책을 느낄 정도로.
- **Current:** They felt so vivid that I got goose bumps across my skin.

Too vivid to believe this was a game.

Too vivid for me to feel guilty over abandoning a single NPC.
- **Defect:** The second 정도로 clause is a result (vivid enough that he feels guilt), not a negated result. “Too vivid for me to feel guilty” reverses the source.
- **Correction:** They felt so vivid that I got goose bumps across my skin. Too vivid to believe this was a game. Vivid enough that I felt guilty over abandoning a single NPC.
- **Rationale:** Both fragments modify 생생했다: 생각할 수 없을 정도로 (cannot think of it as a game) and 양심의 가책을 느낄 정도로 (to the point of feeling pangs of conscience). English “too X for Y” blocks Y; the Korean asserts Y.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 한 시진, 아니 반 시진만 쉬면서 방법을 시도해 보면 될 겁니다.
- **Current:** If we rest for one shichen—no, more than half a shichen—and try something, we should have enough time.
- **Defect:** 아니 반 시진만 scales the request down to only half a shichen. “More than half a shichen” changes the quantity and the bargaining direction.
- **Correction:** If we rest for one shichen—no, just half a shichen—and try the method, that should be enough.
- **Rationale:** 만 marks limitation (“only half”). He first says one shichen, then reduces it; the draft’s “more than” does not match that correction.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 한 식경? 반 시진?
- **Current:** A sikyeong? More than half a shichen?
- **Defect:** The narrator guesses between a sikyeong and half a shichen. “More than” is not in the source.
- **Correction:** A sikyeong? Half a shichen?
- **Rationale:** 반 시진 is “half a shichen,” parallel to 한 식경 as an uncertain estimate of elapsed time, not a comparative “more than.”

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** “후욱. 괜찮, 괜찮습니다.”
- **Current:** “Huff. I’m fine. I’m fine, Squad Leader.”
- **Defect:** Squad Leader is not spoken. Socheon is not a squad member; the added title invents a hierarchy of address.
- **Correction:** “Huff. I’m fine. I’m fine.”
- **Rationale:** The line is only 괜찮, 괜찮습니다. 조장님 appears later from Hyuk Mujin, not from Socheon.
