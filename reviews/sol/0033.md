# Review

One major subject split in Seogwang’s stammer (I vs We); otherwise localized omissions, a wrong astonishment gesture, and a glossary miss on 본문.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “정보를, 정보를 입수했습니다.”
- **Current:** “I received intelligence. We received intelligence.”
- **Defect:** The repeated 정보를 is a stammer with one omitted subject; the draft invents a shift from I to We.
- **Correction:** “Intelligence—I obtained intelligence.”
- **Rationale:** Nothing in the line changes the actor. Seogwang is forcing out the same report twice, not correcting I to We.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** “태원진가의 추격대에 쫓기고 있으니 도와 달라고 했겠지.”
- **Current:** “He must have said he was being pursued by the Jin Family of Taiyuan and asked you to help him.”
- **Defect:** 추격대 is omitted; the supposed plea is pursuit by a chase unit, not the family in general.
- **Correction:** “He must have said he was being pursued by the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s pursuit party and asked you to help him.”
- **Rationale:** 이천백 specifies 추격대 as the content of the false request for aid.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 그런 놈들을 고용했다가는 차후에도 본문의 평판이…….
- **Current:** If we hire people like that, the sect’s reputation will suffer afterward…
- **Defect:** 본문 is the in-group term our/this sect, not a detached the sect.
- **Correction:** If we hire people like that, our sect’s reputation will suffer afterward…
- **Rationale:** Binding glossary: 본문 → our sect / this sect. The speaker is a Mount Heng senior referring to their own sect.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 의원이 혀를 내둘렀다.
- **Current:** The physician clicked his tongue in amazement.
- **Defect:** 혀를 내두르다 is astonishment, not the tongue-click of 혀를 차다.
- **Correction:** The physician was astounded.
- **Rationale:** Clicking the tongue is a different pragmatic cue, usually disapproval; the source is marveling at the recovery.

## F05 — Minor

- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence already renders 은원 as debts and grudges; the 고리 is that same bond, not vengeance only.
