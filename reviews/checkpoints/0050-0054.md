# Review

The packet preserves the major hooks, established terminology, character voices, System formatting, and spoiler boundaries. Actionable issues are two numerical contradictions, two stale or inaccurate continuity-state claims, recurring ellipsis-format drift, one Markdown dialogue-layout problem, and several localized prose defects.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 51, cash-payment calculation
- **Current:** One bundle was a hundred Shin Saimdang bills. Six of those, so…

“Th-three hundred million!”
- **Defect:** Six bundles of one hundred 50,000-won bills total 30 million won, not 300 million won. This also conflicts with the settlement sheet's 30-million-won earnings plus 270-million-won bonus.
- **Correction:** Make the physical bill count total 300 million won—for example, change each bundle to one thousand bills—while retaining the established 300-million-won payment and 270-million-won bonus.
- **Rationale:** The current arithmetic creates an explicit internal contradiction at the payoff of the scene.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapters 52 and 54, C-rank Hunter salary comparisons
- **Current:** The average annual salary of a C-rank Hunter was five hundred million won.

The average annual salary of a C-rank Hunter was 200 million won, including fixed pay and raid pay.
- **Defect:** The same narrator gives incompatible average annual salaries for C-rank Hunters only days apart, with no explanation for the change.
- **Correction:** Reconcile the two figures to the already accepted value, preserving any intended distinction between fixed salary and total annual earnings if one exists.
- **Rationale:** The contradiction changes the perceived scale of Taekyung's income and Choi's contract offer.
