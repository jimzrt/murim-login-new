# Review

The reading copies preserve the checkpoint’s major plot hooks and tone, but the durable state contains material progression, squad-size, and alliance-term contradictions. Two passages also lose required dialogue or thought formatting, and the Chapter 24 System panel shortens an established quest title.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity; Chapters 21 and 24 progression
- **Current:** Taekyung is Lv. 17; his Jin Family’s Cultivation, Spear, and Manoeuvre Techniques are at the Fourth Stage, and Third-Stage Qi Sense detects targets through Lv. 50.
- **Defect:** The active state is stale and internally contradicts the reading copies. Chapter 21 displays the Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques at the Fifth Stage, while Chapter 24 shows at least three level-ups from Level 17.
- **Correction:** Update the technique stages to Fifth Stage. Replace the exact Level 17 and Main Quest 17/30 claims with the latest confirmed progression; because the final Chapter 24 level-up count is truncated, record that Taekyung reached at least Level 20 unless an accepted source-fidelity decision establishes the exact endpoint.
- **Rationale:** The durable state is the handoff for later chapters; stale progression can produce incorrect System windows, combat scaling, and Main Quest status.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary and durable state, reconnaissance-squad descriptions
- **Current:** Taekyung becomes leader of White Tiger Hall’s ten-person reconnaissance squad
- **Defect:** This treats the whole squad as ten people, but Chapter 22 introduces ten members waiting under Taekyung, and Chapter 23 explicitly has eleven horses. The deployed group is eleven people including Taekyung.
