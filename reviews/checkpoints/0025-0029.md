# Review

The packet is broadly consistent in voice and continuity, but it contains one Quest-title drift, one combat-blocking action contradiction, and one contradictory durable-state entry.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 25, first System completion panel
- **Current:** You completed the **Survivor** Quest!
- **Defect:** The System panel shortens the established Quest title and conflicts with the surrounding narration, summary, and translation decision naming it **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**.
- **Correction:** Replace **Survivor** with **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**.
- **Rationale:** The checkpoint explicitly requires this Quest to remain distinct from later System events; changing its title creates terminology drift at the completion notice.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 29, immediately before Sky-Piercing Strike
- **Current:** And I was sure I could finish everything before he drew the sword from his back and swung it.
- **Defect:** The sword is not on Jopil’s back. Chapter 28 places its case at his left hip, and moments earlier in Chapter 29 he drives the drawn sword into the ground.
- **Correction:** Describe Jopil retrieving the sword from the ground, preserving Taekyung’s mistaken confidence that he can strike first.
- **Rationale:** The stated weapon position contradicts both the established sword placement and the immediately preceding combat action, obscuring how Jopil counters Sky-Piercing Strike.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity martial-technique entry
- **Current:** Taekyung's Jin Family's Cultivation, Spear, and Manoeuvre Techniques are at the Fourth Stage; his Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques reached the Fifth Stage after training, and Third-Stage Qi Sense detects targets through Lv. 50.
- **Defect:** The same sentence records the Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques as both Fourth Stage and Fifth Stage.
- **Correction:** State that the Cultivation Technique remains at the Fourth Stage while the Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques are at the Fifth Stage.
- **Rationale:** Durable state must provide one unambiguous current value for each technique; the present wording can propagate contradictory progression into later chapters.
