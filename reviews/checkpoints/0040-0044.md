# Review

The reading copies preserve the packet’s voice and unresolved hooks overall, but the checkpoint summary and durable state contain two substantive continuity errors. Several smaller terminology and formatting inconsistencies are also actionable.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot, Chapter 40
- **Current:** After speaking with his sister Hayeon and thinking of his mother, he accepts that he has returned home and decides to live with his family.
- **Defect:** The summary implies that Taekyung decides to move in or reside with his family, but the reading copy has him return to his room at Hope Goshiwon. His decision is to live in reality because his family is there.
- **Correction:** Change the final clause to say that he decides to live in reality for or alongside his family, without implying shared residence.
- **Rationale:** The current wording contradicts Taekyung’s physical living situation and could cause later continuity notes to place him in the family home prematurely.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** durable state, active_continuity item 7
- **Current:** Taekyung's E-rank Gate party consists of Team Leader Choi, Im, two other veteran E-rank Hunters, and Taekyung as porter.
- **Defect:** The party count drops one veteran Hunter. Chapters 41 and 44 establish Im plus three other veteran E-rank Hunters, along with Team Leader Choi and Taekyung.
- **Correction:** Replace “two other veteran E-rank Hunters” with “three other veteran E-rank Hunters.”
- **Rationale:** Chapter 41 introduces three men besides Im, and Chapter 44 accounts for Im, the second tank, and two additional combatants before the party is incapacitated.

## C04 — Minor
