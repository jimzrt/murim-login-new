# Review

The reading copies retain the intended voice and hooks, but contain one rank-status drift, an inconsistent contact nickname, repeated footnotes, and one awkward phrase. The active state also incorrectly leaves Choi’s response to Taekyung’s secrecy request unresolved.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 48 rooftop message and Chapter 49 opening text message
- **Current:** “Luxury Nutjob? Who’s that?” / “The sender was Luxury Junkie—or rather, Team Leader Choi.”
- **Defect:** The same saved contact name changes between consecutive chapters without explanation.
- **Correction:** Choose the established nickname and use it consistently in both chapters.
- **Rationale:** An unexplained contact-name change reads as terminology drift and may make readers think these are different labels or contacts.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 49, after Taekyung signs the provisional contract
- **Current:** When we shook hands firmly, it sank in that I’d taken my first step as a C-rank Hunter.
- **Defect:** The narration treats Taekyung as an established C-rank Hunter even though his formal reassessment registration remains pending.
- **Correction:** Describe him as C-rank-level or make the sentence explicitly prospective until formal registration is complete.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 48 explicitly says he is technically not yet C-rank, and the checkpoint terminology decision requires “C-rank-level” during this provisional period.
