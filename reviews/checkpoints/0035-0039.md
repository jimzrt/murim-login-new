# Review

The reading copies largely preserve Taekyung’s voice, established terminology, the assassination reveal, and the return-to-reality cliffhanger. Actionable issues remain in the durable state, participant counts, item continuity, and System-panel formatting.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity entry 3
- **Current:** Logout is the Main Quest reward for reaching First Rate, Lv. 30, and 500 Fame. Taekyung is Lv. 32, has fifteen years of internal energy, and is First Rate; only the Fame 500 requirement remains incomplete, with his total at 497/500.
- **Defect:** This presents obsolete Chapter 37 status as active state, contradicting the later entries establishing that Taekyung reached Lv. 33 and 547/500 Fame, completed the Logout conditions, and returned to reality.
- **Correction:** Replace this entry with Taekyung’s Chapter 39 status: Lv. 33, First Rate, 547 Fame, and successfully logged out; retain the requirements only as historical Quest information.
- **Rationale:** Active continuity must describe the latest completed chapter rather than an intermediate state.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint Plot and Durable state, Honju assassin descriptions
- **Current:** At Honju, Gwak Jun, the supposedly allied Level 40 leader of the Three Paths Sect, reveals that he and twenty black-clad fighters are First Rate assassins.
- **Defect:** This makes twenty-one assassins, while Chapter 38’s Quest objective and completion count establish twenty total assassins, including Gwak Jun.
- **Correction:** Describe the group as Gwak Jun and nineteen other assassins, or simply as twenty assassins led by Gwak Jun. Apply the same correction to the active-continuity entry saying “Gwak Jun and twenty supposed Three Paths Sect martial artists.”
- **Rationale:** The Quest advances to 20/20 only when Gwak dies, so he is included in the total of twenty.

- **Current:** I smiled at the eight reconnaissance-squad members. Then a thought suddenly struck me.

*No, wait.*

Eight? Had I counted wrong?

I started counting again from the end, one person at a time. Not counting me, there were nine.
