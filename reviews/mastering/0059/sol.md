# Chapter 59

Shh-shh-shhk!

The fight between Jin Wikyung, Wipeng, and the three Elders raged like a storm.

Sword strikes too fast and fierce for an ordinary martial artist to follow collided from every direction.

Krshhk.

*Blocked.*

The instant he realized it, Wipeng twisted aside. The Second Elder’s thrusting sword grazed his hair as it passed.

The Second and Third Elders pressed him as though they were one.

“You bastards!”

Jin Wikyung charged with a roar, only for the First Elder to cut him off.

“Where are you rushing off to?”

Sword Energy shot upward, then dropped toward the crown of Jin Wikyung’s head.

One strike could split him in two. Jin Wikyung hastily raised his sword to block. Pale Sword Energy wreathed his blade as well.

Boom!

Dust billowed from the thunderous impact. A figure staggered backward within the cloud. Jin Wikyung emerged pale-faced and spat out a wad of bloody phlegm.

*What the hell kind of internal energy…*

He had trained in exceptional martial arts and taken elixirs since childhood, but his opponent was simply too formidable. The First Elder was a vile old monster who had lived more than sixty years while concealing his dark ambitions.

Alongside the Head Elder, he was a living witness to the Great Faction War, having fought his way through it with his own two hands.

“You damned old bastard.”

The First Elder chuckled.

“Lesser Family Head, mind your dignity.”

But the First Elder was not nearly as comfortable as he appeared.

His trembling blade and throbbing wrist were proof enough.

*To think they were this strong.*

Numbers mattered even in a street brawl. How much more so when masters fought? Yet Jin Wikyung and Wipeng were even stronger than he had expected.

Or perhaps an old man’s pride had led him to overestimate himself.

*How cruel. Truly cruel.*

Sixty years. He had accumulated profound internal energy, but even that could not halt the aging of his body.

*If only I were ten years younger.*

With a bitter, self-mocking smile, the First Elder advanced on Jin Wikyung.

The swords of his sworn younger brothers, the Second and Third Elders, grew fiercer still.

Shh-shh-shhk!

“Urgh!”

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng gradually began to give ground. Having just fought the Peak masters of the Mount Heng Sword Sect only made matters worse.

Their vision swam. Minor wounds multiplied across their bodies. Their limbs no longer moved as they wished.

*We can’t keep this up.*

Jin Wikyung’s expression darkened.

Fwoooosh!

The First Elder’s Sword Energy, which had been bearing down on him without pause, abruptly changed course. The next instant, a lightning-fast strike split its target.

Shhk.

Fwaaah!

A man staggered amid a spray of blood. The sword he had aimed at the First Elder’s side had shattered beyond recognition, and blood poured from his chest like a waterfall.

The First Elder saw his face and clicked his tongue.

“Discipline Hall Master, you foolish friend. Did you want to die that badly?”

“F-First Elder…”

The Discipline Hall Master’s voice seemed ready to give out at any moment. Yet he neither fell nor stopped speaking.

“You have… violated the family’s laws.”

“Heh heh. And?”

“As Discipline Hall Master of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, I command you. The guilty shall abolish their own martial arts and enter the Cave of Repentance…”

Shhk.

The First Elder answered with his sword. The Discipline Hall Master’s character was straight as bamboo, but his martial arts were not particularly advanced. He could neither see nor evade the stroke that pierced his throat.

Fire blazed in Jin Wikyung’s eyes.

“You bastard!”

Jin Wikyung was not the only one enraged. The Discipline Hall Master’s death set the hearts of the Jin Family’s senior members ablaze.

“Discipline Hall Master!”

Before they were martial artists, they were human.

They had lived long enough to build much and gain much they needed to protect. That was why they had been afraid—afraid of a Peak master’s might, and of dying like dogs.

But the Discipline Hall Master’s dignified end brought emotions they had briefly forgotten boiling back to the surface.

Shame and anger.

“Strike down those traitors!”

“Show them the spirit of the Jin Family of Taiyuan!”

Those most ashamed of themselves were the first to charge. They were senior members of the so-called Elders’ faction.

They could not forgive themselves for aiding the traitors, nor could they forgive the Elders who had betrayed them and their family.

“Fools.”

Shhk. Shhk. Shhk.

Each flash of the three Elders’ swords extinguished another life.

The difference in strength was obvious. Even so, the Elders’ wrinkled faces stiffened.

*These bastards…!*

Dozens of First Rate masters, prepared to take their enemies with them, surged in from every direction without fear of death.

It was not their martial arts that rattled the Elders, but their momentum.

And amid that distraction, two men seized the opening.

Shhk.

“Gah!”

A scream tore from the Third Elder’s mouth. Wipeng’s Sword Energy had erupted without warning and sliced into his side.

“Little brother!”

The Second Elder faltered at the cry of the sworn brother who had been by his side for decades. For the briefest instant, his attention shifted elsewhere.

It cost him dearly.

Three swords thrust out at once, grazing him from head to toe. As the Second Elder scrambled backward, a bolt of lightning shot toward him.

Fwoooosh!

The instant he felt something cold burrow into his back, the Second Elder knew it was over.

Thuk!

It split flesh and severed bone. Sword Energy rampaged like a living thing, tearing through his blood vessels and burning them.

How long had it been since he had felt pain like this?

The Second Elder’s vision turned white. A moment later, he could no longer feel any pain at all.

“I, Jin Wikyung, Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, have cut down the Second Elder!”

As Jin Wikyung tore his sword from the Second Elder’s chest and roared, the Third Elder’s head fell not far away. Wipeng, drenched in blood, raised it high.

“The Third Elder’s head is here!”

The survivors took up the cry.

“Wipe out the traitors!”

“The Jin Family of Taiyuan is not the enemy of the Mount Heng Sword Sect! Lower your swords!”

The shouts ringing out across the battlefield invigorated the Jin Family’s martial artists.

With their leadership all but annihilated, Mount Heng’s forces still had no idea what to do. Before long, however, they turned their weapons on the black-clad men.

“What are you doing? Those Jin Family bastards are right in front of us—”

“Enough of that nonsense. The only ones attacking us are those black-clad bastards!”

He was right. The Jin Family’s martial artists followed their commanders’ orders to the letter, allowing Mount Heng’s people to realize that one of their enemies had fallen away.

Now the black-clad men who had appeared out of nowhere were their common enemy.

“Show them the strength of the Mount Heng Sword Sect!”

“I don’t know where you crawled out from, but wipe them all out!”

Once the two forces joined together, the black-clad men began to give ground.

They were elites hardened by brutal training, but the deaths of the two Elders inevitably shook their morale. Blades poured in from every direction, exploiting every opening, and the black-clad men began to fall one after another.

“Aaargh!”

“Do not retreat! Anyone who falls back will die!”

Even amid the chaos, the First Elder silently swung his sword.

Everything within his sight, everything within his reach, was his enemy.

Shhk.

Twenty? Thirty? He had lost count. Like a man possessed, the First Elder cut down everything in his path. Among his victims were men who had once addressed him with respect, as well as youths who looked barely twenty.

*What does age matter in life or death? Those who stand at swordpoint are people of Murim.*

At last, a bear of a man blocked the path of the First Elder, who was drenched in the blood of dozens. His gaze seemed fierce enough to burn everything to ash.

“Why did you do it?”

“Wealth and glory. To seize the Jin Family of Taiyuan and swallow Shanxi whole.”

The answer came without a moment’s hesitation, making everyone tremble with rage.

Everyone but Jin Wikyung. He shook his head.

“That isn’t the answer I wanted.”

“Then you tell me, Lesser Family Head. Why would my brothers, my lord, and I do such a thing?”

“Revenge.”

Jin Wikyung continued in a low voice.

“The grand plan you spoke of wasn’t for wealth and glory, or to become the ruler of a single province. Too many opportunities for that have already passed, and all of you are old. And…”

“Enough.”

“The Head Elder has no descendants. Neither did the Second Elder or the Third Elder. Nor do you.”

At that moment, a livid spark flared in the First Elder’s placid eyes.

It was anger suppressed over long years, a remnant that surfaced for the briefest moment before sinking again.

“Why did you do it?”

Instead of answering, the First Elder raised his sword and pointed it at Jin Wikyung.

No—the tip pointed past Jin Wikyung’s shoulder, toward someone somewhere beyond him.

“Hear it from him yourself.”

In the end, the final key lay in the Head Elder’s hands.

Jin Wikyung took a long stride toward the First Elder.

“I will. After I cut you down.”

“Are you sure you can afford to take your time like this?”

“What do you—”

Jin Wikyung’s frown froze.

*Taekyung!*

His precious youngest brother was holding off the Head Elder.

The instant he remembered the fact that had briefly slipped his mind, his blood seemed to turn cold.

*If I delay any longer, something irreversible will happen.*

Realizing the full situation, Jin Wikyung issued an icy command.

“Wipeng. Take care of the First Elder.”

“I obey.”

“The rest of you, lend him your strength.”

“We follow the Lesser Family Head’s command.”

Wipeng and a dozen or so surviving senior members formed a wide ring around the First Elder.

“Everyone else, follow me and open a path! We’re going after the Head Elder!”

Jin Wikyung was about to move out with several dozen guards when he glanced back at the First Elder. Though his end was approaching, the old man showed no agitation. If anything, he looked relieved.

“First Elder.”

“Do you have something left to say?”

Jin Wikyung tossed out a single sentence.

“By the authority vested in me as Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, I expel you from the family.”

“Heh heh. Heh heh heh!”

Leaving the First Elder’s laughter behind, Jin Wikyung sprinted across the battlefield.

Dozens of black-clad men tried to stop him, but the battle had long since turned against them. Martial artists converged from every direction and hacked them to pieces.

“Open a path!”

“It’s the Lesser Family Head! Kill anyone who stands in his way!”

Only moments passed, but to Jin Wikyung they felt like an eternity.

*Taekyung. Please, please…*

He could not even bring himself to think the word *death*.

How long had he run with his heart pounding in his chest? When he finally reached his destination, his eyes flew wide.

*What in the world…*

The sight before him was utterly shocking.

* * *

Raid.

Until just a few decades ago, the word had been used only in games.

But when the Demon King appeared and the Great Cataclysm began, raids became a symbol of Hunters.

It took countless battles and sacrifices to reach that point. The modern method of raiding was finally established on that foundation.

*Tank, damage dealer, healer.*

The tank blocks. The damage dealer attacks. The healer heals.

It sounded simple, but becoming a licensed Hunter required studying an enormous stack of raid manuals and proving yourself through actual combat.

*Hunter training camp… That really was hell.*

But enduring it was what made me a Hunter. Now, as a seven-year veteran, I could come up with the right positions and response for any situation.

And yet, right now—

“Hey, throw dirt! Keep throwing!”

I realized that everything I’d ever learned was worth fuck-all.

A tank? A healer?

Fuck…

I was the idiot who thought I could run a raid with ten melee damage dealers.
