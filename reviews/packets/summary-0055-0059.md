## Previous block summary

# Chapters 50–54

## Plot

Taekyung enters Choi Minwoo’s exclusive D-rank Gate and clears a colony of about twenty Level 40 swamp Lizardmen alone. Choi reveals the encounter was a test, then releases female-Lizardman pheromones that summon a much stronger horde. Taekyung spends all 100 Remaining Points—30 Strength, 30 Stamina, and 40 Agility—and uses One Flash to kill the Level 52 Lizardman Great Chieftain and dozens of monsters. He and Choi clear three C-rank Gates in one day, earning Taekyung 300 million won, while Choi privately recognizes that Taekyung may be stronger than himself.

Sopung Guild’s Guild Master learns that Kim Sangshik dismissed Taekyung and filed a false report. He expels Kim and orders the resignation of Kim’s son, Kim Sangho, provoking Guild-wide gossip about Taekyung’s reawakening.

Taekyung returns home with his C-rank Hunter license and cash, gives his mother and younger sister Hayeon an edited account of his reawakening, and spends the day treating them to clothes and an expensive meal. Back in the goshiwon, increasingly vivid Murim nightmares destabilize him despite Sleep Mode and qi circulation. His injuries worsen during raids, so Choi orders him home. Taekyung lies to Jinho that Jin Wikyung is his Chinese girlfriend, then concludes that Murim may be another reality and the Ark - 2020 capsule a dimensional Gate.

A dream of the Mount Heng Sword Sect–Jin Family battle convinces Taekyung that Murim’s people are real and that he must return. Choi and Butler Kim investigate him, find no evidence that he is an illegal Awakener or deliberately infiltrated Choi’s orbit, and offer an exceptionally generous contract. Taekyung refuses because he cannot abandon Murim, promises to return the next day, enters the capsule, and accepts the prompt to connect to Murim.

## Continuity

- Taekyung clears the Lizardman Gate without injury, then kills the Level 52 Lizardman Great Chieftain and the summoned horde with One Flash.
- He spends all 100 Remaining Points: 30 Strength, 30 Stamina, and 40 Agility. His equipment remains the First Rate Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set and Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon.
- Taekyung completes three C-rank Gates in one day and receives 300 million won, including a 270-million-won bonus. His seven-day provisional Peace Guild contract requires weekends off.
- Choi Minwoo suspects Taekyung may be stronger than him; Choi’s exact current rank remains unknown. Choi continues observing Taekyung and wants to recruit him.
- Sopung Guild expels Kim Sangshik after confirming his misconduct; Kim Sangho also leaves. Sopung’s recruitment of Taekyung remains unresolved.
- Taekyung now holds a C-rank Hunter license. His family knows only the edited explanation that he reawakened and earned money; Murim, the System, and the capsule remain concealed from them and from Seong Jinho.
- Hayeon is nineteen and still a high-school senior. Taekyung’s mother and Hayeon secretly give him additional cash and side dishes.
- Taekyung suffers recurring Murim nightmares and increasing injuries during raids. Sleep Mode repairs his body but does not resolve the mental disturbance.
- The Mount Heng–Jin Family battle is underway. The Head Elder’s third force is waiting for both sides to weaken; the traitor’s identity, betrayal, and the battle’s outcome remain unresolved.
- Taekyung has reconnected to Murim through the Ark - 2020 capsule. Whether the capsule can transport him safely and reliably, its ultimate purpose, and the consequences of his return remain unresolved.
- Choi’s offered contract includes a 500-million-won signing bonus, 50-million-won monthly salary, seventy-percent settlement split, officetel, sedan, and social insurance. Taekyung rejects it while intending to return from Murim.

### Chapter 55

# Chapter 55

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin carries the unconscious Taekyung toward the Jin Family’s main force, but Taekyung wakes after roughly two hours and learns that the reconnaissance squad sent messengers back to the family. He realizes that logging in or logging out places the other side into a deathlike sleep, then orders the squad to reach the battle before the worst can happen. Taekyung forces the exhausted Hyuk and the squad to run as though they can cover two hours in one.

At Eight Spring Gorge, the Jin Family’s forces have trapped Mount Heng’s larger army in the narrow terrain. The Head Elder remembers his past as a Great Faction War hero and explains to Jin Wikyung that they must wait for Mount Heng’s impatience before striking; he intends to use the hidden cliff archers and have himself, Wikyung, and Wipeng engage the enemy command. The Head Elder’s faction controls the archers, and his true plan remains concealed.

Lee Cheonbaek recognizes that his grief and impatience led Mount Heng into the trap. When the Blood Rain Group tries to retreat from the front, he kills its leader and drives the survivors back toward the battle. He then leads Mount Heng’s three Peak masters and dozens of First Rate masters into the assault on the Jin Family line.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has awakened in Murim after reconnecting through the Ark - 2020 capsule; he knows that logging in or logging out places the other side into a deathlike sleep.
- The reconnaissance squad is rushing toward the Eight Spring Gorge battle, with Taekyung demanding that they cover the remaining two-hour distance in one hour.
- The Jin Family has the terrain advantage at Eight Spring Gorge, while Mount Heng’s force is bottlenecked and suffering heavy casualties; the battle’s outcome remains unresolved.
- The Head Elder controls the cliff archers and plans to strike Mount Heng’s command after its morale breaks, while his betrayal and full purpose remain unresolved.
- Lee Cheonbaek has committed Mount Heng’s three Peak masters and dozens of First Rate masters to the attack after forcing the Blood Rain Group back to the front.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Candy in your ear” and the mock-ASMR footnote as Taekyung’s deliberately absurd wake-up greeting.
- Preserve “Blood Wolf Sword” for Lee Cheonbaek’s established epithet and “Blood Rain Group” for the wandering martial artists’ band.
- Keep “Eight Spring Gorge,” “Peak master,” and “First Rate” consistent with established terminology.
### Chapter 56

# Chapter 56

## Plot

Lee Cheonbaek arrives at Eight Spring Gorge with Mount Heng’s core forces and launches the decisive assault. Jin Wikyung orders the Jin Family’s masters forward, and the hidden cliff archers open fire as the Head Elder, Wikyung, and Wipeng engage Mount Heng’s command.

The Head Elder intercepts Lee Cheonbaek and reveals that he is a master far beyond the Blood Wolf Sword. After roughly three hundred exchanges, he wounds Lee’s forearm, snaps his wrist, cuts his knee, and tears through his body with Sword Energy. Lee offers his life and Mount Heng’s surrender, but the Head Elder demands total annihilation. He confirms through Sound Transmission that “they” killed Lee Seogeun, then prepares to finish Lee.

Before the Head Elder can strike, Jin Taekyung arrives and splits a spear flying at the elder’s back. The battle and the Head Elder’s betrayal remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Mount Heng–Jin Family battle at Eight Spring Gorge is still in progress.
- Lee Cheonbaek is gravely wounded and defeated by the Head Elder but survives the chapter’s final interruption.
- The Head Elder has revealed that Lee Seogeun’s death was connected to “their” intervention, without explaining the full plan or the accomplice.
- Taekyung has reached the battlefield and directly confronted the Head Elder.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Blade of Flowers,” “Blood Wolf Sword,” “Peak master,” and “Number One Under Heaven” consistent with established usage.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s Sound Transmission reveal that “they” killed Lee Seogeun without resolving the unidentified group or accomplice.
### Chapter 57

# Chapter 57

## Plot

Taekyung races to the Eight Spring Gorge battlefield with the reconnaissance squad and throws a spear at the Head Elder, who splits it with Sword Energy. Taekyung publicly curses the elder and calls him a traitor.

Jin Wikyung recognizes the accusation as the missing explanation for Lee Seogeun’s death and orders Wipeng to cut down the First Elder. The First Elder reveals concealed Peak-level martial arts, and the Second and Third Elders join him. They kill the White Tiger Hall Leader and expose a factional betrayal inside the Jin Family.

The First Elder fires a signal flare. The Three Paths Sect, Byeokdo Sect, Gunggwimun, and the other Five Gates of Shanxi forces turn on the battlefield, revealing a decades-old conspiracy. The Head Elder keeps the gravely wounded Lee Cheonbaek alive and immobilizes him, while Taekyung charges the elder with the reconnaissance squad.

## Continuity

- The Mount Heng–Jin Family battle at Eight Spring Gorge remains in progress, but the Head Elder’s betrayal and the Five Gates conspiracy are now exposed.
- Jin Wikyung has ordered Wipeng to attack the First Elder; the First, Second, and Third Elders have revealed hidden Peak-level martial arts and turned against the Jin Family command.
- Lee Cheonbaek survives but is silenced and paralyzed by the Head Elder, who intends to keep him alive for an unresolved purpose.
- Taekyung has reached the battlefield with the reconnaissance squad and directly confronts the Head Elder.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the established titles “Blade of Flowers,” “Blood Wolf Sword,” “Peak master,” and “Number One Under Heaven.”
- Keep the Head Elder’s betrayal exposed without resolving the full plan, the unidentified accomplice, or the purpose of the Five Gates signal.

# Chapter 58

## Plot

Taekyung attacks the Head Elder with the Gambler Title active, but the elder blocks the spear and overwhelms him with Sword Energy. The Head Elder slices the spear down to an iron rod, while Taekyung throws it and dives for the fallen Jin Wikyung.

Taekyung grabs the wrong man. He rescues the gravely wounded, silenced, and paralyzed Lee Cheonbaek instead of Wikyung, then retreats with Hyuk Mujin and the reconnaissance squad as the Head Elder attacks. With Sword Energy surging again, Taekyung orders the squad into an encircling formation to face the Head Elder like a raid boss.

- Taekyung’s location is with Hyuk Mujin and the reconnaissance squad outside the Head Elder’s immediate range; he has ordered an encircling formation.
- Taekyung mistakenly rescued Lee Cheonbaek rather than Jin Wikyung. Lee remains alive but cannot speak or move, and Jin Wikyung’s location and condition are unresolved.
- The Eight Spring Gorge battle, the Head Elder’s full plan, and the identity of his accomplice remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 59

# Chapter 59

## Plot

The Discipline Hall Master dies defending the Jin Family’s laws, and his dignified death reignites the senior members’ shame and anger. Jin Family martial artists rally against the traitors, while Mount Heng joins them after realizing that the black-clad forces are the common enemy.

Wipeng wounds the Third Elder, and the distraction lets Jin Wikyung and the surrounding fighters kill the Second Elder. Wipeng then takes the Third Elder’s head. The First Elder cuts down dozens of Jin Family martial artists but admits that the betrayal was driven by wealth and glory; Taekyung identifies revenge as the deeper motive and the First Elder directs him to ask the Head Elder. Jin Wikyung orders Wipeng and ten surviving senior members to hold the First Elder, expels him from the family, and leads guards toward the Head Elder after realizing Taekyung is in danger.

Taekyung reaches the Head Elder’s battlefield with the reconnaissance squad and realizes that his Hunter raid training is useless here: the squad has no tank, healer, or damage-role balance, only ten melee damage dealers. He orders them to keep throwing dirt as the fight remains unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Second and Third Elders are dead. The First Elder remains alive but is expelled from the Jin Family and left encircled by Wipeng and ten surviving senior members; his fate is unresolved.
- Jin Wikyung leads several dozen guards toward the Head Elder after the First Elder reveals that Taekyung is beyond the Head Elder’s position. Taekyung and the reconnaissance squad are confronting the Head Elder.
- The Head Elder’s betrayal, the First Elder’s reference to revenge, the unidentified Sound Transmission accomplice, and the full purpose of the Five Gates conspiracy remain unresolved.
- Lee Cheonbaek remains alive but unable to speak or move after Taekyung mistakenly rescued him instead of Jin Wikyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the “tank, damage dealer, healer” raid framing and Taekyung’s profane self-mockery at the cliffhanger.
- Keep “Discipline Hall Master,” “Sword Energy,” “First Elder,” and the established Jin Family and Mount Heng terminology.
