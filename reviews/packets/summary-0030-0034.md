## Previous block summary

# Chapters 25–29

## Plot

Gong Yacheong survives his fall and reunites with Socheon and Soyul. The **Survivors of the Sakju Branch** Quest completes, granting Taekyung EXP, Merit, Fame, and several level-ups before generating a Chain Quest. Gong identifies Jopil, One Question, One Kill, as the leader of the Sakju Branch massacre, and Socheon vows revenge. Taekyung promises to kill Jopil under the false name Hong Gil-dong, then tries to retreat after learning that Jopil is a Peak master. Hyuk Mujin exposes Taekyung’s identity to the survivors.

Jopil examines the massacre site, kills the Mount Heng overseers and guides, and deduces that an exceptionally skilled spearman killed Black Mountain Blade and the other pursuers. He tracks Taekyung’s group through the blizzard while Jin Wikyung sends Wipeng and more than twenty riders to find and protect Taekyung. The group struggles toward the main family as Gong’s poison worsens. Taekyung’s Main Quest still requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500 for Logout; he has reached Level 24 with displayed progress of 24/30 and 250/500.

Gong asks Taekyung to return Socheon and Soyul alive, creating the no-reward **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request** Quest. Taekyung initially orders the squad to abandon Gong, but Han Yeop disobeys and carries him. Taekyung turns back after realizing that abandoning the seemingly fictional survivors is emotionally impossible. A secret Sound Transmission reveals that the Head Elder sent Jopil, expects Taekyung’s death to change Jin Wikyung’s mind, and intends to drive out the Lower District Sect afterward; the accomplice remains unidentified.

The System forces Taekyung to confront Jopil. Jopil admits he is employed for a mission, recognizes Taekyung as the Jin Family’s third Young Master and the Sleeping Dragon, and offers to release only Taekyung. Taekyung refuses to abandon Gong, Socheon, Soyul, or the reconnaissance squad. Jopil attacks, killing one unnamed squad member with a throwing knife and overwhelming Taekyung with Peak-level speed and strength. His Berserk effect magnifies his Strength and Agility but makes his attacks broad and imprecise. Taekyung’s cut triggers the effect, and he orders the squad to stay back to preserve his Gambler Title’s one-on-one bonus. Hyuk and Han Yeop ignore him and attack; Jopil shatters Hyuk’s sword, cuts the head from Han’s spear, and throws both into trees. Taekyung’s Sky-Piercing Strike fails, and Jopil repeatedly wounds him before preparing another killing blow.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong, Socheon, and Soyul are the three recognized survivors of the Sakju Branch massacre. Gong is poisoned by the pursuers’ weapon coating; fasting pills restore stamina but do not detoxify him.
- Taekyung accepted **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request**, promising to return Socheon and Soyul alive. The Quest has no reward.
- Taekyung used the alias Hong Gil-dong until Hyuk Mujin revealed that he is Jin Taekyung.
- Jopil, One Question, One Kill, is a Peak master employed to complete an unspecified mission. He leads roughly fifty wandering martial artists and tracks the group through the blizzard with the help of a subordinate who is Third Rate in martial arts but Peak-level in tracking.
- Jopil killed Black Mountain Blade, the other pursuers, and the Mount Heng overseers and guides. The identity of the highly skilled spearman Jopil inferred from the corpses remains unknown.
- Jin Wikyung sent Wipeng and more than twenty riders to locate and protect Taekyung.
- The reconnaissance squad is traveling toward the main family. One unnamed member is dead. Hyuk Mujin remains Taekyung’s injured deputy; Han Yeop remains the Level 13 spear user, though Jopil destroyed his spearhead.
- The Head Elder secretly sent Jopil and plans to exploit Taekyung’s death against Jin Wikyung before driving out the Lower District Sect. His Sound Transmission accomplice and their full plan remain unresolved.
- Taekyung’s forced System Quest against Jopil is limited to Taekyung, requires survival, and fails on death. Jopil’s Berserk effect has worn off by the end of the fight.
- Taekyung’s Gambler Title grants a 10% combat-stat increase only in a one-on-one fight. His Sky-Piercing Strike failed against Jopil’s defense.
- Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Han Yeop are in immediate danger after Jopil’s final attack begins. The outcome remains unresolved.
- Logout still requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500. Taekyung has not advanced to First Rate and still cannot move the unidentified energy in his dantian.
- Taekyung’s growing inability to abandon the survivors intensifies the unresolved conflict between his belief that NPCs are unreal and his experience that they feel real. The capsule’s purpose, route home, and Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

- Preserve **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**, **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request**, and the forced Quest against Jopil as distinct System events.
- Keep Jopil’s epithet as **One Question, One Kill**, his Peak rank, and his recognition of Taekyung as the **Sleeping Dragon**.
- Preserve the Hong Gil-dong alias and briefly footnote the folk-hero and “Father”/“Brother” wordplay where needed.
- Retain **Sound Transmission**, **Gambler**, **Berserk**, **Sky-Piercing Strike**, **First Rate**, **Peak**, **Merit**, **Fame**, and **Logout** as established System or martial terminology.
- Preserve the contrast between wandering martial artists’ experience and ordinary martial artists, and keep Jopil’s tracking subordinate as Third Rate in martial arts but Peak-level in tracking.
- Keep the distinction between fasting pills that restore stamina and treatment that detoxifies poison.
- Preserve the dark action-comedy tone, including Taekyung’s Hong Gil-dong lie, his attempted retreat, the unnamed squad member’s death, and his conflicted attachment to NPCs.
- Footnote **Mount Beimang** as a burial mountain whose “hiking” idiom means dying, **goshiwon** as tiny inexpensive room-for-rent housing, and **junichi** as a fish-quality image if those references recur.

## Chapter beats

### Chapter 30

# Chapter 30

## Plot

Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm strikes Taekyung in the chest and nearly kills him. A brief flashback returns Taekyung to seven years earlier, when an instructor explains that his Hunter skill, **Thrust with All My Might**, has destructive power far beyond his F-rank body and can only be used when his life is in danger. Taekyung wakes on the snowfield and tricks Jopil by spitting blood, then uses daggers from his Inventory to stab Jopil’s foot, sever both Achilles tendons, and force qi deviation by driving a dagger into Jopil’s dantian as Jopil’s internal energy peaks.

Jopil burns his life to launch one final attack, his hair turning white and his body withering as he directs all his power at Taekyung. Taekyung picks up the broken **Sharp Spear**, awakens the hardened third internal energy in his dantian, and unleashes **Thrust with All My Might**. The attack evaporates half of Jopil’s upper body and kills him; Taekyung collapses as Socheon cries out while holding a sword.

## Continuity

- **Thrust with All My Might** is Taekyung’s Hunter skill from his first awakening. Its power exceeds his body’s limits and normally leaves him incapacitated, but it can save him in a life-or-death situation.
- Taekyung survives Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm, uses Inventory daggers to cripple Jopil, and causes qi deviation by stabbing Jopil’s dantian while Jopil’s internal energy is at its peak.
- Jopil is the nineteenth-generation successor of the Fire Gate Clan. He burns his life in a final attack, but Taekyung’s awakened hardened third internal energy powers the broken Sharp Spear’s decisive thrust.
- Jopil is dead. The immediate condition of Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop after Jopil blasted them into trees remains unclear.
- Taekyung’s unidentified hardened third internal energy can be awakened under extreme danger and greatly increase his physical power, but it tears through his already damaged qi and blood channels.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Thrust with All My Might**, **Flame Divine Palm**, **Fire Gate Clan**, **qi deviation**, **Inventory**, and **Sharp Spear** as established terminology.
- Preserve the abrupt seven-years-earlier Hunter-training flashback and its warning that Taekyung’s skill exceeds his body’s limits.
- Preserve Taekyung’s improvised, darkly comic dagger ambush and the violent final clash without softening the consequences of his injuries.
### Chapter 31

# Chapter 31

## Plot

After Jopil’s defeat, Taekyung remains unconscious for five days with life-threatening injuries. He wakes from a nightmare about his modern-world past: during an unusually lucrative goblin raid, greed led his team into a boss zone, where every teammate died except him. The memory leaves him confronting his responsibility for their deaths.

Wipeng tells Taekyung that the reconnaissance squad and the Sakju Branch survivors returned safely; two people are seriously injured, but neither is in danger. The unnamed squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan, and the Jin Family recovered and buried his body. Wipeng urges Taekyung not to reduce the young man’s death to a dog’s death, but to remember him and live his share as well. Taekyung asks for the dead man’s name and learns that the young man chose a dream as vast as his own: to become Number One of All Time. Wipeng’s dream is to become Number One Under Heaven. Taekyung begins to recover his resolve, and Wipeng promises even better news.

## Continuity

- Taekyung was unconscious for five days after killing Jopil; the Medicine King Hall Leader had expected him to die within a day.
- The reconnaissance squad and Socheon, Soyul, and Gong Yacheong returned safely. Two survivors have serious injuries, but their lives are not in danger.
- The unnamed squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan. His body was recovered, buried, and his chosen name was finally shared with Taekyung.
- Wipeng tells Taekyung to remember the dead and live the dead man’s share as well. Wipeng’s dream is Number One Under Heaven; the dead member’s dream was Number One of All Time.
- Taekyung’s modern-world backstory includes a disastrous Gate raid two years earlier: after he insisted on entering the boss zone, the rest of his team died and he alone survived.
- Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop survived Jopil’s attack; they suffered serious injuries, but their lives are not in danger.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the modern Hunter raid’s terminology—Gate, Demon Realm, Magic Gems, boss zone, F-rank, E-rank, and D-rank—distinct from Murim’s qi terminology.
- Preserve the contrast between Taekyung’s comic money-and-marriage conversation, the nightmare’s guilt, and Wipeng’s sincere counsel.
- Render Wipeng’s counsel as “live his share as well,” preserving the meaning of carrying the dead man’s remaining life rather than merely living for him.
### Chapter 32

# Chapter 32

## Plot

Wipeng reports that the Jin Family won a major battle at Honju: one hundred Jin Family elites defeated two hundred Mount Heng vanguard troops, fewer than thirty Mount Heng fighters escaped, and the Jin Family’s casualties were similar. The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s Lesser Family Head escaped, but Wipeng and Jin Wikyung each killed one of the Mount Heng Twin Devils, both Peak masters.

Wipeng praises Taekyung for defeating Jopil, One Question, One Kill, then recounts the fight with him in detail. He sincerely acknowledges Taekyung’s achievement but rejects Taekyung’s claim that he was stronger than Jopil, pointing out that Taekyung won through a surprise attack. Jin Wikyung arrives, hears the story, and becomes so emotional that he damages Taekyung’s bedroom while raging about Jopil.

Wikyung then carries Taekyung’s bandaged body to the broken wall and presents him to a crowd. The family’s exaggerated rumor says Taekyung led a death squad, defeated Jopil and one hundred wandering martial artists, and rescued the Sakju Branch’s household. Wikyung turns the rumor into a public celebration, and the crowd hails Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family won at Honju with one hundred elites against two hundred Mount Heng vanguard troops. Fewer than thirty Mount Heng fighters escaped, and Jin Family casualties were similar in number.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s Lesser Family Head escaped the Honju battle.
- Wipeng and Jin Wikyung each killed one of the Mount Heng Twin Devils, who were Peak masters.
- Wipeng considers Taekyung’s defeat of Jopil extraordinary but says Taekyung was not stronger than Jopil; Taekyung won through a surprise attack.
- Jin Wikyung publicly celebrates Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi after hearing an exaggerated account of his actions.
- Taekyung remains heavily bandaged and is carried by Wikyung before a crowd; his awaited System Rewards are not checked in this chapter.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved. The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the full purpose of their plan also remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Mount Heng Twin Devils**, **Peak**, **One Question, One Kill**, and **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi** as established titles and terminology.
- Preserve Wipeng’s sincere praise alongside his blunt correction that Taekyung only won through surprise, and retain Wikyung’s destructive affection and the crowd’s exaggerated rumor as dark action-comedy.
- Render the crude insult about Taekyung smearing shit on the family’s reputation without softening it.
### Chapter 33

# Chapter 33

## Plot

Lee Seogwang returns from Honju with only twenty-three Mount Heng survivors, including himself. Lee Cheonbaek, the Blood Wolf Sword and Sect Leader, learns that Jopil deceived the detachment into advancing to Honju, orders Seogwang back to the sect, and decides to replace the three lost Peak masters by hiring wandering martial artists, black-market fighters, and mounted bandits. With five hundred men assembled, Mount Heng plans to march on Taiyuan in three days; Cheonbaek also orders the suppression of rumors that Jin Taekyung killed Jopil.

Taekyung wakes on his second day of recovery with every injury healed. The physician explains that he stole and consumed a hundred-year snow ginseng capable of granting twenty years of internal energy; most of that energy was spent during the fight with Jopil. Taekyung checks his Status Window: he is Level 30, has 410 Fame and fifteen years of internal energy, and remains Second Rate. He also discovers that Thrust with All My Might has become the Peak Skill One Flash, whose power and cost can be adjusted but which leaves him helpless if overused.

Taekyung reflects on becoming a hero in Murim despite being a bottom-rung F-rank Hunter in the real world. He checks the Logout Main Quest and learns that Level 30 is complete, while First Rate and Fame 500 remain. Jin Wikyung is summoned as the best person to explain how to reach First Rate.

## Continuity

- Lee Seogwang is Lee Cheonbaek’s son and the Young Sect Leader of Mount Heng; twenty-three Mount Heng soldiers, including Seogwang, survived the Honju disaster.
- Mount Heng lost more than two hundred troops and three Peak masters at Honju. Lee Cheonbaek has about five hundred men assembled, plans to march on Taiyuan in three days, and is willing to hire wandering martial artists and mounted bandits.
- Taekyung recovers from Jopil’s attack within two days. The hundred-year snow ginseng granted twenty years of internal energy, but most of it was consumed when he used his Skill.
- Taekyung is Level 30 with Fame 410, fifteen years of internal energy, and the Second Rate realm. The Logout Main Quest still requires First Rate and Fame 500; Level 30 is complete.
- Thrust with All My Might is now the Peak, Second-Stage Skill **One Flash**, which allows its stamina and internal-energy cost to be adjusted and can leave Taekyung helpless.
- Taekyung recognizes that he enjoys living as a martial artist in Murim but still wants to return to his family and true self in the real world. Jin Wikyung is the person he intends to ask about reaching First Rate.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **One Flash**, **One Question, One Kill**, **Peak**, **First Rate**, **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi**, and **Logout** as established System or martial-world terminology.
- Preserve Lee Cheonbaek’s ruthless wartime pragmatism and the dark contrast between Taekyung’s heroic reputation in Murim and his self-mocking F-rank Hunter identity.
- Render the goshiwon explanation in a footnote without interrupting the first-person reflection.
### Chapter 34

# Chapter 34

## Plot

- Jin Wikyung tells Taekyung that Mount Heng is gathering troops and that the Jin Family will march north in two days to strike before the reinforcements join the enemy main force. The Five Gates of Shanxi alliance has agreed to help, and Wikyung asks Taekyung to command the rear guard. Taekyung accepts the Rear Guard Defense Quest.
- When Taekyung asks how to advance from the Second Rate realm, Wikyung tells him to believe in himself. Taekyung realizes that he has been trusting the System instead of his own achievements and reaches First Rate. The System raises every martial art by one stage, improves his body and meridians, expands his dantian, and grants two level-ups.
- Taekyung deliberately seeks out onlookers, gaining Fame from their awe and rumors. A representative of the Three Paths Sect, one of the Five Gates, introduces himself and pledges support to the Jin Family. Still short of the Logout Fame target, Taekyung heads to Medicine King Hall.

## Continuity

- The coming battle is scheduled to follow the Jin Family’s march in two days; the enemy could number about one thousand after reinforcements join the Blood Wolf Sword’s main force.
- Taekyung is now First Rate, but the Logout Main Quest still requires more Fame. Jin Wikyung’s advice is the trigger for the realm breakthrough.
- The Three Paths Sect has joined the Jin Family’s wartime support through the Five Gates of Shanxi alliance.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep First Rate, Second Rate, and Super First Rate as distinct realm terminology, and use Grade for the System field.
- Preserve Taekyung’s dry, profane self-mockery, the belief-in-yourself wordplay, and the awkward “God bless you” exchange.
- Render 三道問 as Three Paths Sect and retain the note that the given final character is 問 rather than the usual 門.
