## Previous block summary

# Chapters 20–24

## Plot

The Jin Family and the Lower District Sect formalize a wartime alliance. In exchange for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive rights to its pleasure district, the Lower District Sect stops selling Shanxi intelligence to other sects and devotes its resources to war intelligence. Wolhwa disguises the agreement by publicly claiming that she and Taekyung spent a passionate night together, presenting it as an unpaid Honghwaru tab. Her investigation has found neither evidence clearing Taekyung nor Lee Seogeun’s killer; she supports the Jin Family because either Taekyung is legitimate or the family is powerful enough to conceal the truth.

Taekyung recognizes Wolhwa’s coachman as Yama Whip, a Peak master. When the gate guards mistake Taekyung for the hero who helped Yama Whip defeat the Heavenly Axe, he encourages the story. The System reports that Poisoner rumors are fading, the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor is strengthening, and Taekyung gains 20 Fame. Meanwhile, Lee Cheonbaek, Mount Heng’s Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun’s father, expels the poison from his son’s body, vows revenge, and sends about two hundred armed martial artists toward the Jin Family.

Mount Heng’s attack begins with the murder of twenty-five children from the Jin Family’s Saneum, Eung-hyeon, and Sakju branches. The Head Elder calls the deaths necessary sacrifices and uses the Sleeping Dragon rumor and Taekyung’s supposed alliance with Yama Whip to force him into wartime service. Taekyung becomes leader of White Tiger Hall’s reconnaissance squad under the Head Elder’s faction and receives a repeating quest to earn 100 Merit.

The squad consists mostly of inexperienced second-rate martial artists. Taekyung appoints Level 22 Hyuk Mujin, who has killed five bandits, as deputy squad leader and assigns everyone numbers. He imposes a practical Hunter-style schedule, equips three members with wooden shields, and drills formations, dispersal, and all-out retreat. Hyuk mocks the retreat strategy and accuses Taekyung of causing the war. Taekyung knocks him unconscious with a sequence of slaps, then does so again when Hyuk attacks after waking in a hunter’s shelter during a blizzard. The rest of the squad accepts Taekyung’s methods and asks him to train them.

The squad is ordered to scout near Jeongyang and return within five days. A Lower District Sect messenger hawk reports that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, and a special detachment have appeared there. Jopil leads about fifty wandering martial artists in massacring survivors from the Sakju Branch, killing the defending martial artist and several women and children. He orders his First Rate subordinate Black Mountain Blade to pursue one escaping martial artist and two children toward Honju.

Fourteen-year-old Socheon flees with his younger sister, Soyul. Their mother led other survivors away earlier, while their father and the branch families were killed. Gong Yacheong, an old friend of Socheon’s father, guides the children and stays behind to delay the pursuers; his fate is unknown. The siblings reach the reconnaissance squad’s hunter’s shelter as more than twenty Mount Heng pursuers arrive. The System creates the Sudden Quest **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**.

Taekyung first orders an attack formation, then changes to a defensive formation so the squad can hold the enemy back while he claims the kills, EXP, and Merit. He charges alone through the low-level pursuers, using the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre and Spear Techniques and cycling through their forms. He finishes Level 32 Black Mountain Blade with Sky-Piercing Strike, completes the Survivors of the Sakju Branch Quest, receives substantial EXP and Merit, reaches at least Level 20, and triggers a Chain Quest.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family–Lower District Sect alliance lasts for the war. Its terms are half of Mount Heng’s shops, exclusive pleasure-district rights, and exclusive Lower District Sect intelligence support.
- Wolhwa is Eun Sowol, the Level 50 Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch. Yama Whip is her Peak-level coachman.
- Lee Cheonbaek is Mount Heng’s Sect Leader, the Blood Wolf Sword, and Lee Seogeun’s father. He has committed Mount Heng to revenge.
- Taekyung leads ten reconnaissance-squad members in White Tiger Hall. The deployed group has eleven people including Taekyung. The group has nine sword users, one spear user—Level 13 Han Yeop—and no experienced shield user before Taekyung assigns three wooden shields.
- Hyuk Mujin is Level 22, has killed five bandits, and is Taekyung’s deputy squad leader. Han Yeop enthusiastically follows Taekyung’s orders.
- The squad is scouting near Jeongyang under a five-day return deadline. Taekyung’s standard movement cycle is two hours of travel followed by fifteen minutes of rest.
- Jopil, One Question, One Kill, commands about fifty wandering martial artists. Black Mountain Blade, his First Rate right-hand man, is dead.
- Socheon and Soyul survived the Sakju Branch massacre. Their mother’s fate and Gong Yacheong’s fate remain unresolved.
- Taekyung completed the Sudden Quest **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**, gained large EXP and Merit, levelled up repeatedly, and activated a Chain Quest. Its requirements and outcome remain unresolved.
- The war, the unidentified assassin who killed Lee Seogeun, the capsule’s purpose, the route home, and Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

### Chapter 25

# Chapter 25

## Plot

Gong Yacheong awakens after his fall and reunites with Socheon and Soyul. Taekyung learns that the three are the only survivors identified by the System, and the **Survivors of the Sakju Branch** Quest completes, creating a Chain Quest and granting multiple level-ups, Merit, and Fame. Gong explains that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, led the Sakju Branch massacre; Socheon vows revenge.

Taekyung gives the survivors the false name Hong Gil-dong, promises to kill Jopil, and immediately tries to retreat after learning that Jopil is a Peak master. Socheon clings to him until Gong pulls the child away, calling Taekyung a criminal’s would-be victim. Hyuk Mujin then bursts into the shelter and exposes Taekyung’s identity as Jin Taekyung.

Jopil examines Black Mountain Blade’s corpse, learns that more than twenty men were killed, and murders the Mount Heng Sword Sect Hall Master and the ten-odd martial artists sent to oversee and guide the wandering force. He decides to pursue Taekyung’s group toward Honju. From the scars and footprints, Jopil reconstructs that a single highly skilled spearman killed the pursuers and Black Mountain Blade; the spearman’s identity remains unknown. Meanwhile, Jin Wikyung sends Wipeng and more than twenty riders to find and protect Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong, Socheon, and Soyul survive the Sakju Branch pursuit and are the three survivors recognized by the System.
- The **Survivors of the Sakju Branch** Quest completes and creates a Chain Quest; Taekyung receives Merit, Fame, and multiple level-ups.
- Taekyung uses the alias Hong Gil-dong with the survivors, promises Socheon that Jopil will die, and then tries to flee after learning Jopil is a Peak master.
- Hyuk Mujin arrives at the shelter and reveals Taekyung’s real identity to Gong Yacheong and the children.
- Jopil is a Peak master. He kills the Mount Heng Hall Master and the ten-odd martial artists assigned as overseers and guides, then orders the pursuit toward Honju.
- Jopil identifies an unknown highly skilled spearman as the killer of Black Mountain Blade and the other pursuers; the spearman is not identified as a Peak master.
- Jin Wikyung sends Wipeng with more than twenty riders to locate and protect Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the full quest name **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**; use the System’s later **Survivor** shorthand only where it appears.
- Keep Taekyung’s Hong Gil-dong lie and add concise footnotes for the folk hero and the tale’s “Father” and “Brother” wordplay.
- Keep the distinction between Jopil’s Peak rank and the unidentified spearman’s skill, and preserve the corpse-pile staging and the Mount Heng overseers’ joint role.
### Chapter 26

# Chapter 26

## Plot

Taekyung confirms his identity to Gong Yacheong after Hyuk Mujin exposes him. The reconnaissance squad rides toward the main family through a blizzard, and Gong explains that wandering martial artists are dangerous because of their hard-earned experience and Jopil’s relentless persistence. The group camps in crude snow burrows, still two or three days from the main family.

Taekyung opens the Main Quest and sees that Logout requires reaching First Rate, Level 30 (24/30), and Fame 500 (250/500). He distributes his remaining stat points and cultivates, but still cannot determine what he is missing for realm advancement. Jopil tracks the group through the storm: his subordinate is only Third Rate in martial arts but has Peak-level tracking skill, and Jopil orders stragglers killed before following the fugitives’ traces. Taekyung senses that danger is approaching while Jopil looks forward to meeting the mysterious master who has interested him for the first time in a long while.

Taekyung banters with the battered Hyuk and talks with Soyul about mind-reading and martial arts. Socheon keeps pace despite exhaustion, while Soyul says she wants to see her father; Taekyung recognizes the lie Socheon tells her about their parents. Gong remains pale and badly injured, and Taekyung realizes the group may soon be caught. Although he considers fleeing alone because the others are NPCs while he is real, an inexplicable resistance keeps him with them. He cannot yet explain that choice when night falls.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong now knows Taekyung is Jin Taekyung; Hyuk Mujin’s exposure of the identity lie is no longer concealed from the survivors.
- The reconnaissance squad travels toward the main family with Gong, Socheon, and Soyul; the journey should take two or three days, but a blizzard has slowed them.
- Gong explains that wandering martial artists’ battle experience makes them different from ordinary martial artists and warns that Jopil’s persistence makes him especially dangerous.
- The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500 for Logout; Taekyung’s displayed progress is 24/30 and 250/500.
- Taekyung distributes his remaining stat points and cultivates, but still does not know what is required to advance from Second Rate to First Rate.
- Jopil’s tracking subordinate is Third Rate in martial arts but has Peak-level tracking skill; Jopil commands numerous such hunting dogs.
- Jopil orders stragglers killed during the blizzard, leaves twenty stripped corpses behind, and resumes pursuit after finding the fugitives’ traces. He is interested in the mysterious master who has caught his attention for the first time in a long while.
- Taekyung senses that danger is near, continues traveling with the group, and wrestles with the fact that he is real while Gong, Socheon, Soyul, and the squad members are game-created NPCs.
- Gong is still pale and injured and may collapse despite fasting pills and circulated qi; Socheon is exhausted but refuses to fall behind.
- Soyul wants to become a proper young lady rather than learn martial arts and longs to see her father; Socheon has told her that their parents went out together.
- Taekyung’s decision to remain with the group is emotionally compelled but unexplained; the chapter ends with night falling before the pursuers catch them.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the contrast between ordinary martial artists and wandering martial artists rather than making the latter sound non-martial.
- Keep the tracker subordinate as the subject of the Third Rate/Peak tracking description, and preserve Jopil’s hunting-dog imagery.
- Retain the Main Quest’s exact Logout, First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500 terminology and displayed progress.
- Keep Taekyung’s dry, occasionally profane humor while rendering Hyuk’s caught-out “Hah” as a gasp rather than added profanity.
- Preserve the traditional time-period footnote for “shichen” and the chapter’s unresolved emotional conflict over NPCs and reality.
### Chapter 27

# Chapter 27

## Plot

Gong Yacheong reveals that the pursuers’ weapons were coated with cheap poison. Fasting pills restore stamina but cannot detoxify it, and Gong’s condition rapidly worsens. He asks Taekyung to return Socheon and Soyul alive, causing the System to create **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request**, a no-reward Quest that Taekyung accepts.

Taekyung orders the reconnaissance squad to leave Gong behind so the enemy cannot catch them. Han Yeop openly disobeys, then secretly leaves the rear of the formation to carry the unconscious Gong. Taekyung turns back after Soyul’s tiny hands feel real enough to make abandoning an NPC unbearable, finds Han carrying Gong, and helps him.

A hidden retreat scene reveals a secret Sound Transmission conversation. The Head Elder confirms that he sent Jopil and expects Taekyung’s death to change Jin Wikyung’s mind. He says he has waited too long for the resulting bloodshed and plans to drive out the Lower District Sect after the matter ends. The other participant’s identity and the wider plan remain unknown.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong is poisoned and rapidly deteriorating; fasting pills do not detoxify poison.
- Taekyung accepts **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request** to return Socheon and Soyul alive; the Quest offers no reward.
- Taekyung initially orders the squad to leave Gong behind, but Han Yeop disobeys and carries Gong away unconscious. Taekyung turns back and helps him.
- Taekyung’s guilt over abandoning an NPC intensifies when Soyul feels completely real to him.
- The Head Elder secretly sent Jopil and expects Taekyung’s death to change Jin Wikyung’s mind; he intends to remove the Lower District Sect afterward.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request** as the full System Quest title.
- Preserve the distinction between fasting pills that restore stamina and poison treatment that detoxifies.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s revelation and the unidentified Sound Transmission participant without naming or explaining either beyond the text.
### Chapter 28

# Chapter 28

## Plot

Taekyung’s attempt to cultivate the unidentified energy in his dantian still fails. As the reconnaissance squad prepares to leave, a pack of wolves approaches but flees when Taekyung releases enough qi to frighten them. The System creates a forced Quest to confront Jopil, One Question, One Kill, a Peak master; the Quest limits are Jin Taekyung, survival, and death on failure.

Jopil reveals that he killed Black Mountain Blade and the other pursuers, then offers to let Taekyung go if he answers truthfully. He recognizes Taekyung as the Jin Family’s third Young Master and the Sleeping Dragon, and says he is employed to complete a mission. After Taekyung answers, Jopil permits only Taekyung to leave, demanding that Gong Yacheong, Socheon, Soyul, and the reconnaissance squad remain behind. Taekyung refuses, and Jopil laughs savagely.

## Continuity

- Taekyung still cannot move the unidentified energy in his dantian.
- A wolf pack flees when Taekyung releases qi.
- The System creates a forced Quest to confront Jopil, One Question, One Kill; the Quest is limited to Jin Taekyung, requires survival, and fails on death.
- Jopil killed Black Mountain Blade and the other pursuers, is employed to complete a mission, and will allow only Taekyung to leave.
- Taekyung refuses to abandon Gong Yacheong, Socheon, Soyul, and the reconnaissance squad; Jopil laughs savagely.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Jopil’s epithet as One Question, One Kill and his recognition of Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon.
### Chapter 29

# Chapter 29

## Plot

Jopil attacks Taekyung and overwhelms him with the speed and power of a Peak master. A throwing knife kills one of the reconnaissance-squad members, and Taekyung’s anger finally drives him to fight rather than retreat. Jopil recognizes Taekyung’s talent and silk-reeling force, but a cut to Jopil’s clothes triggers the System’s Berserk effect and turns the fight into a one-sided bombardment.

Taekyung orders the squad to stay back so he can preserve the Gambler Title’s one-on-one bonus. Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop ignore him and attack Jopil together. Jopil shatters Hyuk’s sword, slices the head from Han Yeop’s spear, and blasts both men into trees. Taekyung then uses every bit of internal energy for Sky-Piercing Strike, but Jopil draws his sword faster, wounds Taekyung repeatedly, and prepares a final blow.

## Continuity

- Jopil is a Peak master whose speed and strength decisively exceed Taekyung’s and the earlier First Rate opponents.
- Jopil’s System Berserk effect increases his Strength and Agility but makes his attacks larger and less precise; it wears off before the chapter ends.
- Taekyung’s Gambler Title grants a 10% combat-stat increase only in a one-on-one fight, so he orders the reconnaissance squad not to intervene.
- One unnamed reconnaissance-squad member is killed by Jopil’s throwing knives; Taekyung cannot remember the member’s name despite having trained him.
- Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop attack Jopil despite Taekyung’s order. Jopil destroys Hyuk’s sword and Han Yeop’s spearhead, then throws both of them into trees.
- Taekyung’s Sky-Piercing Strike fails to pierce Jopil’s defense. Jopil cuts Taekyung’s chest, shoulder, knee, and side, then strikes toward his chest again; the outcome is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Footnote Mount Beimang as a burial mountain whose “hiking” idiom means being dead.
- Keep goshiwon and footnote it as very small, inexpensive room-for-rent housing.
- Preserve the junichi proverb image: “Even rotten, it’s still a junichi, I suppose,” with a brief fish-quality footnote.
- Preserve the rough sound effect “*Skrrrng.*” and the blood-slick crimson blade mechanics.
- Keep Jopil’s unfinished threat as “If you can take this, too.”
