## Previous block summary

# Chapters 15–19

## Plot

Taekyung defeats Lee Seogeun in the duel, completing the Duel Quest and gaining three levels, the Gambler title, Fame, Fourth-Stage Cultivation, and Third-Stage Qi Sense. Lee is later killed in the departing Jin Family carriage by an unidentified masked assassin using Sound Transmission, paralysis or toxin, and a blue-black needle; “going to Mount Beimang” foreshadows his death.

The Jin Family’s belief that Taekyung is the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi earns him additional Fame. While cultivating, he discovers a powerful unidentified energy in his dantian that rejects his contact and nearly consumes the internal energy he sends toward it. His cultivation raises his Sinews and Bones, but his Status Window still shows ten years of internal energy.

The Elder Council interrogates Taekyung over Lee’s poisoning and blames him for provoking Mount Heng. Jin Wikyung rejects the proposal to hand him over and calls for war. The Head Elder unexpectedly supports Wikyung and condemns the faction that framed Taekyung, though his political motives remain unclear. Mount Heng declares war, creating a System War relationship, designating Taekyung a public enemy, and triggering the Main Quest — War. Wikyung seals the family grounds and assumes operational control.

The Jin Family is badly outmatched: roughly 200 martial artists and three Peak masters against Mount Heng’s 300 or more martial artists and five Peak masters. With Shanxi sects refusing to answer the family’s requests for aid, Wikyung accepts assistance from Wolhwa, revealed to be Eun Sowol, the Level 50 Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch. In exchange, she demands half of Mount Heng’s shops and assets.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 17. His Cultivation, Spear, and Manoeuvre Techniques are Fourth Stage; Qi Sense is Third Stage and detects targets through Lv. 50.
- The Main Quest still requires First Rate, Lv. 30, and Fame 500 for the reward of Logout. Taekyung has 17/30 levels and approximately 70/500 Fame.
- Taekyung’s ten years of internal energy remain insufficient for advancement despite his strong physical stats. An unidentified, much stronger energy occupies his dantian and resists control.
- The Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor is spreading through the Jin Family. Taekyung deliberately confirms it, gaining 10 Fame, with later belief producing periodic Fame increases.
- Lee Seogeun is dead. His masked killer remains unidentified; the assassin used Sound Transmission, paralysis or poison, and a large blue-black needle.
- Jin Wikyung is Taekyung’s protective eldest brother and acting Family Head during the war. Wipeng remains his trusted Peak-level ally.
- The Head Elder is Taekyung’s great-uncle and the Jin Family’s highest-ranking elder. He supports Wikyung publicly, but his true plan is unresolved.
- Mount Heng Sword Sect and the Jin Family of Taiyuan are at war. Taekyung is a public enemy, and fleeing incurs severe System penalties. The family grounds are sealed without Wikyung’s authorization.
- Wolhwa is Eun Sowol, a Level 50 martial artist and Lower District Sect Branch Leader. Honghwaru is that sect’s Shanxi branch. Wikyung accepted her demand for half of Mount Heng’s shops and assets.
- The capsule’s purpose, the route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

- Keep **Sound Transmission**, **Elder Council**, **Head Elder**, **acting Family Head**, and the Jin Family’s **Cultivation**, **Spear**, and **Manoeuvre Techniques** consistent.
- Render the System’s war clause as: “Those who survive are strong, and only the strong will survive.”
- Preserve the brisk dark action-comedy and Taekyung’s dry, self-mocking profanity.
- Retain the gold-spoon/**God-Spoon** wordplay with a brief footnote where needed.
- Keep the assassination cues explicit: burning throat pain or loss of voice where applicable, paralysis or toxin effects, the Mount Beimang death idiom, and the masked assassin’s blue-black needle.

## Chapter beats

### Chapter 20

# Chapter 20

## Plot

The Jin Family and the Lower District Sect conclude their wartime alliance. In exchange for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive rights to the pleasure district, the Lower District Sect will stop selling Shanxi information to other sects and devote its resources to war intelligence. Wolhwa then provokes Taekyung by publicly claiming they spent a hot night together, allowing rumors to disguise the alliance as an unpaid Honghwaru tab.

Wolhwa admits that her sect has not found evidence clearing Taekyung or identified Lee Seogeun’s killer. She frames the alliance as a profitable investment: either Taekyung is innocent and legitimate, or the Jin Family concealed the crime so perfectly that its capability merits support. At the gate, Taekyung recognizes Wolhwa’s coachman as the Peak master Yama Whip. The guards mistake Taekyung for the hero who helped defeat Cheonryeokbu, and Taekyung deliberately encourages the legend. The System reports that Poisoner rumors are dying down, the Sleeping Dragon rumor is gaining credibility, and Taekyung gains 20 Fame and passionate supporters.

The chapter closes with Lee Cheonbaek, Sect Leader of Mount Heng and Lee Seogeun’s father, grieving over his poisoned son. He expels the poison, vows a thousandfold revenge, and sends about two hundred armed martial artists out that night.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family’s alliance with the Lower District Sect lasts for the duration of the war; the Lower District Sect will provide exclusive information support and devote all resources to war intelligence in exchange for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive pleasure-district rights.
- Wolhwa’s investigation has not identified Lee Seogeun’s killer or proved Taekyung innocent. Her support is a calculated investment based on either Taekyung’s legitimacy or the Jin Family’s ability to conceal the truth.
- Yama Whip is a Peak whip master who serves as Wolhwa’s coachman and previously survived an encounter with Cheonryeokbu alongside Taekyung.
- Taekyung gains 20 Fame when the gate guards’ mistaken hero story strengthens the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor and weakens Poisoner rumors; passionate supporters appear.
- Lee Cheonbaek is the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun’s father. He vows revenge and sends about two hundred armed martial artists toward the Jin Family.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the alliance terms precise: half of Mount Heng’s shops, exclusive pleasure-district rights, and exclusive Lower District Sect information support for the Jin Family during the war.
- Preserve Taekyung’s staged title delivery as “Yama. Whip. Great Hero!” rather than turning Yama Whip into a personal name.
- Render approximate counts as “about two hundred,” preserve **Super First Rate**, and retain the senior guards’ crude insult.
### Chapter 21

# Chapter 21

## Plot

Taekyung repeatedly practices the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique but still cannot draw out the hardened internal energy. When Mount Heng’s attack begins, he is brought to twenty-five murdered children from the Jin Family’s Saneum, Eung-hyeon, and Sakju branches, each marked with the character for “blood.” The Head Elder coldly frames the deaths as war’s necessary sacrifices, forcing Jin Wikyung to recover his composure while revealing the depth of his own ruthlessness.

The Head Elder then uses the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor and Taekyung’s supposed alliance with Yama Whip to push him into frontline service. Taekyung is appointed leader of White Tiger Hall’s reconnaissance squad and receives a repeating quest to earn 100 Merit. He accepts the compromise despite serving under the Head Elder’s faction, partly because of the murdered children and his growing tendency to treat NPCs as real people. After changing into his uniform and hiding the issued sword in his Inventory, he arrives at the squad’s dilapidated quarters with the Sharp Spear.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains Lv. 17. His Skill Window shows the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique at Fourth Stage, the Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques at Fifth Stage, Sinews and Bones at 105, and no remaining Skill Points.
- Mount Heng’s war against the Jin Family has begun; twenty-five children from the Saneum, Eung-hyeon, and Sakju branches were killed and marked with the character for “blood.”
- The Head Elder pressures Taekyung into wartime service by invoking his direct-family status, the Sleeping Dragon rumor, and his alleged role in defeating Cheonryeokbu with Yama Whip.
- Taekyung is the leader of White Tiger Hall’s reconnaissance squad. The System’s repeating quest requires him to earn 100 Merit, with rewards and failure penalties varying by outcome.
- Taekyung serves under White Tiger Hall’s Leader, who belongs to the Head Elder’s faction; the appointment was a compromise after Jin Wikyung opposed placing Taekyung in a more direct Elder Council combat unit.
- Taekyung hides the issued sword in his Inventory, carries the Sharp Spear, and moves into the reconnaissance squad’s communal quarters.
- Taekyung recognizes that he is increasingly treating NPCs as real people and worries that he is becoming immersed in the game.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the “blood” character carved into the children’s foreheads and the Head Elder’s cold war-sacrifice argument without softening the violence.
- Keep the quest as a repeating quest for 100 Merit and retain **White Tiger Hall**, **reconnaissance squad**, **Sharp Spear**, and **Ordinary Martial Artist #1**.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s ambiguous combination of political pressure, apparent aid to Wikyung, and ruthless composure.
### Chapter 22

# Chapter 22

## Plot

Taekyung takes command of White Tiger Hall’s ten-person reconnaissance squad and finds a room full of second-rate rookies. Hyuk Mujin is the strongest at Level 22, while Han Yeop is Level 13. Taekyung tests their combat and killing experience, appoints the only veteran—Hyuk, who has killed five bandits—as deputy squad leader, and assigns everyone a number. He then tries to organize their party composition, only to discover that nine members use swords, Han Yeop alone uses a spear, and nobody knows how to use a shield. A bell announces Mi-si and the squad’s first deployment.

The squad leaves to scout the county towns near the Jin Family. A day later, Wipeng reassures Jin Wikyung that Taekyung should be fine. Wikyung initially misunderstands him, then insists Taekyung is an adult and says he can stop worrying about his youngest brother while focusing on the main family. The silence lasts only two hours before a messenger hawk from the Lower District Sect reports that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, and twenty members of a special detachment have appeared in Jeongyang. Wikyung orders fifty martial artists sent there to bring the family members home safely, then anxiously asks where Taekyung went; Wipeng answers, “……Jeongyang.”

## Continuity

- Taekyung leads a ten-person White Tiger Hall reconnaissance squad and departs to scout county towns near the Jin Family; the trip to the area should take up to two days.
- Hyuk Mujin is Lv. 22 and has killed five people during a bandit suppression campaign. Taekyung appoints him deputy squad leader and calls him Number One.
- Han Yeop is Lv. 13, has combat experience but fewer than five battles, uses a spear, and enthusiastically follows Taekyung’s orders.
- The squad has nine sword users, one spear user, and no shield user; four members drop their hands when asked who has killed someone.
- Taekyung applies his F-rank Hunter experience to command and plans to teach the rookies practical melee tricks for his own survival.
- The squad’s first deployment is announced by three bells at Mi-si, approximately 1–3 p.m.[^1]
- After one day, Jin Wikyung remains worried about Taekyung but argues that his grown younger brother can manage without being coddled.
- The Lower District Sect’s messenger hawk reports that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, and twenty special-detachment members have appeared in Jeongyang.
- Wikyung orders fifty martial artists to Jeongyang to retrieve Jin Family members safely and asks where Taekyung is; Wipeng says Taekyung went to Jeongyang.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Taekyung’s numbered-squad joke, his black-cat/white-cat pragmatism, and the contrast between Murim sword orthodoxy and practical Hunter combat.
- Keep **One Question, One Kill** as Jopil’s title and retain the Mi-si footnote as a factual explanation of the time division.

[^1]: Mi-si is one of the traditional two-hour divisions of the day, corresponding roughly to 1–3 p.m.
### Chapter 23

# Chapter 23

## Plot

The reconnaissance squad receives its first mission: scout near Jeongyang and return within five days. Taekyung imposes a two-hour travel and fifteen-minute rest schedule, assigns three reluctant members as shield-bearing tanks, and drills the ten-person squad through basic formation, dispersal, and all-out retreat. Hyuk Mujin mocks the retreat order and accuses Taekyung of causing the war. Taekyung answers with five slaps, catching Hyuk’s fourth punch and holding him upright until the next blow knocks him unconscious. When Hyuk wakes in a hunter’s shelter during a sudden blizzard, he charges again and is slapped unconscious a second time. The rest of the squad, now convinced that Taekyung’s methods have merit, asks to train; Taekyung reflects that one beating is better than a hundred explanations.

At around noon, Jopil, One Question, One Kill, leads about fifty wandering martial artists in attacking Jin Family survivors from the Sakju Branch. They kill the defending martial artist and six or seven women and children, including the Branch Leader’s family. Jopil orders Black Mountain Blade, a First Rate wandering martial artist and his right-hand man, to pursue the last escapees: one martial artist and two children who passed through Jeongyang and are heading for Honju. Black Mountain Blade takes half the group and has half a day to finish the hunt.

## Continuity

- Taekyung’s reconnaissance squad is ordered to scout near Jeongyang and return within five days.
- Taekyung enforces two hours of travel followed by fifteen minutes of rest and equips three squad members with wooden shields taken from the Jin Family armory.
- The squad formation has three shield bearers in front, six swordsmen behind them, and Taekyung with spear user Han Yeop in the rear; Taekyung also drills dispersal and all-out retreat.
- Hyuk Mujin is contemptuous of Taekyung’s retreat tactics and accuses him of causing the war. Taekyung catches Hyuk’s fourth punch, keeps him upright by holding his fist, then knocks him out with a fifth blow; Hyuk is knocked out again after charging Taekyung at the shelter.
- A sudden blizzard forces the squad into a small hunter’s shelter. After seeing Taekyung beat Hyuk, the other squad members ask him to train them.
- Jopil, One Question, One Kill, leads about fifty wandering martial artists in a massacre of Jin Family survivors from the Sakju Branch.
- Black Mountain Blade is Jopil’s right-hand man, a First Rate wandering martial artist. He is ordered to pursue one martial artist and two children toward Honju with half the group.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Taekyung’s practical Hunter-style command, the party-composition joke, and his darkly comic use of violence as instruction.
- Keep Hyuk’s accusation, Han Yeop’s self-correction from Third Young Master to Squad Leader, the five-blow sequence, and the causal mechanic of Taekyung’s grip holding Hyuk upright.
- Preserve the twisted proverb “Seeing once, my ass—beating once is better than a hundred hearings” and its explanatory follow-up.
- Retain Jopil’s title as **One Question, One Kill**, Black Mountain Blade as the subordinate’s nickname, and the Sakju Branch massacre as the chapter’s closing threat.
### Chapter 24

# Chapter 24

## Plot

Fourteen-year-old Socheon flees the destruction of the Sakju Branch with his younger sister, Soyul. Their mother led other people away earlier, while their father and the branch’s families were killed. Gong Yacheong, an old friend of Socheon’s father, guides and protects the children toward Honju. When pursuers catch up, Gong stays behind to delay them, and Socheon reaches a hilltop clearing where ten men in navy uniforms bearing the Jin character are waiting.

At the hunter’s shelter, Taekyung’s reconnaissance squad hears the approaching chase. Socheon runs toward them carrying Soyul, followed by more than twenty Mount Heng Sword Sect pursuers. The System creates the Sudden Quest **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**, requiring Taekyung to rescue the survivors. Taekyung orders an attack formation, then changes it to a defensive formation so the squad can hold the enemy back while he takes the kills and collects the EXP and Merit himself.

Qi Sense reveals several pursuers at Levels 11 and 12. Taekyung charges into the group and cuts them down with the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre and Spear Techniques, advancing through their center and cycling through the first four forms. He finishes the last opponent with Cheongwan-il, the final form of the Jin Family’s Spear Technique. The System identifies the opponent as Level 32 Black Mountain Blade, announces the completion of the Survivors Quest, creates a Chain Quest, grants large amounts of EXP and Merit, and reports multiple level-ups.

## Continuity

- Socheon is a fourteen-year-old survivor of the Sakju Branch massacre; he protects and carries his younger sister, Soyul.
- Socheon’s mother led other survivors away before the siblings fled; her fate is unknown.
- Gong Yacheong, called Uncle Gong, is an old friend of Socheon’s father and the siblings’ guide and protector. He stays behind to delay the pursuers; his fate is unresolved.
- Taekyung’s reconnaissance squad encounters Socheon and Soyul at the hunter’s shelter while more than twenty Mount Heng Sword Sect pursuers close in.
- The System creates the Sudden Quest **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**. Taekyung completes it, triggering a Chain Quest.
- Taekyung deliberately claims the pursuers’ kills for EXP and 50 Merit per kill while ordering the squad to hold them back.
- Black Mountain Blade is Level 32 and is defeated by Taekyung’s Cheongwan-il. Taekyung receives large EXP and Merit and levels up repeatedly.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the contrast between Socheon’s desperate flight and Taekyung’s immediate dread of another disaster, followed by his greedy EXP-driven response.
- Keep the distinction between Gong Yacheong leading the siblings and his uncertain fate after staying behind.
- Render `반 시진` as “more than half a shichen” and retain the spoiler-free explanatory footnote for the roughly two-hour traditional time period.
- Keep the System quest as a single blockquote panel and preserve the unknown reward and failure fields.
- Preserve the formation commands as “Form up,” Taekyung’s stunned “...Huh?” at the pursuers’ low levels, the Manoeuvre Technique’s role in his advance, and “Splurt!” for the final impact.
