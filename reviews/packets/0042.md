# Chapter 42

A Gate.

The greatest scar left behind by the Great Cataclysm.

More than thirty years ago, Demon King Asmodeus had used one as an invasion route. These days, Gates had long since become both a means of earning a living for Hunters and mining sites for a higher-dimensional energy resource called Magic Gems.

*Damn, what’s that smell?*

“This place is something else. Do they not air it out?”

1.98 million won.

Being a Hunter was a profession where your spending was almost as high as your income. Most of that spending went toward equipment.

*Because equipment without mana—or magic—imbued in it breaks down in no time.*

The most common method was to use Magic Gems obtained from Gate monsters as materials for crafting equipment.

Click.

*Money really is the best.*

The world was ruled by capital, and Gates were no different. People with money fought comfortably with magical equipment, while people without it had to carry torches.

The other four were veteran E-rank Hunters with at least a decade of experience each.

The man who had been directing the flow of the battle until then.

Slash—

A head shot into the air along a clean arc. The monsters couldn’t react properly to what had happened.

Slash. Slash. Slash.

A mid-level Hunter and the lowest-level Hunter.

As a porter.

I wanted to tell him.

*I’m stronger than you.*

But in the end, I couldn’t say it. This was my reality.

*I was* stronger than you.
