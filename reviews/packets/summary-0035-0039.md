## Previous block summary

# Chapters 30–34

## Plot

Taekyung survives Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm and uses Inventory daggers to sever Jopil’s Achilles tendons and pierce his dantian, causing qi deviation. When Jopil burns his life for one final attack, Taekyung awakens the hardened third energy in his dantian, channels it through the broken Sharp Spear, and uses **Thrust with All My Might** to kill him. The skill’s destructive power exceeds Taekyung’s body’s limits, leaving him critically injured.

Taekyung remains unconscious for five days and confronts, in a nightmare, the guilt surrounding a disastrous modern-world Gate raid where his team died after he insisted on entering the boss zone. Wipeng reports that the reconnaissance squad and the Sakju Branch survivors returned safely. The squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan who dreamed of becoming Number One of All Time; Wipeng urges Taekyung to remember him and live his share as well.

The Jin Family wins a major battle at Honju, where one hundred elites defeat two hundred Mount Heng vanguard troops. Wipeng and Jin Wikyung each kill one of the Mount Heng Twin Devils, while the Mount Heng Lesser Family Head escapes. Jin Wikyung publicly celebrates Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, though the family’s rumor greatly exaggerates his victory.

Taekyung recovers rapidly after consuming a hundred-year snow ginseng. He reaches Level 30, has fifteen years of internal energy, and remains Second Rate until Wikyung explains that martial arts begin with belief. Realizing he has trusted the System’s label instead of his own achievements, Taekyung reaches First Rate. His martial arts advance by one stage, his body and meridians improve, his dantian expands, and he gains two levels. **Thrust with All My Might** evolves into the Peak, Second-Stage Skill **One Flash**, whose cost can be adjusted but whose overuse can leave him helpless.

Mount Heng gathers roughly five hundred fighters and plans to march on Taiyuan. The Jin Family will march north in two days, aided by the Five Gates of Shanxi, and strike before Mount Heng’s reinforcements join its main force. Taekyung accepts the **Rear Guard Defense** Quest, deliberately cultivates public awe to gain Fame, and goes to Medicine King Hall while still short of the Fame required for Logout.

## Continuity

- Jopil, One Question, One Kill, is dead. He was the nineteenth-generation successor of the Fire Gate Clan.
- Taekyung survived the fight but suffered severe damage to his qi and blood channels. The awakened hardened third dantian energy remains a major but dangerous power source.
- **Thrust with All My Might** evolved into **One Flash**, a Peak, Second-Stage Skill with adjustable stamina and internal-energy costs.
- Taekyung is now First Rate, has reached Level 30 and gained two additional level-ups, and possesses fifteen years of internal energy. His Fame remains below 500, so Logout is still unavailable.
- The reconnaissance squad and Gong Yacheong, Socheon, and Soyul returned safely. Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop survived with serious but nonfatal injuries.
- The unnamed squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan; his body was recovered and buried, and his dream was to become Number One of All Time. Wipeng’s dream is Number One Under Heaven.
- Taekyung’s modern-world team died during a Gate raid after he insisted on entering the boss zone; he alone survived.
- The Jin Family defeated Mount Heng’s vanguard at Honju. Fewer than thirty Mount Heng fighters escaped, Jin Family casualties were similar in number, and Mount Heng lost three Peak masters. The Lesser Family Head escaped.
- Wipeng and Jin Wikyung killed the Mount Heng Twin Devils, both Peak masters.
- Mount Heng now has about five hundred assembled fighters, including hired wandering martial artists, black-market fighters, and mounted bandits. Lee Cheonbaek intends to march on Taiyuan and suppress rumors of Taekyung’s victory over Jopil.
- The Jin Family will march north in two days. The Five Gates of Shanxi has pledged support, including the Three Paths Sect. The enemy force could reach about one thousand after reinforcements join the Blood Wolf Sword’s main force.
- Taekyung accepted the **Rear Guard Defense** short-term Quest and is expected to command the rear guard.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.
- The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the full purpose of their plan remain unidentified.
- Medicine King Hall entry is restricted to authorized personnel; Taekyung has gone there seeking additional Fame.

### Chapter 38

# Chapter 38

## Plot

- Gwak Jun reveals that the twenty supposed Three Paths Sect martial artists in the rear guard are assassins ordered to eliminate everyone and join the main force. They are all First Rate, and Taekyung also detects an ambush beyond his Qi Sense range.
- A herd of Level 1 water deer accidentally breaks the ambush. The System creates the Peak Slay the Assassins Quest for Taekyung alone: kill twenty assassins for EXP and Fame, with death as failure.
- Taekyung and the reconnaissance squad fight the assassins. The squad's drilled formations hold against the stronger opponents, while Taekyung uses Inventory weapons and One Flash to destroy them. Gwak Jun tries to flee but is caught and beaten into submission.
- Under interrogation, Gwak insists that the real Three Paths Sect was established thirty years earlier according to “that person’s” will. Hyuk calculates that more than one hundred allied martial artists came from the Three Paths Sect and Gunggwimun, while nearly half the main force belongs to the Head Elder’s faction. Taekyung concludes that the Head Elder may be the traitor who united the Jin Family’s strength to seize Shanxi in one battle.
- Gwak severs his own heart meridian and dies before revealing the full plan. The System completes Slay the Assassins, grants EXP, a level-up, and 50 Fame, raising Taekyung to the Logout threshold. Logout begins its three-second countdown, but Taekyung loses consciousness as darkness closes in.

## Continuity

- Gwak Jun and the twenty infiltrators are assassins posing as the Three Paths Sect; the infiltrators are all First Rate, and Gwak is Lv. 40.
- The Three Paths Sect was established thirty years ago according to the will of an unidentified conspirator. More than one hundred martial artists from the Three Paths Sect and Gunggwimun joined the Jin Family’s force, and nearly half the main force belongs to the Head Elder’s faction.
- Taekyung remains First Rate and begins the chapter at Fame 497/500. He completes the Peak Slay the Assassins Quest, gains 50 Fame and a level-up, meets all Logout conditions, and enters the three-second Logout countdown; whether he actually returns remains unresolved.
- The reconnaissance squad’s formation training lets them survive and defeat stronger First Rate opponents. Taekyung’s trust in the squad is returned through coordinated fighting.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved. The Head Elder’s betrayal is strongly suspected, but the unidentified accomplice and full plan remain unresolved.

- Preserve the abrupt water-deer interruption, Taekyung’s violent dark comedy, the squad’s practical teamwork, and the cliffhanger ending.

### Chapter 39

# Chapter 39

## Plot

- Taekyung wakes inside the old capsule after successfully logging out of Murim. He finds that exactly thirty days passed there but only three hours passed in reality, and discovers Seong Jinho drunk in the room. He wakes Jinho roughly and explains what happened; Jinho dismisses the story as insanity.
- Jinho tries the capsule himself, but its door opens immediately because the power cord is unplugged. Taekyung is left questioning how the experience could have been a delusion when he remembers Murim and its people as real.
- The capsule's product manual identifies it as the Ark - 2020, manufactured by H Soft on January 1, 2020. Its precautions repeat that the player cannot log out at will and cannot resurrect after death. Its key features include permanent user binding until death, a slow and reversible reality/game time ratio, and Character Synchronization.
- Taekyung searches for H Soft and finds only an unrelated porn studio, then decides to lock the door and think.

## Continuity

- Logout succeeds, but the System is unavailable in reality. The observed thirty-days-to-three-hours ratio is not quantified; the manual only says time passes very slowly in both directions.
- The capsule is unplugged despite Taekyung's experience. Its origin, purpose, permanent binding, Character Synchronization, and route back to Murim remain unexplained.
- Taekyung knows the Murim events were real and worries that the Head Elder's betrayal and the war may still be unfolding while time behaves differently there. Jinho currently believes Taekyung is mentally ill.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the product's displayed forms **Ark - 2020**, **H Soft**, and **Character Synchronization**.
- Keep Taekyung's modern, dry, self-mocking first-person voice and Jinho's blunt informal speech in the return-to-reality scenes.
