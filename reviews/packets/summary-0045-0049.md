## Previous block summary

# Chapters 40–44

## Plot

Taekyung wakes in reality after dreaming of the Jin Family’s battle. Seong Jinho cannot see the Ark - 2020 manual’s text, confirming that its contents are visible only to Taekyung. Taekyung tests a popular virtual-reality game, but its artificial NPCs and sensations cannot compare with Murim. After speaking with his sister Hayeon and thinking of his mother, he accepts that he has returned home and decides to live with his family. He discards the capsule and leaves for work; Jinho, still skeptical but curious, begins investigating H Soft.

Taekyung returns to Hunter work after Sopung Guild fires him. His old acquaintance Im Kkeokjeong, now the E-rank Hunter Im Hyeokjun, recommends him to Team Leader Choi of the newly formed Peace Guild. Taekyung joins an E-rank Gate as a porter. Inside, Choi and the veteran Hunters defeat a Hobgoblin group, while Taekyung efficiently processes the corpses.

In the Boss Zone, an old Hobgoblin Priest uses nearly one hundred corpses to restore a stone gate, trap the party, and summon a C-rank Hobgoblin Great Warrior. The Great Warrior overwhelms Im and the other veterans, leaving them unconscious. Taekyung lures it away, then returns to protect them and blocks its greatsword. This triggers Character Synchronization in reality: “Synchronization complete” and “All systems are inherited.” The System identifies the monster as Level 45. Taekyung reverses the fight with his spear. Choi kills the Hobgoblin Priest, then watches in disbelief as the supposedly F-rank porter severely maims the Great Warrior.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is back in reality, officially an F-rank Hunter and working as a porter for Peace Guild.
- Character Synchronization has restored all of Taekyung’s systems in reality, though the consequences and limits of this restoration are unknown.
- Im Kkeokjeong/Im Hyeokjun and the other veteran E-rank Hunters are unconscious but alive in the Boss Zone.
- Team Leader Choi is a C-rank Hunter, Peace Guild’s leader for this raid, and an equipment-focused fighter who uses a sword and wind-based Haste magic.
- Peace Guild is newly formed and has only three members, including its Guild Master.
- The Hobgoblin Priest is dead. The Level 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior has been severely wounded, but its final fate and whether Taekyung can defeat it remain unresolved.
- The party remains inside the Boss Zone; the stone gate and route out have not been resolved.
- The Ark - 2020 capsule’s purpose, H Soft’s connection to it, Character Synchronization’s full scope, and any route back to Murim remain unexplained. The manual’s permanent user binding and adjustable time ratio remain binding facts.
- Murim’s death and resurrection limits remain unknown.
- The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the wider plan against the Jin Family remain unresolved.

### Chapter 45

# Chapter 45

## Plot

Taekyung severs both arms of the Hobgoblin Great Warrior and kills the Level 45 C-rank Rare Monster, triggering a System victory notice and level-up. Team Leader Choi has already killed the Hobgoblin Priest and is stunned by Taekyung’s strength. Officials learn that an E-rank Gate contained two C-rank Rare Monsters and begin an investigation; Im Kkeokjeong and the other veterans are sent to the hospital. Taekyung confirms that his restored System now shows Level 33, First Rate Martial Artist, renewed stats and titles, and the incomplete Traitor Chain Quest. Choi takes him out for top-grade Hanwoo after Taekyung asks him to keep his strength secret.

## Continuity

- The Hobgoblin Priest and Level 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior are dead; the Peace Guild party survives, though Im and the other veterans need about a week in the hospital.
- Taekyung’s restored System displays Level 33, First Rate Martial Artist, 120 Strength, 125 Stamina, 121 Agility, 20 Intelligence, 20 Charm, fifteen years of Internal Energy, and 30 Remaining Points.
- Character Synchronization has changed Taekyung’s Status Window: Fame is 0 and only Novice Trainee and Gambler remain among his Titles.
- The Traitor Chain Quest is active but incomplete; its mission is to punish the traitor and lead the battle to victory, with failure resulting in death.
- Officials are investigating how two C-rank Rare Monsters appeared in an E-rank Gate, and Taekyung’s unusual power may draw scrutiny.
- Taekyung now accepts that Murim was real and continues to wonder whether he can return.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the System wording “You have defeated Lv. 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior!” and “Level Up!”
- Keep “First Rate Martial Artist,” “Fame,” “Titles,” “Remaining Points,” “Internal Energy,” and “Traitor” consistent with established System terminology.
- Retain Taekyung’s dry, self-mocking humor in the investigation, equipment-damage excuse, and Hanwoo ending.
- Keep the Hanwoo footnote identifying it as beef from Korean native cattle.
### Chapter 46

# Chapter 46

## Plot

After the Boss Zone, Team Leader Choi treats Taekyung to an extravagant Hanwoo meal and offers him a Peace Guild position with a 100 million won signing bonus. Taekyung refuses for now because he fears the System could disappear and leave him unable to earn a living. Choi investigates Taekyung's history and learns that he was the sole survivor of the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate, then returned to Gate work for a year and a half before being fired three days earlier; Choi concludes that Taekyung is at least C-rank and may have recently reawakened.

Taekyung discovers that his discarded Ark - 2020 capsule was taken by Seong Jinho, who returned it to his room after bargaining over a finder’s fee. The manual confirms that the capsule is permanently bound to its registered user until death. Taekyung’s Item Check reads the capsule and its manual as unreadable even though it reads ordinary objects correctly. Realizing how much stronger he has become, he goes to the Bucheon Branch of the Hunter Association for a Hunter rank reassessment.

## Continuity

- Team Leader Choi has offered Taekyung a Peace Guild position and a 100 million won signing bonus; Taekyung has not accepted because the System’s continued availability is uncertain.
- Choi now knows Taekyung was the sole survivor of the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate, returned to Gate work for a year and a half, and was fired three days ago. Choi suspects Taekyung is at least C-rank and may have recently reawakened.
- Seong Jinho took the discarded Ark - 2020 capsule and returned it to Taekyung’s room after demanding a finder’s fee. The manual confirms permanent user binding until death.
- The System can read ordinary objects but cannot read the capsule or its manual. Taekyung is traveling to the Bucheon Branch of the Hunter Association for a rank reassessment.
- Whether Choi will keep Taekyung’s strength secret, what the capsule is, and whether Taekyung can return to Murim remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the 100 million won recruitment offer and Taekyung’s fear that losing the System would make him a “human disaster.”
- Keep “Hunter rank reassessment” for the ordinary Hunter classification, distinct from System/UI “Grade.”
- Preserve the *sip-pal* (“eighteen”) and swear-word echo in the finder’s-fee exchange with a footnote.
- Keep the exact System notification “This Item cannot be read.” and the capsule manual’s permanent-binding rule.
- Retain the dry, self-mocking humor in the Hanwoo meal, Jinho’s finder’s fee, and the Seoul-taxi punchline.
### Chapter 47

# Chapter 47

## Plot

Taekyung visits the Hunter Association’s Bucheon Branch for a Hunter rank reassessment and runs into Kim Sangshik, his former team leader at Sopung Guild. Kim mocks the reassessment and Taekyung’s prospects, but Taekyung confronts him over arranging his dismissal and taking money to install a replacement. When Kim grabs him, Taekyung effortlessly catches and crushes his wrist with his synchronized martial-arts strength before both men avoid a scene in front of an Association examiner. The reassessment begins, but the measuring device malfunctions when it scans Taekyung’s internal energy, leaving his result unresolved.

From Kim’s viewpoint, his promising D-rank son has secured a place at Sopung Guild because Kim pushed out the lowest-ranked Hunter. Kim suspects Taekyung may have reawakened after feeling his strength, but a report of a new C-rank Hunter with A-rank mana control draws his attention. The candidate is Choi Min-su of Sangdong Guild. Sopung’s Guild Master orders Kim to secure him at any price and promises to exceed the major Guilds’ offers; when the measurement-room elevator opens, Kim instead finds Taekyung waiting to meet him again.

## Continuity

- Taekyung’s Hunter rank reassessment is underway at the Bucheon Branch; the device detects an error while measuring his internal energy, so his new rank is unresolved.
- Kim Sangshik is Taekyung’s former boss, a founding member and team leader of Sopung Guild. He helped arrange Taekyung’s dismissal and apparently took money to place a replacement; he is one of Sopung’s three D-rank Hunters and is Level 24.
- Taekyung’s synchronized strength overwhelms Kim when Kim grabs his shoulder, but their confrontation does not become a public Association incident.
- Kim has a promising D-rank son whose Guild placement he arranged by cutting the lowest-ranked Hunter. He now suspects Taekyung may be a reawakened Hunter.
- Choi Min-su of Sangdong Guild has newly measured as C-rank with A-rank mana control. Sopung’s Guild Master is traveling to recruit him and has authorized Kim to beat competing offers.
- Taekyung and Kim are set to meet again immediately after the chapter’s cliffhanger; Taekyung’s reassessment result and Choi Min-su’s recruitment remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Hunter rank reassessment” distinct from System/UI “Grade.”
- Preserve Kim Sangshik’s petty, abusive voice and the contrast between Taekyung’s calm confrontation and his effortless strength.
- Retain the C-rank/A-rank distinction for Choi Min-su’s Hunter rank and mana-control ability.
### Chapter 48

# Chapter 48

## Plot

The Hunter Association’s reassessment identifies Taekyung as C-rank-level, and Guild scouts immediately surround him with recruitment offers. Kim Sangshik switches from insulting him to obsequious apologies and asks him to join Sopung Guild, promising to exceed every competing offer. Taekyung tells Kim to inform the Guild Master that he will consider it only if Sopung gets rid of one man he cannot stand—the obvious target being Kim himself.

Taekyung returns to Huimang Goshiwon, buys beef with the 10.3 million won Team Leader Choi paid him for the raid, and tells Seong Jinho a cover story about the two Rare Monsters and the reassessment. Jinho is moved to tears while congratulating him on becoming a C-rank Hunter, then immediately demands the capsule finder’s fee as a joke. Taekyung becomes afflicted with the System’s Dead Drunk status effect, reflects on the price of the System and his years of hardship, and dreams of indistinct Murim voices calling him squad leader and telling the youngest to survive. He forgets the dream after waking.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is recognized as C-rank-level at the Hunter Association, but the formal reassessment procedure will take several days, so he is not technically registered as a C-rank Hunter yet.
- Guild scouts compete to recruit Taekyung. Sopung Guild’s Guild Master authorizes Kim Sangshik to exceed competing offers, but Taekyung rejects Kim’s approach and demands that Sopung remove one person he hates.
- Team Leader Choi pays Taekyung 10.3 million won so far for the raid, including an extra ten million won; additional payment for selling the Rare Monsters’ equipment, leather, and Magic Gems remains due.
- Taekyung shares a fabricated account of his reawakening and the Gate with Seong Jinho, keeping the Ark - 2020 capsule and Murim’s true nature secret. Jinho congratulates him sincerely, then resumes teasing him about the finder’s fee.
- The System afflicts Taekyung with Dead Drunk, which can be detoxified by circulating qi. His dream contains unclear Murim voices and the words “Youngest, survive,” but he remembers none of it after waking.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the distinction between being recognized as C-rank-level and formal Hunter registration as a C-rank Hunter.
- Preserve the exact System status effect name “Dead Drunk” and its qi-detoxification notice.
- Preserve Jinho’s emotional “You worked hard” congratulations alongside the finder’s-fee joke and the prime-minister/goshiwon-manager footnote.
### Chapter 49

# Chapter 49

## Plot

Taekyung wakes from the System’s Sleep Mode with a Hangover and detoxifies it by circulating qi. He finds that reality’s qi is weak and polluted, then meets Team Leader Choi at an expensive café. Taekyung declines Choi’s unusually generous one-year Peace Guild contract because the System may disappear, but accepts a seven-day provisional contract instead.

Choi immediately takes Taekyung to a Gate and lets him choose equipment from a lavish trunk. The System identifies a First Rate Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set whose Scale Armor activates when fully equipped and a First Rate Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon that can cause Bleeding. At the Gate, a deferential middle-aged man addresses Choi as “young master,” revealing Choi’s wealthy background.

Choi says that only he and Taekyung will enter the D-rank Gate. When Taekyung hesitates, Choi reveals that he is also a reawakened Hunter: his C-rank classification came before his reawakening, so Taekyung infers that Choi is at least B-rank. Taekyung accepts the raid despite the danger, and they enter together.

## Continuity

- Taekyung signs a seven-day provisional contract with Peace Guild after declining the one-year affiliated-Hunter contract; the provisional contract leaves him free to withdraw if the System disappears.
- Taekyung equips the First Rate Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set and Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon from Choi’s equipment collection. The armor’s Scale Armor set effect activates, and the harpoon has a high probability of causing Bleeding.
- Choi arranges restricted access to a D-rank Gate and enters with Taekyung alone. The outcome of this raid is unresolved.
- Choi reveals that he was a C-rank Hunter before a later reawakening. Taekyung infers that Choi is at least B-rank, but Choi’s exact current ability or rank is not stated.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the System’s exact notices “Exiting Sleep Mode,” “Afflicted with the status ailment Hangover,” “Beginning Qi Circulation,” and “Hangover disappears.”
- Keep **Grade** for the System item classifications and rank for Hunter classifications.
- Retain “Black Ivory” and the blunt elephant-dung explanation, the “young master” reveal, and the Pocheongcheon reference with its concise footnote.
- Keep Choi’s measured dialogue and Taekyung’s dry reactions to the luxury setting, fashionable equipment, and two-person D-rank raid.
