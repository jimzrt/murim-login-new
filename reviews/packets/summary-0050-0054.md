# Chapters 45–49

## Plot

Taekyung kills the Level 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior after Character Synchronization restores his System in reality. His Status Window shows Level 33, First Rate Martial Artist, powerful renewed stats, fifteen years of Internal Energy, and the incomplete Traitor Chain Quest. Officials investigate how two C-rank Rare Monsters appeared in an E-rank Gate, while Taekyung asks Team Leader Choi to conceal his strength.

Choi offers Taekyung a 100 million won Peace Guild position, but Taekyung refuses a long-term contract because he fears the System may disappear. After recovering the Ark - 2020 capsule from Seong Jinho, Taekyung confirms that its manual binds it permanently to its user and that the System cannot read either the capsule or manual. He visits the Hunter Association for reassessment, where his former boss Kim Sangshik confronts him. Taekyung effortlessly crushes Kim’s wrist when Kim grabs him, but the reassessment device malfunctions while scanning his Internal Energy.

The Association recognizes Taekyung as C-rank-level, prompting Guild recruitment offers. Taekyung rejects Kim’s apology and says he will consider Sopung Guild only if it removes someone he hates—clearly Kim. He tells Jinho a false account of his reawakening and the Gate, then becomes afflicted with Dead Drunk and dreams of indistinct Murim voices telling the youngest to survive. After detoxifying a Hangover through qi circulation, Taekyung signs a seven-day provisional contract with Peace Guild.

Choi takes Taekyung into a D-rank Gate alone after giving him expensive equipment. Taekyung equips a First Rate Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set and Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon. Choi reveals that he was already C-rank before a later reawakening, leading Taekyung to infer that Choi is at least B-rank. They enter the Gate together.

## Continuity

- The Hobgoblin Priest and Level 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior are dead. Im Hyeokjun and the other veteran Hunters survived but require hospitalization.
- Taekyung’s restored System shows Level 33, First Rate Martial Artist, 120 Strength, 125 Stamina, 121 Agility, 20 Intelligence, 20 Charm, fifteen years of Internal Energy, and 30 Remaining Points.
- Fame is 0; Taekyung’s active Titles are Novice Trainee and Gambler. The Traitor Chain Quest remains incomplete and carries a death penalty for failure.
- Taekyung is recognized as C-rank-level but is not yet formally registered as a C-rank Hunter; the official reassessment takes several days.
- Taekyung has signed a seven-day provisional Peace Guild contract, not the offered one-year contract. He enters a D-rank Gate with Choi alone.
- Taekyung’s equipment includes the First Rate Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set, whose Scale Armor effect activates when complete, and the Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon, which can cause Bleeding.
- Choi was C-rank before reawakening. Taekyung infers that Choi is at least B-rank, but Choi’s exact current rank remains unknown.
- Kim Sangshik helped arrange Taekyung’s dismissal from Sopung Guild and is now threatened with expulsion if Sopung wants Taekyung’s consideration. Choi Min-su remains a C-rank Hunter with A-rank mana control whom Sopung is trying to recruit.
- Taekyung continues hiding Murim, the capsule’s true nature, and the System from Seong Jinho. The capsule remains permanently bound to Taekyung until death, and its purpose and route back to Murim are unresolved.
- Reality’s qi is weak and polluted. Murim’s death and resurrection limits, the Head Elder’s accomplice, the officials’ investigation, Taekyung’s formal registration, and the outcome of the D-rank raid remain unresolved.

### Chapter 50

# Chapter 50

## Plot

Taekyung learns that Team Leader Choi holds exclusive rights to the D-rank Gate. The wetlands are inhabited by swamp Lizardmen, and Choi equips Taekyung with gear that makes every Lizardman focus on him. Choi refuses to help, leaving Taekyung to clear a nearby colony of about twenty Level 40 Lizardmen alone. Taekyung kills them all without injury and levels up twice.

Choi apologizes and explains that the fight was a test of Taekyung’s skill, endurance, and ability to work with him. He then gives Taekyung a cylinder of pheromones collected from female Lizardmen and triggers it, attracting a powerful horde; the raid’s outcome remains unresolved.

At Sopung Guild, the Guild Master learns from Sangdong Guild’s Guild Master that the newly reawakened C-rank Hunter was formerly one of Sopung’s own. He confronts Kim Sangshik over Taekyung’s dismissal and false report, expels Kim, and orders HR to process the resignations of both Kim Sangshik and his son, Kim Sangho. The Guild then erupts in gossip about Taekyung’s reawakening and current whereabouts.

## Continuity

- Choi holds exclusive rights to the D-rank Gate and knows its wetlands well enough to guide Taekyung through them.
- Taekyung kills about twenty Level 40 swamp Lizardmen alone with the Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set and Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon, then levels up twice without being injured.
- Choi admits he arranged the encounter to assess Taekyung’s skill, endurance, and compatibility with him. He releases a powerful female-Lizardman pheromone lure, attracting an approaching horde; the Gate raid is unresolved.
- Sopung Guild’s Guild Master fires Kim Sangshik after learning that Kim dismissed Taekyung and filed a false report; Kim and his son Kim Sangho leave the Guild together.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “exclusive rights” and “transfer of rights” for Choi’s Gate ownership arrangement.
- Preserve the “raise hell” callback in the Guild Master’s opening exchange and the direct address “Team Leader Three.”
- Keep the contrast between green scales and yellow reptilian eyes, and retain the established Lizardman equipment names.
### Chapter 51

# Chapter 51

## Plot

Taekyung faces the pheromone-attracted horde in the C-rank Gate. He spends all 100 Remaining Points—30 each on Strength and Stamina, and 40 on Agility—then uses One Flash to kill the Level 52 Lizardman Great Chieftain and dozens of monsters instantly.

Choi Minwoo is stunned by the result and privately wonders whether Taekyung may be stronger than him. Taekyung and Choi complete three C-rank Gates in one day. Afterward, Choi enforces weekends off under the provisional contract and pays Taekyung a weekly total of 300 million won, including a 270 million won bonus.

## Continuity

- The pheromone-attracted horde and the C-rank Gate raid are resolved: Taekyung kills the boss and dozens of monsters with One Flash.
- Taekyung has spent all 100 Remaining Points: 30 Strength, 30 Stamina, and 40 Agility.
- Choi Minwoo now knows Taekyung's power may exceed his own, though Choi's exact current rank remains unknown.
- Taekyung has completed three C-rank Gates in one day and received 300 million won under the seven-day provisional contract.

## Translation Decisions

- Use **Lizardman Great Chieftain** for the System's 대족장 title and **One Flash** for 일섬.
- Keep *Bulgeum* for “Burning Friday” and footnote it as Korean slang for Friday night.

# Chapter 52

Taekyung returns home after months with his C-rank Hunter license and the 300 million won he earned from the previous day’s Gates. He carries the cash in a drink box, alarming the taxi driver, then reunites with his younger sister Hayeon and his mother.

Taekyung gives his family an edited explanation of his reawakening and income. - Taekyung returns to the family’s safe-zone apartment near Ilsan after months away. He has paid off his debts and resolves to spend freely on his family, including new clothes and an expensive meal.
- Hayeon is nineteen and still a high-school senior. The school founding anniversary explains her early-morning schedule.
- Taekyung’s mother and Hayeon now know that his recent income includes 300 million won from Gate work.

### Chapter 53

# Chapter 53

## Plot

Taekyung wakes from Sleep Mode at his family’s home after a nightmare and is unsettled when Hayeon says he called Jin Wikyung’s name in his sleep. He spends the day with his mother and sister, then discovers that they secretly packed bundles of cash into his backpack. On the taxi ride back to the goshiwon, he remembers an indistinct voice ordering him to survive.

Back in the goshiwon, Taekyung’s nightmares become increasingly vivid and leave him mentally trapped in Murim even after circulating his qi. His mistakes worsen during Gate raids: he kills a Level 50 Lizardman Chieftain but is injured repeatedly, prompting Team Leader Choi to order him home and ask what is wrong. Seong Jinho visits and jokes about disassembling the capsule; Taekyung lies that Jin Wikyung is his Chinese girlfriend. Alone with the untouched capsule, Taekyung realizes that Murim could be another reality and that the capsule might be a Gate to another dimension.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is back in reality, but recurring nightmares of Murim are destabilizing him; Sleep Mode restores his physical condition without resolving the mental disturbance.
- Taekyung has returned to the goshiwon with side dishes and cash secretly given by his mother and Hayeon. He continues concealing Murim, the System, and the capsule’s true nature from his family and Jinho.
- Team Leader Choi has seen Taekyung injured three times in one day and five times by the fourth day, and has ordered him to go home while asking whether something is wrong.
- Taekyung now suspects that Murim is another reality and that the Ark - 2020 capsule may be a Gate to another dimension; its purpose and route remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the distinction between Hayeon’s gender-neutral question about Jin Wikyung and Taekyung’s later false claim that Wikyung is his girlfriend.
- Keep 차 시간 as a missed bus or scheduled departure, not a missed taxi; preserve the night-surcharge joke.
- Render the fading remembered command as an unnamed voice rather than Taekyung’s thought or his mother’s words.
- Use goshiwon with a concise first-use footnote, and retain the Chinese pronunciation joke with the existing footnote.
### Chapter 54

# Chapter 54

## Plot

Taekyung dreams of the great battle between the Mount Heng Sword Sect and the Jin Family of Taiyuan, with the Head Elder’s third force waiting to move. He worries about Jin Wikyung, Wipeng, Hyuk Mujin, the reconnaissance squad, and the others he left behind, then realizes that Murim’s people no longer feel like NPCs. He decides he will return, even though he has no idea what he could do in a battle involving thousands of martial artists and fighters stronger than Jopil.

Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim review Taekyung’s background and conclude that he is clean: not an illegal Awakener and not someone who deliberately approached Choi. Choi decides to keep watching him and recruit him if possible. He offers a contract with a 500-million-won signing bonus, a 50-million-won monthly salary, a seventy-percent settlement split, an officetel, a sedan, and full social insurance. Taekyung begins signing, but breaks the fountain pen when he admits that he cannot abandon Murim. He refuses the offer, promises to return the next day, and tells Choi to raise the terms for a fourth offer.

Taekyung enters the capsule, confirms that he has already decided to go back, and accepts the System prompt to connect to Murim. He logs in.

## Continuity

- The Mount Heng–Jin Family battle has begun; the Head Elder’s third force is waiting for both sides to weaken, and the battle’s outcome and the betrayal’s exposure remain unresolved.
- Taekyung now accepts that the people he met in Murim are real to him rather than NPCs and has successfully reconnected to Murim through the Ark - 2020 capsule.
- Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim have investigated Taekyung and found no evidence that he is an illegal Awakener or deliberately infiltrated Choi’s orbit. Choi intends to keep observing him and recruit him.
- Taekyung rejected Choi’s first three contract offers despite the latest offer’s 500-million-won signing bonus and extensive benefits, and plans to meet him again after returning from Murim.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the Jin banner as a separate image at the weapon tips; do not attach it to the Mount Heng force with a possessive pronoun.
- Render Taekyung’s relationship to the remembered figures as “I had been a blood brother, too,” preserving his role rather than reversing the subject.
- Use “illegal Awakener,” “reconnaissance squad,” and “son and older brother they could be proud of” for the reviewed terminology and perspective.
- Render the System prompt as “Would you like to connect to Murim?” and reserve “logged in” for Taekyung’s following action.
