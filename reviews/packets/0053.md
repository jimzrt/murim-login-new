# Jin Wikyung (진위경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 37
- **Aliases:** None revealed
- **Role:** Thirty-five-year-old Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan
- **Personality:** Calm and authoritative in public; affectionate and protective toward Taekyung beneath a stern mask; accepts responsibility from his subordinates and shows immediate concern for family
- **Voice:** Restrained, formal, and commanding with subordinates; openly affectionate with Taekyung
- **Relationships:** Taekyung’s eldest brother and future Family Head; head of Wipeng; member of the Jin Family
- **Continuity:** At Medicine King Hall, he worries over Taekyung’s memory loss and supplies him with basic family facts; despite long workdays, he visits Taekyung’s pavilion and discovers him practicing footwork late at night. He stages Taekyung’s confinement in the training hall to shield him from the family power struggle, orders Shanxi branches to prepare after contact from the Mount Heng Sword Sect, and waits in the main assembly hall with its envoy. He refuses the envoy’s demand and the duel, then tries to intervene through Sound Transmission when Taekyung accepts, but Wipeng restrains him. After Taekyung wins, Wikyung uses force and family pressure to end his confinement and is openly delighted by the victory. The next day, he draws heroic pictures of Taekyung, receives an urgent messenger hawk, and summons a secret late-night family-council meeting before the Head Elder appears. At the council, he acts as Family Head, guides Taekyung through the interrogation with Sound Transmission, remains on Taekyung’s side when the senior faction proposes handing him over, and declares the family must fight. After Mount Heng declares war, he takes operational control and seals the family grounds against entry or exit without his seal. In the Lesser Family Head’s office, he reassures Taekyung, discusses the Head Elder’s possible ulterior motive, and weighs the spreading poisoning rumor and failed support requests. He receives Wolhwa as Eun Sowol, accepts the Lower District Sect’s demand for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive rights to the pleasure district in exchange for aid, and invokes his authority as acting Family Head. He finalizes the wartime alliance with Wolhwa: the Lower District Sect will stop selling Shanxi information to other sects and devote all resources to war intelligence in exchange for half of Mount Heng’s shops and exclusive pleasure-district rights. When Taekyung goes missing near the Sakju Branch survivors, Wikyung sends Wipeng and more than twenty riders to find and protect him.
- **Chapter 21 continuity:** When Mount Heng’s war begins with the murder of twenty-five children from Jin Family branches, Wikyung is shaken and blames himself. The Head Elder’s cold argument about wartime sacrifice restores his composure, though the two remain politically opposed over Taekyung’s assignment.

A Lower District Sect messenger hawk then reports that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, and twenty special-detachment members have appeared in Jeongyang. Wikyung orders fifty martial artists to retrieve Jin Family members safely and becomes alarmed when he learns Taekyung is also in Jeongyang.

- **Chapter 34 continuity:** Wikyung learns that Mount Heng is gathering troops and plans to march north with all Jin Family forces in two days, before reinforcements join the Blood Wolf Sword’s main force. He asks Taekyung to command the rear guard and explains the Five Gates of Shanxi alliance’s support. When Taekyung asks how to advance realms, Wikyung tells him that martial arts begin with belief; Taekyung’s realization triggers his First Rate breakthrough, and Wikyung laughs aloud at the result.
- **Chapter 36 continuity:** Wikyung leads the departure ceremony for more than five hundred Jin Family and allied martial artists and predicts victory within three days. He refuses Taekyung’s request for a reconnaissance mission to gain Fame, tells him to survive, and orders him to flee if the family loses so Taekyung and Jin Mukyung can become the family’s roots.

He predicts the enemy will clash rather than retreat, plans to seize Jeongyang’s high ground, and uses the narrow Eight Spring Gorge as a kill zone. With help from a resourceful Lower District Sect ally, he has hidden about one hundred horn bows on the cliffs above the gorge. He sends 300 Jin Family martial artists and 150 allied reinforcements toward the gorge at dawn.
