## Previous block summary

# Chapters 10–14

## Plot

Taekyung enters a staged confinement in the training hall, secretly arranged by Jin Wikyung to protect him from the Elder Council. He acquires and trains the Jin Family’s Spear Technique, combines it with his Manoeuvre Technique, and creates a crater with the Sky-Piercing Strike. The Unity of Self and Object achievement grants him the Clear-Heart Pill, a ring that improves concentration and cultivation. During three days of intensive training, his Cultivation Technique reaches the Third Stage, his Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques reach the Fourth Stage, and he reaches Level 14. Training Mode lets him practise against adjustable versions of opponents, including Hyuk Mujin.

After confinement ends, Taekyung is taken to the main assembly hall rather than allowed home. Lee Seogeun, a Level 30 envoy of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, accuses him of attempting to rape the sect leader’s daughter and demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan. When Jin Wikyung refuses the demand and the duel, Taekyung accepts the System’s Duel Quest to prevent war and his designation as a public enemy. Lee initially overwhelms him with killing intent, but Taekyung blocks more than twenty strikes, kicks Lee’s exposed chest, and breaks through the Intimidation effect. The duel continues with Taekyung promising to defeat him.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 14. His Cultivation Technique is Third Stage; his Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques are Fourth Stage.
- The Clear-Heart Pill is a ring that steadies Taekyung’s mind and improves concentration and cultivation.
- Training Mode can summon prior opponents and partially adjust their abilities; Hyuk Mujin is Lv. 20.
- Taekyung’s three-day protective confinement has ended. He is at the main training ground, still not returned to his residence.
- Jin Wikyung is Taekyung’s protective eldest brother and Lesser Family Head; Wipeng restrains him from entering the duel and supports the protective plan.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect and the Jin Family are sworn enemies. Lee Seogeun is a Lv. 30 envoy acting on Mount Heng’s behalf.
- The Myeongwollu evidence remains suspicious: witnesses claim Taekyung entered the wrong room while drunk and that someone heard screaming, while Taekyung denies the alleged assault through his amnesia pretence.
- Taekyung accepted the Duel Quest. Its reward is the Gambler title, EXP, and Fame 50; refusal would cause the Sex Fiend title, injury, war with Mount Heng, and public-enemy status.
- Taekyung can read and block Lee’s attacks despite the Level gap, has kicked Lee’s chest, and has lost the Intimidation status effect.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

- Render the achievement as **Unity of Self and Object**, the reward as the **Clear-Heart Pill**, and the quest titles as **Gambler** and **Sex Fiend**.
- Render Mount Heng’s demand as withdrawal from every commandery and county except Taiyuan.
- Preserve the brisk dark action-comedy, Taekyung’s self-mocking profanity, and the distinction between the first weapon clash (“Kra-kra-kraang”) and later cannon-like impacts.
- Preserve the gold-spoon/**God-Spoon** wordplay with a brief footnote where needed.

### Chapter 15

# Chapter 15

## Plot

Wipeng is stunned to see Taekyung hold his own against Lee Seogeun, recognizing the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre and Spear Techniques in his movements and suspecting that the supposed amnesia conceals a fundamental change. When Mount Heng warriors protest the duel, Jin Wikyung silences one with a brutal palm strike, knocking out his remaining teeth, then returns to watching with undisguised delight. Taekyung defeats Lee in unarmed combat after discarding his spear: he uses a full mount, survives Lee’s internal-energy punch, crushes Lee’s foot into the floor, and sends him flying with a concentrated strike to the solar plexus. The Duel Quest completes, granting the Gambler title, EXP, Fame, three levels, the Fourth Stage of the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, and Third-Stage Qi Sense, which can detect targets up to Level 50. Mount Heng leaves with its injured warriors, while Wikyung ends Taekyung’s confinement through a coercive consensus among the family seniors. In the closing scene, Lee wakes in a Jin Family carriage and is killed by a masked assassin using Sound Transmission, paralysis, and a large blue-black needle; “going to Mount Beimang” foreshadows his death.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 17. His Jin Family’s Cultivation, Spear, and Manoeuvre Techniques are Fourth Stage; Qi Sense is Third Stage and detects targets up to Lv. 50.
- Taekyung completed the Duel Quest and received the Gambler title, EXP, and Fame, plus the overwhelming-performance rewards.
- Taekyung defeated the Lv. 30 Lee Seogeun in the duel by combining martial-arts experience with his techniques, then unarmed grappling and concentrated internal-energy strikes.
- Taekyung’s three-day protective confinement has ended; he is free to leave the training hall after the family seniors accept Wikyung’s decision.
- Jin Wikyung remains fiercely protective of Taekyung, restrains the Mount Heng protest through force, and is delighted by Taekyung’s victory. Wipeng recognizes Taekyung’s concealed change and helps Wikyung control the situation.
- Lee Seogeun is dead. A masked, unidentified assassin killed him in the carriage after the Mount Heng delegation departed, using Sound Transmission, a toxin or needle effect, and a blue-black needle.
- Mount Beimang is a burial mountain; “going to Beimang” is an idiom for dying.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **Mount Heng Sword Sect**, **Mount Beimang**, **Taiyuan**, **Sound Transmission**, **Gambler**, and the Jin Family’s **Cultivation**, **Spear**, and **Manoeuvre Techniques**.
- Render the Korean yellow-sprouts/gold wordplay as “a bad seed with yellow sprouts… practically gold,” with a brief footnote; do not turn it into a wealth or golden-spoon joke.
- Preserve Wikyung’s “What trash?”, Wipeng’s “Crazy bastard. He’s talking shit,” Lee’s rage, and the failed shouted “Who is it!” without sanitizing or changing their speakers.
- Keep the assassination cues explicit: burning throat pain, loss of voice, Mount Beimang’s death idiom, paralysis, and the masked man’s bright smile as he retrieves the large needle.
### Chapter 16

# Chapter 16

## Plot

Jin Wikyung spends the morning drawing triumphant scenes of Taekyung’s duel, leaving Wipeng to marvel at his lord’s priorities. A messenger hawk arrives, signaling an urgent matter. Han Yeop introduces himself to Taekyung and explains that the Jin Family now believes the absurd rumor that Taekyung is Shanxi’s Sleeping Dragon. Taekyung confirms it, and the System awards 10 Fame while warning that further belief will increase the total.

Taekyung reviews the duel’s lessons: his stats are strong, but his ten years of internal energy are far below Lee Seogeun’s likely twenty. He concludes that internal energy controls martial realm classification, much as mana capacity controls Hunter rank, and plans to cultivate while pursuing EXP-giving Quests. The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30 (17/30), and Fame 500 (70/500) for the reward of Logout. Two days remain in his confinement, with occasional +3 Fame notices.

Late that night, Jin Wikyung summons the family council. Before he can explain, the Head Elder appears after years of seclusion and identifies the Mount Heng Sword Sect as the subject.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 17 with ten years of internal energy; his stats are sufficient for combat, but internal-energy growth is the apparent requirement for advancing his martial realm from Second Rate.
- The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500; current progress is Level 17/30 and Fame 70/500, with Logout as the reward.
- The Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor is spreading through the Jin Family; Taekyung deliberately confirms it, earning 10 Fame and later periodic 3-Fame increases as belief spreads.
- Taekyung has two days left in the three-day protective confinement and intends to cultivate during that time.
- Jin Wikyung draws heroic paintings of Taekyung’s duel, receives an urgent messenger hawk, and summons a secret late-night family-council meeting.
- The Head Elder, absent from the world for years, has returned and is now involved in the Mount Heng Sword Sect matter.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi**, **First Rate**, **Second Rate**, **Fame**, **Logout**, and the Main Quest’s Level 30/Fame 500 requirements.
- Keep the mechanic precise: mages start at D-rank at the lowest because their profession provides high basic mana; D is not described as the system’s lowest rank.
- Format every System notice as a `> **System**` block, including the later +3 Fame notice.
### Chapter 17

# Chapter 17

## Plot

Taekyung cultivates with the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique and discovers an unidentified energy occupying his dantian. It contains more power than his ten years of internal energy, but when he cautiously touches it, the energy rejects him and nearly consumes him. Training still raises his internal energy slightly and increases Sinews and Bones by 1 each, though his Status Window continues to display ten years of internal energy. He considers asking Jin Wikyung for a spirit pill and for an explanation of the energy.

Han Yeop and an unfamiliar martial artist summon Taekyung to the family council at Wikyung’s command. The council is a chaotic factional struggle between Wikyung and the Elder Council. Wikyung guides Taekyung through Sound Transmission, warning him to tell only the truth, then begins an interrogation about Lee Seogeun’s poisoning. Taekyung denies involvement and confirms through the System that he has no poison-related martial arts, abilities, or Items.

The White Tiger Hall Leader demands evidence of Taekyung’s innocence, while the senior faction repeatedly looks to the Elder Council before pressing him. The Head Elder, Taekyung’s great-uncle and the family’s most senior elder, watches from the seat of honor. The Elder Council finally asks where the matter began, and the senior faction blames Taekyung for disgracing the family and provoking the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s prized treasure.

## Continuity

- An unidentified energy occupies Taekyung’s dantian. It is more powerful than his ten years of internal energy, rejects contact, and nearly consumes the energy he sends toward it.
- Cultivation training raises Taekyung’s internal energy slightly and increases Sinews and Bones by 1 each, but his Status Window still displays ten years of internal energy.
- Jin Wikyung summons Taekyung to the secret family council and privately guides him through the interrogation with Sound Transmission; he is on Taekyung’s side.
- The Elder Council is in a power struggle with the young Lesser Family Head. The council interrogates Taekyung over Lee Seogeun’s poisoning and the Mount Heng Sword Sect crisis.
- The Head Elder is Taekyung’s great-uncle and the most senior elder in the Jin Family; he sits beside Wikyung and observes the council.
- Taekyung denies poisoning Lee Seogeun and confirms he has no poison-related martial arts, abilities, or Items. The council’s accusation remains unresolved.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **First Rate**, **Second Rate**, **Sound Transmission**, **Elder Council**, **Head Elder**, and **White Tiger Hall Leader** consistent with established terminology.
- Distinguish **spirit pill** (영단) from **elixir** (영약); preserve the *boja* / *bojagi* wordplay with a footnote.
### Chapter 18

# Chapter 18

## Plot

The Jin Family council debates Lee Seogeun’s poisoning and the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s demand. A senior faction member proposes handing Taekyung over to stop a war, but Jin Wikyung angrily rejects the idea, defends the fairness of the duel, and declares that the family must prepare to fight. The Head Elder unexpectedly backs Wikyung, condemns the faction members who framed Taekyung, and turns the meeting into an apparent public show of support for Wikyung; Taekyung recognizes the unsettling resemblance to an election campaign.

The council compares forces: the Jin Family has roughly 200 martial artists, fewer than 20 confirmed First Rate masters outside the wider senior group, and three Peak masters—Wikyung, the Head Elder, and Wipeng. Mount Heng has at least 300 martial artists, more than 50 First Rate masters, and five Peak masters. A messenger then arrives with Mount Heng’s war declaration, and Wikyung seals the family grounds under strict guard. The System announces a War relationship, marks Taekyung as Mount Heng’s public enemy, warns that fleeing will incur severe penalties, and creates the Main Quest — War.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung openly defends Taekyung, declares war preparation the only path forward, and assumes operational control after Mount Heng’s declaration; without his seal, no one may enter or leave the family grounds.
- The Head Elder’s motives remain unclear. He backs Wikyung, threatens severe discipline for the faction that framed Taekyung, then publicly elevates Wikyung and rallies the family around him.
- The Jin Family is badly outmatched by Mount Heng: three versus five Peak masters, roughly 200 versus at least 300 martial artists, and fewer than 20 versus more than 50 First Rate masters.
- Mount Heng Sword Sect has declared war on the Jin Family of Taiyuan, established a War relationship through the System, and designated Taekyung a public enemy; fleeing now carries severe penalties.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Enemies Who Cannot Live Beneath the Same Sky**, **War relationship**, **Main Quest — War**, **Peak**, **First Rate**, **Head Elder**, and **White Tiger Hall Leader** consistent with established terminology.
- Preserve Taekyung’s dry, profane reaction and the council’s shift from factional interrogation to war mobilization.
### Chapter 19

# Chapter 19

## Plot

Jin Wikyung assumes command after Mount Heng’s war declaration, but Taekyung remains shaken by the danger. In the Lesser Family Head’s office, Wikyung, Wipeng, and Taekyung discuss the Head Elder’s inscrutable political motives, the rapidly spreading rumor that Lee Seogeun was poisoned, and the failure of Shanxi sects to answer their requests for support. A messenger pigeon arrives with a reply. At the Jin Family’s gate, a carriage is stopped by a guard before its coachman and passenger enter; the passenger is Wolhwa, who is revealed to be Eun Sowol, the Level 50 Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch.

Wolhwa embarrasses Taekyung by revealing that she knows him from Honghwaru and then explains that she came to discuss aid for the Jin Family. She demands half of Mount Heng’s shops and assets as compensation. Wikyung accepts immediately, invoking his authority as acting Family Head while their father is absent. Wolhwa also says she likes Taekyung, leaving him unsure how much is sincere.

## Continuity

- The Head Elder’s support is now viewed by Wikyung and Wipeng as certain to conceal an ulterior motive, though his true plan remains unknown.
- The Lee Seogeun poisoning rumor is spreading across Shanxi within half a day, while the Jin Family’s support requests receive no replies.
- Wolhwa is Eun Sowol, a Level 50 martial artist and the Lower District Sect’s Branch Leader for Shanxi; Honghwaru in Taiyuan serves as that sect’s Shanxi branch.
- The Lower District Sect offers assistance in exchange for half of Mount Heng Sword Sect’s shops and assets, and Wikyung accepts as acting Family Head.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the System’s clause order: “Those who survive are strong, and only the strong will survive.”
- Keep the gate scene focalized through the guard, and retain Taekyung’s comic reaction, “Why is noona coming out of there…?”
- Keep **Lower District Sect**, **Branch Leader**, **War**, **Main Quest**, **Level Window**, and **acting Family Head** consistent with the established register.
