## Previous block summary

# Chapters 5–9

## Plot

Taekyung completes the tutorial, learns that Logout requires First Rate, Lv. 30, and 500 Fame, and is taken to the Medicine King Hall after his fight with Hyuk Mujin. He begins cultivating the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, pretends that his memory has not returned, and discovers an unused room filled with martial arts manuals. The System reveals that martial arts occupy ten slots, three of which are already filled. Jin Wikyung and Wipeng catch him practicing footwork at night, but accept his explanation. In Chapter 9, Taekyung sorts the manuals, acquires the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique, completes its achievement, and earns the title Novice Trainee. Seeking a proper place to practice, he asks for an empty room. Jin Wikyung instead orders his indefinite confinement in the training hall as a protective measure against the Elder Council’s coming attack. Wipeng secretly explains the plan through Sound Transmission and promises to release him within seven days; Taekyung negotiates that down to three days before being escorted away.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains trapped in Murim; Logout and death rules remain unresolved.
- Logout requires First Rate, Lv. 30, and 500 Fame.
- He is practicing the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique and has acquired the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique; he has also located the Jin Family’s Spear Technique.
- His martial arts interface has ten slots, with three already filled.
- Taekyung continues pretending that his memory has not fully returned.
- Jin Wikyung is the Lesser Family Head and Taekyung’s protective older brother; Wipeng is his capable aide and can use Sound Transmission.
- The Elder Council is preparing to challenge Jin Wikyung’s authority by attacking Taekyung’s conduct.
- Taekyung is being held in the training hall for an indefinite period, with Wipeng promising release within three days after their negotiation.

### Chapter 10

# Chapter 10

## Plot

Taekyung is escorted to the training hall under a staged closed-door confinement order. Jin Wikyung is using the arrangement to shield him from the Jin Family’s power struggle, while Wipeng has negotiated three days of training. Inside the cavern, Taekyung finds a surprisingly comfortable private suite, grain-repelling pills, water, and a weapons rack. He acquires the Jin Family’s Spear Technique, struggles with a training wooden spear, then draws internal energy through his body and switches to a heavy sharp spear. Outside, Jin Wikyung and Wipeng discuss Taekyung’s supposed frailty, including his history of consuming medicine, before learning that the Mount Heng Sword Sect has made contact. Jin Wikyung orders every Shanxi branch to prepare.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains confined in the training hall for an announced-later period, though the arrangement is protective and temporary.
- He has acquired the Jin Family’s Spear Technique and is practicing it with internal energy and a sharp iron spear.
- The training hall contains supplies, a cold underground spring, and a weapons rack; the System identifies grain-repelling pills and a training wooden spear.
- Jin Wikyung is protecting Taekyung amid a Jin Family power struggle; Wipeng knows and supports the arrangement.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect has contacted the Jin Family, prompting preparations across Shanxi.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the “gold spoon” and “God-Spoon” wordplay with a footnote.
- Keep the gate’s protruding mechanism as the object pulled by the waiting NPC.
- Render the System prompt as learning the named Jin Family’s Spear Technique.
- Render whole-body qi movement naturally rather than literally as “four limbs and a hundred bones.”
### Chapter 11

# Chapter 11

## Plot

Taekyung completes one hundred successful repetitions of the Jin Family’s Spear Technique, gaining the technique and a small stat increase. A grain-repelling pill restores his fatigue and hunger and briefly boosts his stats, but a second pill does not stack and leaves him overeaten. While continuing to train, he discovers that his feet keep mixing the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique into the spear forms. He eventually makes the two techniques mesh, channels his internal energy through the combined movement, and drives the Sky-Piercing Strike into the cavern wall hard enough to create a crater. The effort exhausts his internal energy and knocks him unconscious. Afterward, the System grants the Unity of Self and Object achievement, substantially raises the realms of his three martial arts, and levels him up twice to Level 13. He distributes his new points and prepares to inspect the achievement reward.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains in the training hall and has completed the spear-training sequence.
- The Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique and Spear Technique are at the Third Stage; the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique is at the Second Stage.
- Taekyung is now Level 13 after gaining insight through training and completing the Unity of Self and Object achievement.
- His internal energy was fully depleted by the combined technique, but sleep mode restored his condition.
- The Unity of Self and Object achievement granted an item reward that Taekyung has not yet inspected.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the accepted names Jin Family’s Cultivation, Manoeuvre, and Spear Techniques.
- Keep Taekyung’s dry, profane self-mockery around the foul grain-repelling pill and the crater-making strike.
- Render the achievement as Unity of Self and Object and the System’s martial-arts insight as realization.
### Chapter 12

# Chapter 12

## Plot

Taekyung opens the Unity of Self and Object reward and receives the Clear-Heart Pill, a ring that steadies his mind and improves his cultivation. He trains through the night, circulating his qi until the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique reaches the Third Stage. On the second day, Training Mode activates and lets him summon opponents he has fought. He tests himself against a half-strength Hyuk Mujin, then adjusts the illusion's physical abilities and discovers that even a stronger version is not enough. He proposes grafting the Jin Family's Manoeuvre and Spear Techniques onto the illusion for a more useful fight.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains inside the training hall on the second day of his three-day confinement.
- The Jin Family's Cultivation Technique has reached the Third Stage; his Manoeuvre and Spear Techniques remain at the Third Stage.
- The Clear-Heart Pill is a ring that calms the wearer and improves concentration and cultivation.
- Training Mode can summon Lv. 20 Hyuk Mujin and Lv. 10 Cheon Ryeokbu, with summoned abilities partially adjustable.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 청심환 as Clear-Heart Pill and retain the item-window ring designation.
- Keep the Chinese Martial Arts Alliance's failed expedition as dry, irreverent backstory.
- Preserve Cheongwanil as the final form of the Jin Family's Spear Technique.
### Chapter 13

# Chapter 13

## Plot

Taekyung masters linking forms out of sequence while fighting the Training Mode version of Hyuk Mujin. The Jin Family’s Spear Technique and Manoeuvre Technique reach the Fourth Stage, and Taekyung reaches Level 14. After his three-day confinement ends, Wipeng escorts him to the main assembly hall, where Jin Wikyung, Jin Family seniors, and a Mount Heng Sword Sect envoy are waiting. Wipeng questions Taekyung about his former self allegedly trying to rape a Mount Heng daughter. In the hall, the Level 30 Lee Seogeun confronts Taekyung with killing intent and witness statements about a drunken visit to Myeongwollu. Taekyung finds the evidence suspicious, but Lee Seogeun challenges him and the System generates the Duel Quest.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 14; his Jin Family’s Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques are Fourth Stage, and his Cultivation Technique is Third Stage.
- Taekyung has left the three-day training-hall confinement but has not yet returned to his residence.
- Jin Wikyung and Wipeng are present at the main assembly hall with Jin Family seniors and a Mount Heng Sword Sect envoy.
- Lee Seogeun is Lv. 30 and openly hostile toward Taekyung over the alleged assault of his sister.
- The witness statements establish only that Taekyung entered the wrong room while drunk and that someone heard screaming; the System’s Duel Quest is active.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Mount Heng Sword Sect, main assembly hall, Lesser Family Head, Third Young Master, Honghwaru, and Myeongwollu as established names and titles.
- Render the accusation directly as attempted rape and preserve Taekyung’s dark, self-mocking reaction without softening the profanity.
- Explain goshiwon in a footnote as a very small, inexpensive room-for-rent.
### Chapter 14

# Chapter 14

## Plot

The System updates the Duel Quest: declining would make the Mount Heng Sword Sect declare war on the Jin Family and name Taekyung a public enemy. Lee Seogeun, acting as the sect’s envoy, demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan; Jin Wikyung refuses both that demand and the duel, so Taekyung accepts to prevent war. At the main training ground, Taekyung faces the Lv. 30 Lee while about fifty people watch. Wikyung sends frantic Sound Transmission and tries to intervene, but Wipeng holds him back. Lee’s intimidation and overwhelming strength initially terrify Taekyung, but Taekyung blocks more than twenty greatsword strikes, realizes he can see them, redirects one, and kicks Lee’s unguarded chest. The Intimidation effect disappears, and Taekyung promises to beat Lee badly.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains Lv. 14 and has accepted the Duel Quest, whose reward is the Gambler title, EXP, and Fame 50; declining would incur the Sex Fiend title, injury, war, and public-enemy status.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s envoy demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan; Jin Wikyung refuses, and the duel is underway at the main training ground.
- Lee Seogeun is Lv. 30. Taekyung can read and block Lee’s attacks despite the sixteen-Level gap, has kicked him in the unguarded chest, and has lost the Intimidation status effect.
- Jin Wikyung remains protective of Taekyung and is restrained from entering the duel by Wipeng.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the System titles Gambler and Sex Fiend, the Duel Quest, Sound Transmission, and Taekyung’s dark self-mocking profanity.
- Render the Mount Heng demand as withdrawal from every commandery and county except Taiyuan, and preserve “eldest brother” for Wikyung’s self-reference.
- Keep the first weapon clash as “Kra-kra-kraang” and the later impacts as cannon-like booms to distinguish the fight’s sounds.
