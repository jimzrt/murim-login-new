Meanwhile, Jopil, One Question, One Kill, leads about fifty wandering martial artists in massacring Sakju Branch survivors near Jeongyang and sends Black Mountain Blade after the last escapees heading for Honju. Taekyung’s squad encounters them and more than twenty Mount Heng pursuers; he accepts the Sudden Quest to rescue the survivors, orders the squad to hold the enemy, and kills the pursuers himself for EXP and Merit. He defeats Level 32 Black Mountain Blade with Cheongwan-il, completes the Survivors of the Sakju Branch Quest, receives large EXP and Merit, reaches at least Level 20, and triggers a Chain Quest.
