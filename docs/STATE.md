## Current Context

Chapter 0 establishes Jin Taekyung as a recently fired, first-person modern-world protagonist. Chapter 1 shows his friendship with Seong Jinho, his dismissal from Guild employment, and the capsule registering him as a player for Murim. Chapter 2 traps him in Murim as the youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, introduces Wolhwa, and completes the first tutorial stage. Chapter 3 has Taekyung complete Tutorial—Stage 2, allocate his Status and Skill Window points, advance to Second Rate Martial Artist, equip a sharp spear, and begin Tutorial—Stage 3 when bandits appear. Chapter 4 has him defeat the bandit leader Jang Sam, unlock Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, gain level-up points and Fame, and complete Tutorial—Stage 3. Chapter 5 has him return the coachman and captured bandits to the Jin Family, inspect his three family skills, and complete the final tutorial by circulating qi. He gains Qi Sense, the Main Quest opens, and he finally arrives at the Jin Family of Taiyuan. Chapter 6 reveals that Logout is the Main Quest reward for reaching First Rate, Lv. 30, and 500 Fame; Taekyung is treated with contempt at the family gate, attacks gate captain Hyuk Mujin, and is carried to the Medicine King Hall. The Lesser Family Head, Jin Wikyung, learns of the incident and leaves with his guard Wipeng.

## Active Continuity

- **Old game capsule:** Jin Taekyung found and took possession of an old game capsule. It registered him as a player and logged him into Murim; he cannot log out at will, and death during gameplay is presented as permanent.
- **Seong Jinho:** Taekyung’s three-years-older friend, Hope Goshiwon manager, and thirty-year-old exam candidate. He is blunt, knowledgeable about IT, and prone to heavy drinking.
- **Jin Taekyung in Murim:** The youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan; the family has three sons, and its third son is notorious as a good-for-nothing.
- **Wolhwa:** A high-ranking courtesan at Honghwaru in central Taiyuan. She is composed, teasing, observant, and smokes a long-stemmed tobacco pipe.
- **Taekyung’s build:** The System grants 100 Status points; he assigns 30 each to Strength and Stamina and 40 to Agility, then assigns 60 Skill points to Bones and 40 to Sinews. He reaches Second Rate Martial Artist and receives a Sharp Spear.
- **Tutorial—Stage 3:** The next chain quest requires Taekyung to defeat suddenly appearing bandits. Its reward is recovery from all injuries; failure means death.
- **Taekyung’s first Murim combat:** He kills Jang Sam, known as the Heavenly Axe, with the Sharp Spear after using newly accessible internal energy. The five remaining bandits surrender.
- **Jin Mukyung:** Taekyung’s older brother and the Jin Family’s Second Young Master; known throughout Shanxi as the Heaven Shaking Sword, a Peak-level genius swordsman.
- **Tutorial—Stage 4:** Taekyung is forced to circulate qi despite the risk of qi deviation or death. The helper teaches him the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique; he deliberately leaves a second mass of unassimilated internal energy untouched.
- **New System abilities:** Taekyung gains **Qi Sense** and the **Main Quest** after completing all tutorials.
- **Main Quest:** Logout is the reward for reaching the First Rate realm, Lv. 30, and 500 Fame.
- **Chapter 6 gate incident:** Taekyung is treated as the disgraced third son at the Jin Family gate, loses his temper, and attacks Hyuk Mujin. Taekyung is unconscious with bruises; Hyuk Mujin has a cracked bone, and both are taken to the Medicine King Hall.
- **Jin Wikyung:** The thirty-five-year-old Lesser Family Head learns of the gate incident and leaves his office to investigate; Wipeng is his trusted guard.
- **Chapter 7 recovery:** Taekyung wakes in the Medicine King Hall after about an hour unconscious. He is Lv. 11 with 10 unassigned points, invests them in Stamina, and realizes Hyuk Mujin’s Level 20 and martial arts made the difference in their fight.
- **Taekyung’s eldest brother:** An unnamed middle-aged Jin Family man with a level beyond Qi Sense’s Lv. 30 detection limit confronts Taekyung in the Medicine King Hall. He appears threatening but examines Taekyung like a physician, then reveals that he is Taekyung’s older brother.
