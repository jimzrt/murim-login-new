# Chapter 43

## Plot

Taekyung processes the Hobgoblin corpses with professional speed, earning praise from Team Leader Choi and disbelief from Im Kkeokjeong, while the Peace Guild party advances through the E-rank Gate. At the Boss Zone, an old Hobgoblin Priest uses the corpses of nearly a hundred Hobgoblins to restore the stone gate, seal the party inside, and summon a C-rank Hobgoblin Great Warrior. Team Leader Choi identifies both as C-rank Rare Monsters, attacks the Priest, and orders the others to hold the Great Warrior down.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has years of paid raid experience processing monster byproducts and can butcher Hobgoblins at professional speed.
- The Boss Zone contains an old Hobgoblin Priest and a summoned Hobgoblin Great Warrior, both C-rank Rare Monsters.
- The party is trapped inside the Boss Zone; Team Leader Choi attacks the Priest while the others are ordered to restrain the Great Warrior.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Hobgoblin Priest” and “Hobgoblin Great Warrior” as the established monster names.
- Keep the Boss Zone reveal and the party’s unresolved survival situation as the chapter ending.
- Footnote “gukbap” on first use as Korean soup served with rice.
