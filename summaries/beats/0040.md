# Chapter 40

## Plot

- Taekyung wakes from a dream of the Jin Family's battle and finds Seong Jinho eating ramen in his room. He shows Jinho the Ark - 2020 manual, but Jinho can see only blank pages; Taekyung realizes its contents are visible only to him.
- Taekyung visits a capsule room and tries the most popular virtual-reality game. Its graphics are good, but its NPCs, dialogue, sensations, and artificiality cannot match Murim, so he asks for a solo open-world wuxia game and leaves disappointed.
- Taekyung calls his younger sister Hayeon from Hope Goshiwon. Hearing her and thinking about his mother convinces him that he has returned to reality, and he decides to live in reality with his family nearby.
- The next morning, Jinho sees Taekyung throw the junk capsule away and jokes about whether he is returning to Murim. Taekyung denies believing it and leaves for work, while Jinho decides to investigate the H Soft manufacturer.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's logout has returned him to reality; he remembers exactly thirty days in Murim and only three hours passing outside, while the System remains unavailable.
- The manual's text is inaccessible to Jinho even when Taekyung can read it. The capsule's purpose, Character Synchronization, and route back to Murim remain unexplained.
- Taekyung's family is alive and reachable, and Hayeon is a high-school senior with 120 days until the college entrance exam. Jinho remains skeptical of Taekyung's Murim account but is now curious about H Soft.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **Ark - 2020**, **H Soft**, and **Character Synchronization** exactly as established.
- Keep Taekyung's dry, self-mocking modern voice, Hayeon's bratty sibling banter, and Jinho's blunt informal speech in the reality scenes.
