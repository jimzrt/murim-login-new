# Chapter 32

## Plot

Wipeng reports that the Jin Family won a major battle at Honju: one hundred Jin Family elites defeated two hundred Mount Heng vanguard troops, fewer than thirty Mount Heng fighters escaped, and the Jin Family’s casualties were similar. The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s Lesser Family Head escaped, but Wipeng and Jin Wikyung each killed one of the Mount Heng Twin Devils, both Peak masters.

Wipeng praises Taekyung for defeating Jopil, One Question, One Kill, then recounts the fight with him in detail. He sincerely acknowledges Taekyung’s achievement but rejects Taekyung’s claim that he was stronger than Jopil, pointing out that Taekyung won through a surprise attack. Jin Wikyung arrives, hears the story, and becomes so emotional that he damages Taekyung’s bedroom while raging about Jopil.

Wikyung then carries Taekyung’s bandaged body to the broken wall and presents him to a crowd. The family’s exaggerated rumor says Taekyung led a death squad, defeated Jopil and one hundred wandering martial artists, and rescued the Sakju Branch’s household. Wikyung turns the rumor into a public celebration, and the crowd hails Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family won at Honju with one hundred elites against two hundred Mount Heng vanguard troops. Fewer than thirty Mount Heng fighters escaped, and Jin Family casualties were similar in number.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s Lesser Family Head escaped the Honju battle.
- Wipeng and Jin Wikyung each killed one of the Mount Heng Twin Devils, who were Peak masters.
- Wipeng considers Taekyung’s defeat of Jopil extraordinary but says Taekyung was not stronger than Jopil; Taekyung won through a surprise attack.
- Jin Wikyung publicly celebrates Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi after hearing an exaggerated account of his actions.
- Taekyung remains heavily bandaged and is carried by Wikyung before a crowd; his awaited System Rewards are not checked in this chapter.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved. The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the full purpose of their plan also remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Mount Heng Twin Devils**, **Peak**, **One Question, One Kill**, and **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi** as established titles and terminology.
- Preserve Wipeng’s sincere praise alongside his blunt correction that Taekyung only won through surprise, and retain Wikyung’s destructive affection and the crowd’s exaggerated rumor as dark action-comedy.
- Render the crude insult about Taekyung smearing shit on the family’s reputation without softening it.
