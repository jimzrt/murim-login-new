# Chapter 55

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin carries the unconscious Taekyung toward the Jin Family’s main force, but Taekyung wakes after roughly two hours and learns that the reconnaissance squad sent messengers back to the family. He realizes that logging in or logging out places the other side into a deathlike sleep, then orders the squad to reach the battle before the worst can happen. Taekyung forces the exhausted Hyuk and the squad to run as though they can cover two hours in one.

At Eight Spring Gorge, the Jin Family’s forces have trapped Mount Heng’s larger army in the narrow terrain. The Head Elder remembers his past as a Great Faction War hero and explains to Jin Wikyung that they must wait for Mount Heng’s impatience before striking; he intends to use the hidden cliff archers and have himself, Wikyung, and Wipeng engage the enemy command. The Head Elder’s faction controls the archers, and his true plan remains concealed.

Lee Cheonbaek recognizes that his grief and impatience led Mount Heng into the trap. When the Blood Rain Group tries to retreat from the front, he kills its leader and drives the survivors back toward the battle. He then leads Mount Heng’s three Peak masters and dozens of First Rate masters into the assault on the Jin Family line.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has awakened in Murim after reconnecting through the Ark - 2020 capsule; he knows that logging in or logging out places the other side into a deathlike sleep.
- The reconnaissance squad is rushing toward the Eight Spring Gorge battle, with Taekyung demanding that they cover the remaining two-hour distance in one hour.
- The Jin Family has the terrain advantage at Eight Spring Gorge, while Mount Heng’s force is bottlenecked and suffering heavy casualties; the battle’s outcome remains unresolved.
- The Head Elder controls the cliff archers and plans to strike Mount Heng’s command after its morale breaks, while his betrayal and full purpose remain unresolved.
- Lee Cheonbaek has committed Mount Heng’s three Peak masters and dozens of First Rate masters to the attack after forcing the Blood Rain Group back to the front.
