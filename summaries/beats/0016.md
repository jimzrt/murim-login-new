# Chapter 16

## Plot

Jin Wikyung spends the morning drawing triumphant scenes of Taekyung’s duel, leaving Wipeng to marvel at his lord’s priorities. A messenger hawk arrives, signaling an urgent matter. Han Yeop introduces himself to Taekyung and explains that the Jin Family now believes the absurd rumor that Taekyung is Shanxi’s Sleeping Dragon. Taekyung confirms it, and the System awards 10 Fame while warning that further belief will increase the total.

Taekyung reviews the duel’s lessons: his stats are strong, but his ten years of internal energy are far below Lee Seogeun’s likely twenty. He concludes that internal energy controls martial realm classification, much as mana capacity controls Hunter rank, and plans to cultivate while pursuing EXP-giving Quests. The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30 (17/30), and Fame 500 (70/500) for the reward of Logout. Two days remain in his confinement, with occasional +3 Fame notices.

Late that night, Jin Wikyung summons the family council. Before he can explain, the Head Elder appears after years of seclusion and identifies the Mount Heng Sword Sect as the subject.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 17 with ten years of internal energy; his stats are sufficient for combat, but internal-energy growth is the apparent requirement for advancing his martial realm from Second Rate.
- The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500; current progress is Level 17/30 and Fame 70/500, with Logout as the reward.
- The Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi rumor is spreading through the Jin Family; Taekyung deliberately confirms it, earning 10 Fame and later periodic 3-Fame increases as belief spreads.
- Taekyung has two days left in the three-day protective confinement and intends to cultivate during that time.
- Jin Wikyung draws heroic paintings of Taekyung’s duel, receives an urgent messenger hawk, and summons a secret late-night family-council meeting.
- The Head Elder, absent from the world for years, has returned and is now involved in the Mount Heng Sword Sect matter.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi**, **First Rate**, **Second Rate**, **Fame**, **Logout**, and the Main Quest’s Level 30/Fame 500 requirements.
- Keep the mechanic precise: mages start at D-rank at the lowest because their profession provides high basic mana; D is not described as the system’s lowest rank.
- Format every System notice as a `> **System**` block, including the later +3 Fame notice.
