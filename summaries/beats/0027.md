# Chapter 27

## Plot

Gong Yacheong reveals that the pursuers’ weapons were coated with cheap poison. Fasting pills restore stamina but cannot detoxify it, and Gong’s condition rapidly worsens. He asks Taekyung to return Socheon and Soyul alive, causing the System to create **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request**, a no-reward Quest that Taekyung accepts.

Taekyung orders the reconnaissance squad to leave Gong behind so the enemy cannot catch them. Han Yeop openly disobeys, then secretly leaves the rear of the formation to carry the unconscious Gong. Taekyung turns back after Soyul’s tiny hands feel real enough to make abandoning an NPC unbearable, finds Han carrying Gong, and helps him.

A hidden retreat scene reveals a secret Sound Transmission conversation. The Head Elder confirms that he sent Jopil and expects Taekyung’s death to change Jin Wikyung’s mind. He says he has waited too long for the resulting bloodshed and plans to drive out the Lower District Sect after the matter ends. The other participant’s identity and the wider plan remain unknown.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong is poisoned and rapidly deteriorating; fasting pills do not detoxify poison.
- Taekyung accepts **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request** to return Socheon and Soyul alive; the Quest offers no reward.
- Taekyung initially orders the squad to leave Gong behind, but Han Yeop disobeys and carries Gong away unconscious. Taekyung turns back and helps him.
- Taekyung’s guilt over abandoning an NPC intensifies when Soyul feels completely real to him.
- The Head Elder secretly sent Jopil and expects Taekyung’s death to change Jin Wikyung’s mind; he intends to remove the Lower District Sect afterward.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Gong Yacheong’s Last Request** as the full System Quest title.
- Preserve the distinction between fasting pills that restore stamina and poison treatment that detoxifies.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s revelation and the unidentified Sound Transmission participant without naming or explaining either beyond the text.
