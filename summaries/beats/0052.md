# Chapter 52

## Plot

Taekyung returns home after months with his C-rank Hunter license and the 300 million won he earned from the previous day’s Gates. He carries the cash in a drink box, alarming the taxi driver, then reunites with his younger sister Hayeon and his mother.

Taekyung gives his family an edited explanation of his reawakening and income. After Hayeon verifies the Hunter license and the cash spills across the living-room floor, the family burns breakfast and decides to eat out. Taekyung takes them shopping at Mirae Department Store and treats them to an expensive meal, determined to spend the money freely on his family.

## Continuity

- Taekyung now has a C-rank Hunter license and has told his mother and Hayeon an edited version of his reawakening and income; Murim and the capsule remain concealed.
- Taekyung returns to the family’s safe-zone apartment near Ilsan after months away. He has paid off his debts and resolves to spend freely on his family, including new clothes and an expensive meal.
- Hayeon is nineteen and still a high-school senior. The school founding anniversary explains her early-morning schedule.
- Taekyung’s mother and Hayeon now know that his recent income includes 300 million won from Gate work.
