# Chapter 30

## Plot

Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm strikes Taekyung in the chest and nearly kills him. A brief flashback returns Taekyung to seven years earlier, when an instructor explains that his Hunter skill, **Thrust with All My Might**, has destructive power far beyond his F-rank body and can only be used when his life is in danger. Taekyung wakes on the snowfield and tricks Jopil by spitting blood, then uses daggers from his Inventory to stab Jopil’s foot, sever both Achilles tendons, and force qi deviation by driving a dagger into Jopil’s dantian as Jopil’s internal energy peaks.

Jopil burns his life to launch one final attack, his hair turning white and his body withering as he directs all his power at Taekyung. Taekyung picks up the broken **Sharp Spear**, awakens the hardened third internal energy in his dantian, and unleashes **Thrust with All My Might**. The attack evaporates half of Jopil’s upper body and kills him; Taekyung collapses as Socheon cries out while holding a sword.

## Continuity

- **Thrust with All My Might** is Taekyung’s Hunter skill from his first awakening. Its power exceeds his body’s limits and normally leaves him incapacitated, but it can save him in a life-or-death situation.
- Taekyung survives Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm, uses Inventory daggers to cripple Jopil, and causes qi deviation by stabbing Jopil’s dantian while Jopil’s internal energy is at its peak.
- Jopil is the nineteenth-generation successor of the Fire Gate Clan. He burns his life in a final attack, but Taekyung’s awakened hardened third internal energy powers the broken Sharp Spear’s decisive thrust.
- Jopil is dead. The immediate condition of Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop after Jopil blasted them into trees remains unclear.
- Taekyung’s unidentified hardened third internal energy can be awakened under extreme danger and greatly increase his physical power, but it tears through his already damaged qi and blood channels.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Thrust with All My Might**, **Flame Divine Palm**, **Fire Gate Clan**, **qi deviation**, **Inventory**, and **Sharp Spear** as established terminology.
- Preserve the abrupt seven-years-earlier Hunter-training flashback and its warning that Taekyung’s skill exceeds his body’s limits.
- Preserve Taekyung’s improvised, darkly comic dagger ambush and the violent final clash without softening the consequences of his injuries.
