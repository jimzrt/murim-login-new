# Chapter 26

## Plot

Taekyung confirms his identity to Gong Yacheong after Hyuk Mujin exposes him. The reconnaissance squad rides toward the main family through a blizzard, and Gong explains that wandering martial artists are dangerous because of their hard-earned experience and Jopil’s relentless persistence. The group camps in crude snow burrows, still two or three days from the main family.

Taekyung opens the Main Quest and sees that Logout requires reaching First Rate, Level 30 (24/30), and Fame 500 (250/500). He distributes his remaining stat points and cultivates, but still cannot determine what he is missing for realm advancement. Jopil tracks the group through the storm: his subordinate is only Third Rate in martial arts but has Peak-level tracking skill, and Jopil orders stragglers killed before following the fugitives’ traces. Taekyung senses that danger is approaching while Jopil looks forward to meeting the mysterious master who has interested him for the first time in a long while.

Taekyung banters with the battered Hyuk and talks with Soyul about mind-reading and martial arts. Socheon keeps pace despite exhaustion, while Soyul says she wants to see her father; Taekyung recognizes the lie Socheon tells her about their parents. Gong remains pale and badly injured, and Taekyung realizes the group may soon be caught. Although he considers fleeing alone because the others are NPCs while he is real, an inexplicable resistance keeps him with them. He cannot yet explain that choice when night falls.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong now knows Taekyung is Jin Taekyung; Hyuk Mujin’s exposure of the identity lie is no longer concealed from the survivors.
- The reconnaissance squad travels toward the main family with Gong, Socheon, and Soyul; the journey should take two or three days, but a blizzard has slowed them.
- Gong explains that wandering martial artists’ battle experience makes them different from ordinary martial artists and warns that Jopil’s persistence makes him especially dangerous.
- The Main Quest requires First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500 for Logout; Taekyung’s displayed progress is 24/30 and 250/500.
- Taekyung distributes his remaining stat points and cultivates, but still does not know what is required to advance from Second Rate to First Rate.
- Jopil’s tracking subordinate is Third Rate in martial arts but has Peak-level tracking skill; Jopil commands numerous such hunting dogs.
- Jopil orders stragglers killed during the blizzard, leaves twenty stripped corpses behind, and resumes pursuit after finding the fugitives’ traces. He is interested in the mysterious master who has caught his attention for the first time in a long while.
- Taekyung senses that danger is near, continues traveling with the group, and wrestles with the fact that he is real while Gong, Socheon, Soyul, and the squad members are game-created NPCs.
- Gong is still pale and injured and may collapse despite fasting pills and circulated qi; Socheon is exhausted but refuses to fall behind.
- Soyul wants to become a proper young lady rather than learn martial arts and longs to see her father; Socheon has told her that their parents went out together.
- Taekyung’s decision to remain with the group is emotionally compelled but unexplained; the chapter ends with night falling before the pursuers catch them.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the contrast between ordinary martial artists and wandering martial artists rather than making the latter sound non-martial.
- Keep the tracker subordinate as the subject of the Third Rate/Peak tracking description, and preserve Jopil’s hunting-dog imagery.
- Retain the Main Quest’s exact Logout, First Rate, Level 30, and Fame 500 terminology and displayed progress.
- Keep Taekyung’s dry, occasionally profane humor while rendering Hyuk’s caught-out “Hah” as a gasp rather than added profanity.
- Preserve the traditional time-period footnote for “shichen” and the chapter’s unresolved emotional conflict over NPCs and reality.
