# Chapter 18

## Plot

The Jin Family council debates Lee Seogeun’s poisoning and the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s demand. A senior faction member proposes handing Taekyung over to stop a war, but Jin Wikyung angrily rejects the idea, defends the fairness of the duel, and declares that the family must prepare to fight. The Head Elder unexpectedly backs Wikyung, condemns the faction members who framed Taekyung, and turns the meeting into an apparent public show of support for Wikyung; Taekyung recognizes the unsettling resemblance to an election campaign.

The council compares forces: the Jin Family has roughly 200 martial artists, fewer than 20 confirmed First Rate masters outside the wider senior group, and three Peak masters—Wikyung, the Head Elder, and Wipeng. Mount Heng has at least 300 martial artists, more than 50 First Rate masters, and five Peak masters. A messenger then arrives with Mount Heng’s war declaration, and Wikyung seals the family grounds under strict guard. The System announces a War relationship, marks Taekyung as Mount Heng’s public enemy, warns that fleeing will incur severe penalties, and creates the Main Quest — War.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung openly defends Taekyung, declares war preparation the only path forward, and assumes operational control after Mount Heng’s declaration; without his seal, no one may enter or leave the family grounds.
- The Head Elder’s motives remain unclear. He backs Wikyung, threatens severe discipline for the faction that framed Taekyung, then publicly elevates Wikyung and rallies the family around him.
- The Jin Family is badly outmatched by Mount Heng: three versus five Peak masters, roughly 200 versus at least 300 martial artists, and fewer than 20 versus more than 50 First Rate masters.
- Mount Heng Sword Sect has declared war on the Jin Family of Taiyuan, established a War relationship through the System, and designated Taekyung a public enemy; fleeing now carries severe penalties.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **Enemies Who Cannot Live Beneath the Same Sky**, **War relationship**, **Main Quest — War**, **Peak**, **First Rate**, **Head Elder**, and **White Tiger Hall Leader** consistent with established terminology.
- Preserve Taekyung’s dry, profane reaction and the council’s shift from factional interrogation to war mobilization.
