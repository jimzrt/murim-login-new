# Chapter 48

## Plot

The Hunter Association’s reassessment identifies Taekyung as C-rank-level, and Guild scouts immediately surround him with recruitment offers. Kim Sangshik switches from insulting him to obsequious apologies and asks him to join Sopung Guild, promising to exceed every competing offer. Taekyung tells Kim to inform the Guild Master that he will consider it only if Sopung gets rid of one man he cannot stand—the obvious target being Kim himself.

Taekyung returns to Huimang Goshiwon, buys beef with the 10.3 million won Team Leader Choi paid him for the raid, and tells Seong Jinho a cover story about the two Rare Monsters and the reassessment. Jinho is moved to tears while congratulating him on becoming a C-rank Hunter, then immediately demands the capsule finder’s fee as a joke. Taekyung becomes afflicted with the System’s Dead Drunk status effect, reflects on the price of the System and his years of hardship, and dreams of indistinct Murim voices calling him squad leader and telling the youngest to survive. He forgets the dream after waking.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is recognized as C-rank-level at the Hunter Association, but the formal reassessment procedure will take several days, so he is not technically registered as a C-rank Hunter yet.
- Guild scouts compete to recruit Taekyung. Sopung Guild’s Guild Master authorizes Kim Sangshik to exceed competing offers, but Taekyung rejects Kim’s approach and demands that Sopung remove one person he hates.
- Team Leader Choi pays Taekyung 10.3 million won so far for the raid, including an extra ten million won; additional payment for selling the Rare Monsters’ equipment, leather, and Magic Gems remains due.
- Taekyung shares a fabricated account of his reawakening and the Gate with Seong Jinho, keeping the Ark - 2020 capsule and Murim’s true nature secret. Jinho congratulates him sincerely, then resumes teasing him about the finder’s fee.
- The System afflicts Taekyung with Dead Drunk, which can be detoxified by circulating qi. His dream contains unclear Murim voices and the words “Youngest, survive,” but he remembers none of it after waking.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the distinction between being recognized as C-rank-level and formal Hunter registration as a C-rank Hunter.
- Preserve the exact System status effect name “Dead Drunk” and its qi-detoxification notice.
- Preserve Jinho’s emotional “You worked hard” congratulations alongside the finder’s-fee joke and the prime-minister/goshiwon-manager footnote.
