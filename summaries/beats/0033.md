# Chapter 33

## Plot

Lee Seogwang returns from Honju with only twenty-three Mount Heng survivors, including himself. Lee Cheonbaek, the Blood Wolf Sword and Sect Leader, learns that Jopil deceived the detachment into advancing to Honju, orders Seogwang back to the sect, and decides to replace the three lost Peak masters by hiring wandering martial artists, black-market fighters, and mounted bandits. With five hundred men assembled, Mount Heng plans to march on Taiyuan in three days; Cheonbaek also orders the suppression of rumors that Jin Taekyung killed Jopil.

Taekyung wakes on his second day of recovery with every injury healed. The physician explains that he stole and consumed a hundred-year snow ginseng capable of granting twenty years of internal energy; most of that energy was spent during the fight with Jopil. Taekyung checks his Status Window: he is Level 30, has 410 Fame and fifteen years of internal energy, and remains Second Rate. He also discovers that Thrust with All My Might has become the Peak Skill One Flash, whose power and cost can be adjusted but which leaves him helpless if overused.

Taekyung reflects on becoming a hero in Murim despite being a bottom-rung F-rank Hunter in the real world. He checks the Logout Main Quest and learns that Level 30 is complete, while First Rate and Fame 500 remain. Jin Wikyung is summoned as the best person to explain how to reach First Rate.

## Continuity

- Lee Seogwang is Lee Cheonbaek’s son and the Young Sect Leader of Mount Heng; twenty-three Mount Heng soldiers, including Seogwang, survived the Honju disaster.
- Mount Heng lost more than two hundred troops and three Peak masters at Honju. Lee Cheonbaek has about five hundred men assembled, plans to march on Taiyuan in three days, and is willing to hire wandering martial artists and mounted bandits.
- Taekyung recovers from Jopil’s attack within two days. The hundred-year snow ginseng granted twenty years of internal energy, but most of it was consumed when he used his Skill.
- Taekyung is Level 30 with Fame 410, fifteen years of internal energy, and the Second Rate realm. The Logout Main Quest still requires First Rate and Fame 500; Level 30 is complete.
- Thrust with All My Might is now the Peak, Second-Stage Skill **One Flash**, which allows its stamina and internal-energy cost to be adjusted and can leave Taekyung helpless.
- Taekyung recognizes that he enjoys living as a martial artist in Murim but still wants to return to his family and true self in the real world. Jin Wikyung is the person he intends to ask about reaching First Rate.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **One Flash**, **One Question, One Kill**, **Peak**, **First Rate**, **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi**, and **Logout** as established System or martial-world terminology.
- Preserve Lee Cheonbaek’s ruthless wartime pragmatism and the dark contrast between Taekyung’s heroic reputation in Murim and his self-mocking F-rank Hunter identity.
- Render the goshiwon explanation in a footnote without interrupting the first-person reflection.
