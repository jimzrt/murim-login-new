# Chapter 31

## Plot

After Jopil’s defeat, Taekyung remains unconscious for five days with life-threatening injuries. He wakes from a nightmare about his modern-world past: during an unusually lucrative goblin raid, greed led his team into a boss zone, where every teammate died except him. The memory leaves him confronting his responsibility for their deaths.

Wipeng tells Taekyung that the reconnaissance squad and the Sakju Branch survivors returned safely; two people are seriously injured, but neither is in danger. The unnamed squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan, and the Jin Family recovered and buried his body. Wipeng urges Taekyung not to reduce the young man’s death to a dog’s death, but to remember him and live his share as well. Taekyung asks for the dead man’s name and learns that the young man chose a dream as vast as his own: to become Number One of All Time. Wipeng’s dream is to become Number One Under Heaven. Taekyung begins to recover his resolve, and Wipeng promises even better news.

## Continuity

- Taekyung was unconscious for five days after killing Jopil; the Medicine King Hall Leader had expected him to die within a day.
- The reconnaissance squad and Socheon, Soyul, and Gong Yacheong returned safely. Two survivors have serious injuries, but their lives are not in danger.
- The unnamed squad member killed by Jopil was an orphan. His body was recovered, buried, and his chosen name was finally shared with Taekyung.
- Wipeng tells Taekyung to remember the dead and live the dead man’s share as well. Wipeng’s dream is Number One Under Heaven; the dead member’s dream was Number One of All Time.
- Taekyung’s modern-world backstory includes a disastrous Gate raid two years earlier: after he insisted on entering the boss zone, the rest of his team died and he alone survived.
- Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop survived Jopil’s attack; they suffered serious injuries, but their lives are not in danger.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the modern Hunter raid’s terminology—Gate, Demon Realm, Magic Gems, boss zone, F-rank, E-rank, and D-rank—distinct from Murim’s qi terminology.
- Preserve the contrast between Taekyung’s comic money-and-marriage conversation, the nightmare’s guilt, and Wipeng’s sincere counsel.
- Render Wipeng’s counsel as “live his share as well,” preserving the meaning of carrying the dead man’s remaining life rather than merely living for him.
