# Chapter 25

## Plot

Gong Yacheong awakens after his fall and reunites with Socheon and Soyul. Taekyung learns that the three are the only survivors identified by the System, and the **Survivors of the Sakju Branch** Quest completes, creating a Chain Quest and granting multiple level-ups, Merit, and Fame. Gong explains that Jopil, One Question, One Kill, led the Sakju Branch massacre; Socheon vows revenge.

Taekyung gives the survivors the false name Hong Gil-dong, promises to kill Jopil, and immediately tries to retreat after learning that Jopil is a Peak master. Socheon clings to him until Gong pulls the child away, calling Taekyung a criminal’s would-be victim. Hyuk Mujin then bursts into the shelter and exposes Taekyung’s identity as Jin Taekyung.

Jopil examines Black Mountain Blade’s corpse, learns that more than twenty men were killed, and murders the Mount Heng Sword Sect Hall Master and the ten-odd martial artists sent to oversee and guide the wandering force. He decides to pursue Taekyung’s group toward Honju. From the scars and footprints, Jopil reconstructs that a single highly skilled spearman killed the pursuers and Black Mountain Blade; the spearman’s identity remains unknown. Meanwhile, Jin Wikyung sends Wipeng and more than twenty riders to find and protect Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Gong Yacheong, Socheon, and Soyul survive the Sakju Branch pursuit and are the three survivors recognized by the System.
- The **Survivors of the Sakju Branch** Quest completes and creates a Chain Quest; Taekyung receives Merit, Fame, and multiple level-ups.
- Taekyung uses the alias Hong Gil-dong with the survivors, promises Socheon that Jopil will die, and then tries to flee after learning Jopil is a Peak master.
- Hyuk Mujin arrives at the shelter and reveals Taekyung’s real identity to Gong Yacheong and the children.
- Jopil is a Peak master. He kills the Mount Heng Hall Master and the ten-odd martial artists assigned as overseers and guides, then orders the pursuit toward Honju.
- Jopil identifies an unknown highly skilled spearman as the killer of Black Mountain Blade and the other pursuers; the spearman is not identified as a Peak master.
- Jin Wikyung sends Wipeng with more than twenty riders to locate and protect Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the full quest name **Survivors of the Sakju Branch**; use the System’s later **Survivor** shorthand only where it appears.
- Keep Taekyung’s Hong Gil-dong lie and add concise footnotes for the folk hero and the tale’s “Father” and “Brother” wordplay.
- Keep the distinction between Jopil’s Peak rank and the unidentified spearman’s skill, and preserve the corpse-pile staging and the Mount Heng overseers’ joint role.
