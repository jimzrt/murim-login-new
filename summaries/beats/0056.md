# Chapter 56

## Plot

Lee Cheonbaek arrives at Eight Spring Gorge with Mount Heng’s core forces and launches the decisive assault. Jin Wikyung orders the Jin Family’s masters forward, and the hidden cliff archers open fire as the Head Elder, Wikyung, and Wipeng engage Mount Heng’s command.

The Head Elder intercepts Lee Cheonbaek and reveals that he is a master far beyond the Blood Wolf Sword. After roughly three hundred exchanges, he wounds Lee’s forearm, snaps his wrist, cuts his knee, and tears through his body with Sword Energy. Lee offers his life and Mount Heng’s surrender, but the Head Elder demands total annihilation. He confirms through Sound Transmission that “they” killed Lee Seogeun, then prepares to finish Lee.

Before the Head Elder can strike, Jin Taekyung arrives and splits a spear flying at the elder’s back. The battle and the Head Elder’s betrayal remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Mount Heng–Jin Family battle at Eight Spring Gorge is still in progress.
- Lee Cheonbaek is gravely wounded and defeated by the Head Elder but survives the chapter’s final interruption.
- The Head Elder has revealed that Lee Seogeun’s death was connected to “their” intervention, without explaining the full plan or the accomplice.
- Taekyung has reached the battlefield and directly confronted the Head Elder.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Blade of Flowers,” “Blood Wolf Sword,” “Peak master,” and “Number One Under Heaven” consistent with established usage.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s Sound Transmission reveal that “they” killed Lee Seogeun without resolving the unidentified group or accomplice.
