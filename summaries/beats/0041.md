# Chapter 41

- Taekyung returns to the Hunter Manpower Office after Sopung Guild fires him. The six-story office prioritizes Hunters by rank: E- and F-ranks wait on the first floor, while D-ranks and above use the second.
- Taekyung reunites with Im Kkeokjeong, an old acquaintance from the Ilsan office. Im is now an E-rank Hunter named Im Hyeokjun; his nickname comes from his resemblance to the famous Joseon-era folk-hero bandit. He is married with children and has recommended Taekyung to Team Leader Choi.
- Peace Guild hires Taekyung for an E-rank Gate as a porter. The contract pays 300,000 won and grants him an eight-to-two settlement split after the raid; he is not assigned a combat position.
- Taekyung travels by bus with Im and three veteran E-rank Hunters. They accept him despite his F-rank status and seven years of experience, because Im recommended him and Team Leader Choi approved the hire.
- The group reaches a four-meter-high E-rank Gate, Taekyung’s first raid since returning to reality.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has begun working in reality again as a porter for Peace Guild.
- Im Kkeokjeong/Im Hyeokjun is an E-rank Hunter, a veteran of more than ten years, and a friendly acquaintance who calls Taekyung his little brother.
- Team Leader Choi approved Taekyung’s hire and leads the Peace Guild raid group; his first name and further details are unknown.

## Translation decisions

- Keep **Hunter Manpower Office**, **Peace Guild**, **E-rank**, **F-rank**, **porter**, **Gate**, and **Team Leader Choi** consistent.
- Explain Im Kkeokjeong’s namesake with a first-use footnote; retain **hyung** for the informal address.
- Render the tea as **yulmu tea** and explain it briefly in a footnote.
- Preserve Taekyung’s dry, self-mocking voice and the contrast between his cautious rank anxiety and the veteran Hunters’ easy camaraderie.
