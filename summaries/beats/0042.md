# Chapter 42

## Plot

Taekyung enters an E-rank Gate as Peace Guild's porter, alongside Im Kkeokjeong and three veteran E-rank Hunters. Team Leader Choi, a C-rank Hunter, leads the party and reveals an expensive Red Drake leather set and magic equipment. Inside the cave Gate, the party encounters roughly thirty Hobgoblins. The veterans hold their positions, Im smashes the shielded monsters with his mace, and Choi personally massacres the remaining group. Taekyung handles the byproducts and silently acknowledges that this is his reality, despite being stronger than Choi would be in Murim.

## Continuity

- Peace Guild is new and has only three members, including its Guild Master.
- Im Kkeokjeong is a veteran E-rank Hunter and the party's main tank; he uses a tower shield and mace.
- Team Leader Choi is a C-rank Hunter with a strong equipment obsession, including a Red Drake leather set made with five types of magic and B-grade Magic Gems.
- Taekyung's first raid since returning to reality ends with the Hobgoblins defeated; he remains an F-rank Hunter working as a porter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 인원이 없어 as “short on people,” preserving the joke that the guild has only three members rather than saying it has no members.
- Render 알아봐 주는 거 엄청 좋아해 as “really loves it when people recognize his equipment,” not as a statement that Choi likes explaining his gear.
- Render 고생하셨습니다 as “…Thank you for your hard work” to preserve the subordinate-to-superior courtesy.
- Use “mid-rank Hunter” and “low-rank Hunter” for Hunter classifications, reserving “level” for System levels.
- Preserve the combat momentum in “Riding that momentum” and address 웃어? as “You’re laughing?”
- Keep Dongdaemun with a first-use footnote identifying it as Seoul’s major wholesale-market district.
