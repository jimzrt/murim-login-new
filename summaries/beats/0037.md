# Chapter 37

## Plot

- The Jin Family’s march reaches Honju on its second night. Gwak Jun of the Three Paths Sect, a Level 40 martial artist, confidently predicts victory and later leads his sect’s black-clad fighters.
- Jin Wikyung learns that Mount Heng’s exhausted reinforcements have crossed Mount Otae and are about to join the main force. He predicts the enemy will clash rather than retreat and plans to seize Jeongyang’s high ground, forcing them through the narrow Eight Spring Gorge.
- The Head Elder identifies the gorge’s steep, jar-shaped mouth, while Wikyung reveals that a resourceful Lower District Sect ally helped hide about one hundred horn bows on the cliffs. Three hundred Jin Family martial artists and 150 allied reinforcements set out for the gorge at dawn.
- Taekyung spends the night watching his Fame objective approach completion, reaching 497/500 toward Logout. He reflects on his month in Murim, jokes about Jopil’s Supreme Peak manual and dangerous thirty-year elixir, and feels genuine affection for the reconnaissance squad.
- When fear rises among the rear guard, Taekyung says he trusts Jin Wikyung. The squad members, including Hyuk Mujin, affirm their trust in Taekyung, and Gwak Jun’s black-clad Three Paths Sect then prepares to move out.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is First Rate, Lv. 32, and has Fame 497/500; Logout remains unavailable by three Fame points.
- The Mount Heng reinforcements have crossed Mount Otae, are exhausted, and are low on food. The Jin Family’s immediate force for the gorge is 450 martial artists: 300 Jin Family members and 150 allied reinforcements.
- Eight Spring Gorge is a narrow, steep, jar-shaped route into Jeongyang. The Jin Family has hidden about one hundred horn bows on the cliffs above it with help from the Lower District Sect.
- Gwak Jun is a Level 40 martial artist of the Three Paths Sect. He is fearless and intensely confident, and leads the sect’s martial artists in black robes.
- Taekyung openly trusts Jin Wikyung and recognizes that the reconnaissance squad’s loyalty and affection feel real to him despite his calling them NPCs.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved. The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the full purpose of their plan remain unidentified.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **Fame**, **Logout**, **First Rate**, **Peak**, **Qi Sense**, **Sound Transmission**, **rear guard**, **Three Paths Sect**, **Honju**, **Mount Otae**, and **Eight Spring Gorge** as established terminology.
- Preserve Taekyung’s dark comedy and growing emotional attachment to the reconnaissance squad, Gwak Jun’s unsettling confidence, and Jin Wikyung’s tactical authority.
