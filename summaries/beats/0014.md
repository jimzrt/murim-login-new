# Chapter 14

## Plot

The System updates the Duel Quest: declining would make the Mount Heng Sword Sect declare war on the Jin Family and name Taekyung a public enemy. Lee Seogeun, acting as the sect’s envoy, demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan; Jin Wikyung refuses both that demand and the duel, so Taekyung accepts to prevent war. At the main training ground, Taekyung faces the Lv. 30 Lee while about fifty people watch. Wikyung sends frantic Sound Transmission and tries to intervene, but Wipeng holds him back. Lee’s intimidation and overwhelming strength initially terrify Taekyung, but Taekyung blocks more than twenty greatsword strikes, realizes he can see them, redirects one, and kicks Lee’s unguarded chest. The Intimidation effect disappears, and Taekyung promises to beat Lee badly.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains Lv. 14 and has accepted the Duel Quest, whose reward is the Gambler title, EXP, and Fame 50; declining would incur the Sex Fiend title, injury, war, and public-enemy status.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect’s envoy demands that the Jin Family withdraw from every commandery and county except Taiyuan; Jin Wikyung refuses, and the duel is underway at the main training ground.
- Lee Seogeun is Lv. 30. Taekyung can read and block Lee’s attacks despite the sixteen-Level gap, has kicked him in the unguarded chest, and has lost the Intimidation status effect.
- Jin Wikyung remains protective of Taekyung and is restrained from entering the duel by Wipeng.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the System titles Gambler and Sex Fiend, the Duel Quest, Sound Transmission, and Taekyung’s dark self-mocking profanity.
- Render the Mount Heng demand as withdrawal from every commandery and county except Taiyuan, and preserve “eldest brother” for Wikyung’s self-reference.
- Keep the first weapon clash as “Kra-kra-kraang” and the later impacts as cannon-like booms to distinguish the fight’s sounds.
