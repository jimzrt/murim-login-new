# Chapter 44

## Plot

The Hobgoblin Great Warrior overwhelms the Peace Guild party, knocking Im Kkeokjeong and the other veterans unconscious while Taekyung lures it away. Taekyung charges back to protect them and blocks the Great Warrior’s greatsword, triggering Character Synchronization in reality: all systems are inherited, and the System identifies the monster as Level 45. Taekyung turns the fight around with his spear, while Team Leader Choi kills the Hobgoblin Priest and then watches the porter maim the Great Warrior, ending in stunned disbelief.

## Continuity

- Im Kkeokjeong and the other veteran Hunters are knocked unconscious but remain alive during the Boss Zone fight.
- Character Synchronization completes in reality, restoring Taekyung’s systems and allowing him to fight despite his official F-rank status.
- Team Leader Choi kills the Hobgoblin Priest and witnesses Taekyung severely wound the Level 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior.
- The Great Warrior’s final fate and the consequences of Taekyung’s restored systems remain unresolved.
