# Chapter 36

## Plot

- Taekyung’s Fame reaches 475/500 for Logout, then ordinary family-wide gains stall. He tries to secure a reconnaissance mission before the main battle, but Jin Wikyung refuses.
- More than five hundred Jin Family and allied martial artists assemble for the departure ceremony. The force is divided into vanguard, center, and rear guard, with Taekyung commanding the rear; Wikyung predicts victory within three days despite the enemy’s numerical and Peak-master advantage.
- The reconnaissance squad joins Taekyung’s rear guard despite its injuries. Han Yeop remains in Medicine King Hall because his internal injuries have not healed, while Hyuk Mujin sneaks out against the physician’s orders after his internal injuries heal.
- Wikyung tells Taekyung that his mission is to survive. If the Jin Family loses, Taekyung must flee so he and Jin Mukyung can become the family’s roots.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is First Rate, Lv. 32, and has Fame 475/500; Logout remains unavailable.
- Taekyung commands the Jin Family’s rear guard, which contains only a few dozen martial artists. The Jin Family and its allies have begun their march, while the broader battle is expected to involve about fifteen hundred martial artists.
- Han Yeop remains hospitalized with unhealed internal injuries. Hyuk Mujin joins the march despite serious external injuries and recurring chest pain.
- Jin Wikyung expects victory within three days but privately instructs Taekyung to survive and flee if necessary.
- The capsule’s purpose, route home, and the limits of Murim’s death and resurrection rules remain unresolved. The Head Elder’s Sound Transmission accomplice and the full purpose of their plan remain unidentified.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve **First Rate**, **Fame**, **Logout**, **Peak**, **rear guard**, and **Sound Transmission** as established terminology.
- Preserve Taekyung’s Fame-seeking dark comedy, Hyuk’s reckless loyalty, and Wikyung’s protective “survive” counsel.
