# Chapter 35

## Plot

- Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop recover in Medicine King Hall after Jopil’s attack. They acknowledge their helplessness before a Peak master, then vow to become stronger than Jopil and catch up to Taekyung.
- Taekyung visits the injured reconnaissance-squad members, Gong Yacheong, Socheon, and Soyul before the Jin Family’s march. Rumors continue to raise his Fame, but he remains about fifty short of the Logout requirement.
- Gong returns Jopil’s possessions: the Supreme Peak Flame Divine Palm manual, the dangerous Peak Fire Divine Elixir that grants thirty years of internal energy, and the mysterious Nameless Sword made of ten-thousand-year cold iron. Taekyung stores them in his Inventory and gains another point of Fame.
- The Head Elder meets an unidentified ally in the garden. Their preparations, including troop deployments, are complete, and the Head Elder is willing to do anything to become Family Head and the sole hegemon of Shanxi.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop survived Jopil’s final attack and are recovering from serious but nonfatal injuries. Both resolve to grow stronger and catch up to Taekyung.
- Taekyung possesses Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm manual, Fire Divine Elixir, and Nameless Sword in his Inventory. The elixir is dangerous because of its powerful fire qi; the sword’s special power remains unknown.
- Taekyung is still roughly fifty Fame short of the Logout target. The Head Elder’s ally, their identities, and the full purpose of their completed preparations remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the contrast between Han Yeop’s earnest ambition and Hyuk Mujin’s competitive vow, along with Taekyung’s dry hospital-room commentary.
- Keep **Flame Divine Palm**, **Fire Divine Elixir**, **Nameless Sword**, **Supreme Peak**, **Peak**, **Grade**, **Fame**, **Inventory**, and **Logout** as established System terminology.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s garden exchange as dash-marked Sound Transmission rather than ordinary spoken dialogue.
