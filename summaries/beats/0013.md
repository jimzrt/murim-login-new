# Chapter 13

## Plot

Taekyung masters linking forms out of sequence while fighting the Training Mode version of Hyuk Mujin. The Jin Family’s Spear Technique and Manoeuvre Technique reach the Fourth Stage, and Taekyung reaches Level 14. After his three-day confinement ends, Wipeng escorts him to the main assembly hall, where Jin Wikyung, Jin Family seniors, and a Mount Heng Sword Sect envoy are waiting. Wipeng questions Taekyung about his former self allegedly trying to rape a Mount Heng daughter. In the hall, the Level 30 Lee Seogeun confronts Taekyung with killing intent and witness statements about a drunken visit to Myeongwollu. Taekyung finds the evidence suspicious, but Lee Seogeun challenges him and the System generates the Duel Quest.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Lv. 14; his Jin Family’s Spear and Manoeuvre Techniques are Fourth Stage, and his Cultivation Technique is Third Stage.
- Taekyung has left the three-day training-hall confinement but has not yet returned to his residence.
- Jin Wikyung and Wipeng are present at the main assembly hall with Jin Family seniors and a Mount Heng Sword Sect envoy.
- Lee Seogeun is Lv. 30 and openly hostile toward Taekyung over the alleged assault of his sister.
- The witness statements establish only that Taekyung entered the wrong room while drunk and that someone heard screaming; the System’s Duel Quest is active.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Mount Heng Sword Sect, main assembly hall, Lesser Family Head, Third Young Master, Honghwaru, and Myeongwollu as established names and titles.
- Render the accusation directly as attempted rape and preserve Taekyung’s dark, self-mocking reaction without softening the profanity.
- Explain goshiwon in a footnote as a very small, inexpensive room-for-rent.
