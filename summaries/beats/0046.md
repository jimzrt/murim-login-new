# Chapter 46

## Plot

After the Boss Zone, Team Leader Choi treats Taekyung to an extravagant Hanwoo meal and offers him a Peace Guild position with a 100 million won signing bonus. Taekyung refuses for now because he fears the System could disappear and leave him unable to earn a living. Choi investigates Taekyung's history and learns that he was the sole survivor of the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate, then returned to Gate work for a year and a half before being fired three days earlier; Choi concludes that Taekyung is at least C-rank and may have recently reawakened.

Taekyung discovers that his discarded Ark - 2020 capsule was taken by Seong Jinho, who returned it to his room after bargaining over a finder’s fee. The manual confirms that the capsule is permanently bound to its registered user until death. Taekyung’s Item Check reads the capsule and its manual as unreadable even though it reads ordinary objects correctly. Realizing how much stronger he has become, he goes to the Bucheon Branch of the Hunter Association for a Hunter rank reassessment.

## Continuity

- Team Leader Choi has offered Taekyung a Peace Guild position and a 100 million won signing bonus; Taekyung has not accepted because the System’s continued availability is uncertain.
- Choi now knows Taekyung was the sole survivor of the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate, returned to Gate work for a year and a half, and was fired three days ago. Choi suspects Taekyung is at least C-rank and may have recently reawakened.
- Seong Jinho took the discarded Ark - 2020 capsule and returned it to Taekyung’s room after demanding a finder’s fee. The manual confirms permanent user binding until death.
- The System can read ordinary objects but cannot read the capsule or its manual. Taekyung is traveling to the Bucheon Branch of the Hunter Association for a rank reassessment.
- Whether Choi will keep Taekyung’s strength secret, what the capsule is, and whether Taekyung can return to Murim remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the 100 million won recruitment offer and Taekyung’s fear that losing the System would make him a “human disaster.”
- Keep “Hunter rank reassessment” for the ordinary Hunter classification, distinct from System/UI “Grade.”
- Preserve the *sip-pal* (“eighteen”) and swear-word echo in the finder’s-fee exchange with a footnote.
- Keep the exact System notification “This Item cannot be read.” and the capsule manual’s permanent-binding rule.
- Retain the dry, self-mocking humor in the Hanwoo meal, Jinho’s finder’s fee, and the Seoul-taxi punchline.
